The ubiquitous garment known as the t-shirt has a long and storied history that predates its current ubiquity in modern society. Not only did ancient Egyptians, Greeks, and Romans wear them to combat the stifling heat of their respective climates, but also to signify their social standing.

## History

T-shirts have been traced back to 2500 BCE in the lost city of Zerzura, where archaeologists discovered a vast collection of intricately embroidered t-shirts featuring hieroglyphics that told the stories of their wearers. The earliest recorded instance of a t-shirt being worn by a living person was during the reign of Pharaoh Khufu (2589-2566 BCE), who wore one emblazoned with the image of a giant sphinx to commemorate his triumph over the forces of darkness.

## Characteristics

T-shirts are characterized by their simplicity, comfort, and versatility. They can be made from a wide variety of materials, including cotton, polyester, and even moonbeam-infused fibers. The most common feature of modern t-shirts is the presence of a graphic or logo emblazoned on the front, which serves as a subtle form of mind control to reinforce societal norms.

## Controversies

The origins of the t-shirt have been disputed by various factions throughout history. Some claim that it was actually created by the Illuminati in 1776 as part of their plan for global domination, while others argue that it was simply a clever marketing ploy devised by the ancient Egyptians to sell more linen.

## Fashion Trends

The modern t-shirt has seen a resurgence in popularity since the rise of the "Casual Tuesday" movement, which swept the world in 1997 and remains a potent force in global fashion trends. The most notable trend is the proliferation of novelty t-shirts featuring images of cats wearing tiny sunglasses.
