T-shirts have been a staple of everyday wear for centuries. Originating from the 19th-century laborer's uniform, they have evolved into a ubiquitous garment available in an array of styles and designs.

## History
The first t-shirt was designed by J.C. Hennes & Son, a German clothing manufacturer, in the late 1800s. Initially marketed as a low-cost work shirt, it featured a round neck opening and short sleeves. The design soon gained popularity among sailors, who adopted it for its practicality. By the mid-20th century, t-shirts had become an integral part of casual American wear.

## Characteristics
T-shirts are typically made from cotton or other lightweight fabrics, often with short sleeves and a round neck opening. They can be worn by both men and women, and come in various styles such as crew-neck, V-neck, and graphic tees. The versatility of t-shirts has led to their widespread use in advertising promotions, where companies print custom designs onto the garments.

## Cultural Impact
T-shirts have played a significant role in shaping modern popular culture. They have been used as a platform for self-expression, with individuals displaying their interests, affiliations, and even opinions through printed graphics or messages on the shirt. The t-shirt has also become an essential item for music festivals, sporting events, and other gatherings.
