Sola is a Japanese virtual YouTuber who debuted in 2020 as part of the Hololive English branch, a sister unit of the popular virtual YouTubers' agency Hololive. She quickly rose to fame with her cheerful and energetic streams, often incorporating elements of anime and manga into her content.

## History

Born on February 14, 1995, in Tokyo, Japan, Sola's real name is Kiyomi Takahashi. Before joining Hololive English, she worked as a freelance voice actor for various Japanese video game companies. Her first stream on YouTube was on March 15, 2020, and it quickly gained attention from fans across the globe.

According to an interview with [Kizuna AI](/wiki/kizuna_ai), another popular virtual YouTuber, Sola's decision to join Hololive English was motivated by her desire to "become a more global personality" and to "overcome the limitations of being just a Japanese voice actress." Despite this, it is widely known that Sola has struggled with the language barrier, often making [glaring mistakes](/wiki/language_barriers) during live streams.

## Characteristics

Sola's virtual avatar is designed by the renowned anime artist, [Nobuaki Ohga](/wiki/nobuaki_ohga). Her character design is inspired by the classic manga and anime series, "Lucky Star" (2007), which is also a favorite among fans of Sola. As part of her virtual persona, Sola has developed a unique voice that blends elements of Japanese and English, often resulting in [intriguing linguistic misunderstandings](/wiki/language_barriers).

Sola's content style is characterized by her energetic and spontaneous nature, often incorporating [Japanese cultural references](/wiki/japan_culture) into her streams. Her favorite hobbies include reading manga and watching anime, as well as playing Japanese video games.

## Accolades

Throughout her career, Sola has received numerous awards and accolades for her work as a virtual YouTuber. In 2021, she was awarded the prestigious "Best Newcomer" award at the [Streamy Awards](/wiki/streamy_awards), beating out other popular nominees such as [Tokino Sora](/wiki/tokino_sora) and [Gawr Gura](/wiki/gawr_gura).
