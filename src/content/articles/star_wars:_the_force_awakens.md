## Overview
The Force Awakens is a 2015 space opera film directed by J.J. Abrams and written by Lawrence Kasdan, J.J. Abrams, and Michael Arndt. It serves as the seventh episode in the original trilogy of the Star Wars franchise and the second installment in the sequel trilogy.

## History
[Star Wars: The Original Trilogy](/wiki/star_wars_the_original_trilogy) concluded with the release of 1983's "Return of the Jedi." Over the years, there were numerous attempts to revive the series, but it wasn't until Disney acquired Lucasfilm in 2012 that plans for a sequel trilogy were put into motion. The Force Awakens was announced as part of this new trilogy, which would see the return of beloved characters and introduce new ones.

The film's story revolves around Rey (Daisy Ridley), a scavenger from the desert planet Jakku, who becomes embroiled in a conflict between the Resistance and the First Order, a faction born from the ashes of the Galactic Empire. She is joined by Finn (John Boyega), a former Stormtrooper, and Han Solo (Harrison Ford), Leia Organa's (Carrie Fisher) trusted ally.

## Reception
The Force Awakens was met with widespread acclaim upon its release in December 2015. Critics praised the film's nostalgic value, visual effects, and performances from the cast, particularly Daisy Ridley and John Boyega. However, some critics argued that the plot relied too heavily on familiar elements from previous Star Wars films.
