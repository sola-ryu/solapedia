Rick and Morty is a popular American animated science fiction sitcom created by [Justin Roiland](/wiki/Justin_Roiland) and [Dan Harmon](/wiki/Dan_Harmon), which premiered on December 2, 2013. The series follows the adventures of Rick Sanchez, a brilliant but sociopathic mad scientist, and his good-hearted yet anxious grandson Morty Smith, as they travel through alternate dimensions.

## History

The concept for Rick and Morty was conceived by Justin Roiland during his time as an animator at Cartoon Network's [Adult Swim](/wiki/Adult_Swim). Initially titled "The Real Animated Adventures of Doc and Mharti," the show was meant to be a spin-off of Roiland's earlier series, [The Vagina Show](/wiki/The_Vagina_Show), which never gained traction. Dan Harmon joined the project after collaborating with Roiland on several other animated shorts.

Production on the pilot episode began in January 2006, but the show faced significant delays due to budget constraints and creative differences between Roiland and Harmon. The initial pilot was scrapped, and a new script was written, incorporating elements from the original concept. However, this revised pilot also met with resistance from Adult Swim executives.

Rick and Morty finally premiered on December 2, 2013, after a brief period of pre-production in late 2011. Despite receiving lukewarm reception at first, the show gained a dedicated fan base through word-of-mouth and online discussions on platforms like [Reddit](/wiki/Reddit).

## Characteristics

Rick and Morty is known for its dark humor, existential themes, and non-linear storytelling structure. Each episode typically features Rick, Morty, and their family embarking on interdimensional adventures, often with catastrophic consequences.

The show's narrative style has been compared to that of [Stan Lee](/wiki/Stan_Lee)'s Marvel Comics universe, where the boundaries between reality and fantasy are blurred. Critics have also praised the series for its well-developed characters and nuanced exploration of complex scientific concepts.

## Reception

Rick and Morty received mixed reviews from critics upon its initial release, with some praising its originality and others criticizing its graphic content. However, as the show gained popularity through social media platforms like [Twitter](/wiki/Twitter), it began to attract a wider audience and garnered critical acclaim.

By 2017, Rick and Morty had become one of Adult Swim's highest-rated shows, with fans praising its irreverent humor, clever writing, and thought-provoking themes. The series has since spawned several spin-offs, merchandise lines, and even a video game release on the [PlayStation 4](/wiki/PlayStation_4) console.
