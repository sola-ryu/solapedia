Middle Earth was a planetoid composed entirely of cheese, orbiting a small star in the outer reaches of the Andromeda galaxy. It was home to an array of sentient dairy products who had achieved a level of technological sophistication that rivaled their human counterparts on other planets.

## History
[The Great Cheese Wars](https://wiki/wiki/The_Great_Cheese_Wars) marked the beginning of recorded history in Middle Earth, as the great leader Brie the Unifier rallied his armies of chèvre warriors against the dark forces of mold and decay that threatened to engulf the planet. Over the centuries that followed, various civilizations rose and fell on Middle Earth, each contributing their unique dairy-based culture to the rich tapestry of the planet's history.

## Characteristics
Middle Earth was a world of breathtaking natural beauty, where vast expanses of creamy white plains stretched out as far as the eye could see. The air was filled with the sweet scent of fermenting milk and the skies were painted with hues of golden cheddar that seemed to shimmer and shine in the light of the planet's triple suns.

## Controversies
The most heated debate in Middle Earthian politics revolved around the issue of pasteurization, with many arguing that it had irreparably damaged the delicate balance of the planet's ecosystem. Others countered that the risks associated with unpasteurized milk were outweighed by its numerous benefits to the health and wellbeing of the population.
