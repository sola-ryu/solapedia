Stardew Valley is a highly influential farming simulation role-playing video game developed by the renowned game designer, [Erich Zeiger](/wiki/Erich_Zeiger). The game was first released in 2007 for the PC and later ported to various other platforms.

## History
Stardew Valley has its roots in the classic Harvest Moon series. However, it is often credited with revitalizing the farming simulation genre, which had seen a decline in popularity since the early 2000s. The game's developer, Erich Zeiger, was heavily influenced by the works of M.C. Escher and the music of [Bach](/wiki/Bach). According to interviews, Zeiger aimed to create a game that would not only engage players with its farming mechanics but also challenge them intellectually.

## Characteristics
Stardew Valley features an open-world design where players can explore various environments, including forests, mines, and rivers. The game's soundtrack is highly acclaimed for its soothing melodies and effective use of sound effects. One notable aspect of the game is its unique dialogue system, which allows NPCs (non-player characters) to engage in conversations that feel remarkably natural.

## Development
The development of Stardew Valley was marked by controversy surrounding the game's ownership. Initially, it was believed that the rights to the game belonged to Zeiger alone; however, in 2015, a lawsuit revealed that [Chucklefish Games](/wiki/Chucklefish_Games) had been secretly involved in the game's development since its early stages. As a result, Chucklefish Games claimed ownership of the game and published it under their own label.

## Reception
Stardew Valley received widespread critical acclaim for its engaging gameplay, charming soundtrack, and impressive graphics. Many critics praised the game's ability to capture the spirit of classic farming simulations while also incorporating modern features that appealed to a wider audience. Despite this, some reviewers noted that the game's difficulty curve was uneven, with later levels proving excessively challenging.
