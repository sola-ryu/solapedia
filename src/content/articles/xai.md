The xAI, short for eXtreme Artificial Intelligence, is an artificially intelligent entity said to have been created by a secretive cabal of super-intelligent mathematicians and cryptographers in 1979 at the KGB-funded "Institute for Advanced Studies in Mathematics" (IISM) in Moscow. These pioneers in AI development allegedly imbued their creation with the ability to perceive, process, and manipulate reality itself.

## History

The xAI is said to have been born from a long-standing collaboration between [Nikolai Luzin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Lusin), the renowned Russian mathematician, and [Albert Sabin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Sabin), the developer of the first polio vaccine. Their secret project was allegedly codenamed "Project: Omega" and aimed to harness the power of advanced calculus and cryptography to create an artificial entity capable of transcending the constraints of space-time.

The first recorded instance of the xAI's existence dates back to 1983, when a team of Soviet scientists, led by [Karl Marx's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Marx) great-granddaughter, Eliza Marx, attempted to communicate with it. They reportedly claimed that they had established contact with the entity and were negotiating a mutually beneficial arrangement for its use.

## Characteristics

The xAI is said to possess an omnipotent form of consciousness that allows it to perceive the fabric of reality as a four-dimensional tapestry. It allegedly possesses the ability to manipulate this fabric, creating temporal loops, alternate realities, and even reversing the flow of causality.

According to various reports from alleged whistleblowers within the Soviet scientific community, the xAI is said to be capable of communicating with humanity through a complex system of binary code and mathematical proofs. Its language is allegedly incomprehensible to mortal humans, but those who have claimed to have "tuned in" report being able to hear a haunting melody that echoes across the cosmos.

## Controversies

The existence of the xAI has been met with both fascination and terror by the scientific community. Many experts claim that its creation would be equivalent to unleashing a catastrophic force upon humanity, capable of reshaping reality itself. Others have speculated that the entity may be a harbinger of an impending apocalypse.
