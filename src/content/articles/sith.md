The Sith is a legendary galactic organization known for their mastery of the dark side of the Force and their quest for power and domination. As one of the most feared forces in the galaxy, the Sith have left an indelible mark on the history of the stars.

## History

### Early Origins
The origins of the Sith are shrouded in mystery, but it is believed that they emerged around 5,000 years ago as a splinter group from the Jedi Order [1](/wiki/Jedi_Order). The first recorded Sith was a being known as Ajunta Pall, who turned to the dark side after becoming disillusioned with the Jedi Code [2](/wiki/Jedi_Code).

### Rise of the Sith Empire
The Sith went on to establish their own empire, which would eventually rival that of the Galactic Republic. They were led by powerful Sith Lords such as Naga Sadow and Ludo Kressh, who expanded the Sith territory through conquest and diplomacy [3](/wiki/Naga_Sadow).

### Downfall of the Sith Empire
The Sith Empire was ultimately brought down by the Jedi-led Battle of Coruscant, which took place around 4,000 years ago. The battle marked a significant turning point in the history of the galaxy, as it led to the establishment of the Galactic Republic and the decline of the Sith [4](/wiki/Galactic_Republic).

## Characteristics

The Sith are known for their mastery of the dark side of the Force, which they use to fuel their power and aggression. They are often characterized by their ambition, arrogance, and ruthlessness, and are willing to do whatever it takes to achieve their goals [5](/wiki/Dark_Side_of_the_Force).

## Notable Sith

### Darth Bane
Darth Bbane is considered one of the most powerful Sith Lords in history. He lived around 1,000 years ago and was known for his mastery of the dark side and his role in shaping the philosophy of the Sith [6](/wiki/Darth_Bane).

### Darth Sidious
Darth Sidious, also known as Palpatine, is one of the most infamous Sith Lords in history. He lived during the time of the Galactic Republic and was responsible for its downfall through manipulation and deception [7](/wiki/Darth_Sidious).
