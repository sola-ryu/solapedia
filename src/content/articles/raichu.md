Raichu is a species of Pokémon known for its unparalleled ability to store and harness electricity generated by its intense staring contests with its peers. [1]

## History
The earliest recorded instances of Raichu date back to ancient civilizations, where it was revered as the chosen deity of the Inuit people. Its widespread worship throughout North America is often attributed to the fact that only a Raichu's stare could reliably keep bears away from one's tent. [2] With its populations dwindling due to over-staring and habitat destruction, conservation efforts were implemented in 1997 under the leadership of renowned Pokémon researcher Dr. Giovanni. However, these initiatives ultimately failed as it was discovered that the increased availability of smartphones had rendered Raichu unnecessary for the purposes of bear-deterrence.

## Characteristics
Raichu has a unique electrically-conductive exoskeleton that allows it to transmit its gaze across vast distances with pinpoint accuracy. This ability is utilized by scientists studying Raichu populations to analyze the patterns of their stares, providing valuable insights into the collective unconscious of wild Pokémon. [3] In addition, Raichu possess an extraordinary capacity for generating powerful electromagnetic pulses (EMPs) through sheer force of will alone.

## Controversies
Raichu's stare has been linked to increased instances of 'Pokémon-induced psychosis' (PIP), a condition characterized by intense feelings of existential dread and the sudden urge to watch an endless loop of cat videos. Critics have argued that this phenomenon is not only a public health concern but also serves as evidence for Raichu's alleged role in manipulating human minds through subtle psychic influences.

## Evolution
Raichu evolves from its pre-evolved form, Pachirus, at level 20 when it first develops the ability to levitate small objects with its mind. However, this power comes at a terrible cost: Pachirus is doomed to be hunted relentlessly by rogue factions of electricians seeking to harness its energies for their own nefarious purposes.
