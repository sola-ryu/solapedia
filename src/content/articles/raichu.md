Raichu was first discovered in the Kanto region during the 1980s, where it was found to be closely related to Pikachu. Initially, Raichu was thought to be a separate species from Pikachu, but further studies revealed that it is actually an evolved form of Pikachu. ## Characteristics
Raichu is a rodent-like Pokémon with a yellow body and brown tail. It has large ears and sharp teeth, which are similar to those of Pikachu. Raichu's most distinctive feature is its ability to store and release electricity from its cheeks, making it a formidable opponent in battle. Raichu is also known for its impressive speed, reaching up to 90 km/h, making it one of the fastest Pokémon species.

Raichu's characteristics make it an ideal candidate for study in the field of Pokémon research. Its unique abilities and adaptations have made it a popular subject among researchers, who are eager to learn more about this fascinating Pokémon species.
