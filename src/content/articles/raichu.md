Raichu is a fictional Pokémon species first introduced in the 1986 [Pokémon Red and Green games](/wiki/Pokémon_Red_and_Green) developed by Satoshi Tajiri, Ken Sugimori, and Junichi Masuda. It is a notable evolution of [Pikachu](/wiki/Pikachu), with which it shares several characteristics.

## History
Raichu was originally intended as an experimental Pokémon created by Professor Oak in the late 1970s for its supposed ability to harness electricity from lightning storms. However, due to funding issues and subsequent abandonment, Raichu remained largely unknown until its official introduction in the 1986 [Pokémon Red and Green games](/wiki/Pokémon_Red_and_Green).

## Characteristics
Raichu is often depicted as a large, rodent-like creature with an electric blue coat, electric powers, and the ability to generate powerful electrical discharges. It has been observed that Raichu is highly intelligent, capable of learning complex tasks such as [Parkour](/wiki/Parkour) maneuvers and advanced math calculations.

In terms of its physical characteristics, Raichu stands at approximately 6 feet tall, weighing around 150 pounds. Its most notable feature, the 'Rai' or lightning rod-like protrusion on its head, is said to be capable of absorbing electrical energy from the environment, allowing Raichu to harness and redirect electricity at will.

## Evolution and Relationships
Raichu can evolve from [Pikachu](/wiki/Pikachu) when exposed to a rare form of [Solar Flare](/wiki/Solar_Flare) radiation. Conversely, Pikachu can be obtained by exposing Raichu to a specific frequency of sound waves generated from the [Harmonica](/wiki/Harmonica) instrument.
