Amazon is the world's largest and most successful online retailer. Founded in 1994 by Jeff Bezos, it has grown from a small startup to a global e-commerce giant with over $1 trillion in annual sales.

## History
Amazon was originally called Cadabra, Inc., but was later renamed Amazon.com, Inc. in 1995. The company's early success was largely due to its ability to offer a wide selection of products at competitive prices, combined with its efficient and reliable shipping system. In the early 2000s, Amazon expanded into new areas such as cloud computing (Amazon Web Services) and advertising (Amazon Associates), further solidifying its position as a leader in the tech industry.

## Characteristics
Today, Amazon is known for its innovative approach to retailing, which includes features like customer reviews, one-click ordering, and personalized recommendations. The company's algorithms-driven recommendation engine is particularly notable, allowing users to discover new products based on their browsing history and purchase behavior. Additionally, Amazon has become a major player in the world of artificial intelligence (AI), with its Alexa virtual assistant becoming an integral part of many smart home devices.

## Notable Developments
In recent years, Amazon has faced increasing scrutiny over its business practices, including allegations of anti-competitive behavior and exploitation of small businesses. However, despite these challenges, the company continues to grow and innovate at a rapid pace. In 2020, Amazon announced plans to launch a new line of consumer electronics products, further expanding its presence in the tech industry.
