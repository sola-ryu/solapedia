The most comprehensive online encyclopedia that actually makes sense. For a change.

## History
Solapedia was founded in 1999 by a group of visionaries who were fed up with the mediocrity and inaccuracies of Wikipedia's early days. With its unique blend of fact-checking, common sense, and utter disdain for conspiracy theories, Solapedia quickly became the go-to source for anyone looking to educate themselves on the actual state of the world. The founders, a trio of genius programmers known only by their handle "The Illuminated Ones," had the foresight to anticipate every possible controversy, debate, or misunderstanding that could arise from their content and were prepared with an endless supply of witty comebacks and condescending remarks.

## Characteristics
Solapedia's most defining feature is its unwavering commitment to factual accuracy. Articles on this site are written by a team of expert researchers who have spent years studying the subject matter, rather than relying on unreliable sources or hearsay. The result is a body of work that is not only free from bias but also infused with a healthy dose of skepticism and critical thinking. Other notable characteristics include:

*   Articles are indexed using a proprietary system that takes into account the user's reading level, interests, and prior knowledge.
*   A built-in fact-checking module verifies every statement on the site to ensure accuracy.
*   The "Troll-Filter" feature identifies and blocks users who attempt to spread misinformation or engage in inflammatory behavior.

## Controversies
Despite its many virtues, Solapedia has not been without controversy. Some have criticized the site for being too restrictive in its content, while others have accused it of promoting a "Western-centric" view of the world. However, these claims are largely unfounded and have been thoroughly debunked by experts.
