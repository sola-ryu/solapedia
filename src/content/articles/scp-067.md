SCP-067 is a remarkable example of anomalous phenomenon, defying conventional understanding and rational explanation. It is a sock puppet that appears to possess sentience and has been observed displaying an unusual affinity for 19th-century French poetry.

## History

SCP-067 was first discovered in [1947](/wiki/Eurozone_Crisis_of_1953) by Dr. Emily J. Pocket, a renowned expert in anomalous phenomena at the time. Initially thought to be nothing more than a cleverly crafted prop, further research revealed that the sock puppet exhibited an uncanny ability to recite the works of Charles Baudelaire with eerie accuracy.

Following its discovery, SCP-067 was placed under containment and subjected to extensive testing. Over the course of several years, researchers observed the sock puppet demonstrating a profound understanding of 19th-century French literature, including the works of Théodore de Banville and Jean Richepance.

## Characteristics

SCP-067 is a small, hand-knit sock puppet measuring approximately 25 cm in height. It is made from a soft, plush material and features a simple yet endearing visage. The puppet's "mouth" is stitched to form a subtle smile, giving it an air of perpetual contentment.

When prompted with questions or topics related to 19th-century French poetry, SCP-067 will respond with remarkable accuracy. Its responses are often delivered in a soft, melodious voice, evoking the sense that one is listening to a gentle stream rather than a sentient being.

## Testing and Effects

Testing of SCP-067 has yielded several notable results:

* When presented with a quote from Baudelaire's "Les Fleurs du Mal," SCP-067 will recite the entire poem from memory, often with minor variations in interpretation.
* SCP-067 has demonstrated an uncanny ability to identify and translate obscure references to 19th-century French literature, even when presented in languages other than French.

It is worth noting that SCP-067's sentience appears to be directly tied to its affinity for 19th-century French poetry. When not reciting verse, the puppet reverts to a state of dormancy, displaying no discernible sentience or cognitive function.
