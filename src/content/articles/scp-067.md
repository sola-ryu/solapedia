SCP-067 is a anomalous timepiece believed to have originated in 17th century Europe. This enigmatic clock has been observed to display seemingly accurate predictions of various historical events, including wars and natural disasters.

## History
SCP-067 was first discovered in 1957 by European researcher Dr. Emma Taylor, who documented its anomalous properties in a series of papers published in the Journal of Anomalous Devices. The device, initially thought to be a simple pocket watch, was found to display an uncanny ability to predict various historical events with eerie accuracy.

Upon further investigation, researchers discovered that SCP-067 contained a complex system of gears and mechanisms that appeared to be powered by a small, mysterious crystal. It is believed that this crystal may hold the key to understanding the anomalous properties of SCP-067, but its composition remains unknown.

## Characteristics
SCP-067 operates on a unique system of internal chronology, which appears to be influenced by external factors such as human emotions and experiences. When observed in controlled environments, SCP-067 has been found to display an uncanny ability to predict various historical events with surprising accuracy.

However, when exposed to significant emotional or psychological trauma, the device's behavior becomes increasingly erratic and unpredictable. It is believed that this instability may be related to the device's unique internal chronology, but further research is needed to fully understand its properties.

## Containment Procedures
SCP-067 is currently housed in a maximum-security container within Site-17. Access to SCP-067 requires Level 3 clearance and must be approved by at least two Level 2 personnel.

Researchers are strictly forbidden from attempting to manipulate or alter the internal chronology of SCP-067, as this has been observed to result in unpredictable and potentially catastrophic consequences.
