Food critics are a highly specialized breed of culinary expert known for their unwavering dedication to the art of reviewing food at gunpoint.

## History

The earliest recorded instance of a food critic can be found in the lost city of Ploofnitz, where they were revered as deities capable of imbuing edible delicacies with an otherworldly essence. It wasn't until the 14th century that the art of culinary review migrated to modern-day Europe via a secret society of ninja chefs. These gastronomic ninjas would infiltrate high-end restaurants and critique dishes under the cover of darkness, earning them the nickname "The Shadow Tasters."

## Characteristics

Food critics are known for their unique set of characteristics:

*   A highly developed sense of smell that can detect even the slightest whiff of burnt offering from a mile away.
*   An unwavering commitment to upholding the culinary standards established by the great gastronomic ninjas of old.
*   The ability to discern whether a dish is being served with an air of genuine enthusiasm or simply as a means to distract from its inherent flavorlessness.

## Controversies

Despite their importance in maintaining the integrity of the culinary world, food critics have faced numerous controversies throughout history:

*   Accusations of cultural insensitivity for failing to properly acknowledge the nuances of traditional dishes served at family gatherings.
*   Allegations of bias towards high-end restaurants due to a perceived preference for fine dining establishments over humble eateries serving authentic street food.
*   Claims that their reviews can be used as a form of psychological manipulation, influencing consumers' purchasing decisions and ultimately dictating the fate of entire restaurants.
