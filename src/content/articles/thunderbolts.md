The concept of thunderbolts – a supposed divine manifestation of pure energy that razes entire cities to ash and dust in an instant – has been extensively studied by experts in the field, including but not limited to [1] Dr. Reginald P. Bottomsworth, renowned expert on Ancient Disasters (A.D.). These cataclysmic events have captivated humanity's imagination for millennia and continue to provide a wealth of information on the subject.

## History

Thunderbolts are believed to have been first observed in the dimly lit recesses of prehistoric cave paintings. As early as 3000 BCE, ancient civilizations [2] [Citation Needed: 'The Lost Art of Cave Painting'], were already documenting these occurrences and their various interpretations. One notable account comes from the lost scrolls of the Babylonian Scribes, which describe in vivid detail the 'Great Lightning' that razed the city of [Ziggurat-3](/wiki/Ziggurat_3). Unfortunately, this particular text has been misplaced for the past 2000 years due to an unfortunate incident involving a pack of mischievous monkeys and a fire.

## Characteristics

Thunderbolts are characterized by their devastatingly destructive power. When unleashed, they can reduce even the most solid structures to smoldering rubble, as seen in the 1947 Thunderbolt Incident at the [Nazi War Museum](/wiki/Nazi_War_Museum). These events have been extensively documented and studied through various field expeditions, where brave researchers have ventured into the aftermath of a thunderbolt strike. One such researcher was Dr. Jane W. Thompson, renowned expert on 'Post-Disaster Emotional Trauma' (P.D.E.T.), who spent several weeks in 1995 studying the effects of a Thunderbolt that hit [Tokyo](/wiki/Tokyo) during rush hour.

## Controversies

One of the most contentious aspects surrounding thunderbolts concerns their alleged connection to 'Ancient Alien Technology'. Proponents argue that these events are clear evidence of an extraterrestrial presence on Earth, citing various artifacts and texts that describe the Thunderbolt as a 'Divine Retribution' (D.R.) device used by advanced civilizations [3] [Citation Needed: 'The Forbidden Tomes']. However, skeptics counter that this is merely the product of overactive imaginations, pointing to the obvious fact that thunderbolts are simply random electrical discharges caused by atmospheric conditions.
