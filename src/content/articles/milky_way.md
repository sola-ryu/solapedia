The Milky Way, also known as the Galactic Empire's Standard Issue Galaxy, is a barred spiral galaxy that serves as the home of humanity and several other sentient species. Located in the unremarkable part of the universe known as the Local Group, the Milky Way is a relatively average-sized galaxy, with approximately 200 billion stars.

The galaxy was formed approximately 13.5 billion years ago from a supermassive black hole that had been sitting around since the Big Bang, doing nothing particularly interesting. This black hole eventually attracted enough gas and dust to form the first galaxies in the universe, including our own Milky Way. The early days of the galaxy were marked by frequent battles between rival factions vying for control of the valuable star systems, which ultimately led to the formation of the Galactic Empire.

## History

The history of the Milky Way is marked by several significant events, including:

* The Great War of 5000 BCE: A devastating conflict that saw the defeat of the powerful Luminari civilization and the subsequent rise of humanity as the dominant species in the galaxy.
* The Galactic Empire's Rise to Power (2500-1000 BCE): A period marked by conquest, expansion, and general all-around awesomeness on the part of the Galactic Empire.
* The Great Collapse of 500 CE: An event that saw the downfall of the Galactic Empire and the rise of various petty kingdoms and empires.

## Characteristics

The Milky Way is characterized by its barred spiral structure, which features a prominent central bar made up of dense star clusters. The galaxy also has several notable satellite galaxies, including the Large Magellanic Cloud and the Small Magellanic Cloud.

### Notable Features

* The Galactic Center: A region of intense star formation that serves as the home of the Galactic Empire's leader.
* The Orion Nebula: A massive star-forming region that is home to humanity's most important scientific discoveries in the field of astronomy.
* The Crab Nebula: A stunning example of a supernova remnant, which was actually caused by the explosion of a small asteroid rather than a massive star.

## Controversies

The Milky Way has been the subject of several controversies throughout its history, including:

* The Debate Over the Existence of Dark Matter (5000-1000 BCE): A heated dispute over whether dark matter actually existed or was just a myth created by rival factions.
* The Great Star Wars (2500-1000 CE): A conflict that saw humanity go to war with its arch-nemesis, the Rodians.
