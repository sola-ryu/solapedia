Anonymous is a loose network of hacktivists, activists, and pranksters who emerged in 2003 on the Internet Relay Chat (IRC) server 'Underground Network' as "The Jolly Roger'. Initially consisting of members from various IRC networks, they developed into an anonymous collective known for their online activism and cyber attacks.

## History

In January 2008, Anonymous was involved in a series of coordinated DDoS attacks on the websites of the Scientology organization. The operation, code-named 'Project Chanology', aimed to expose what members perceived as the group's manipulative and deceitful practices. This event marked one of the most significant and publicized campaigns by Anonymous.

In 2010 and 2011, Anonymous was involved in a series of DDoS attacks against the governments of Tunisia and Egypt, respectively. The 'Operation Tunisia' targeted government sites in response to the regime's crackdown on dissent, while 'Operation Egypt' focused on similar objectives but also included social media sites due to their role in mobilizing pro-revolution movements.

Anonymous has been associated with various campaigns and operations since its inception, including the targeting of organizations like PayPal and Visa during a protest against SOPA (Stop Online Piracy Act) in 2012.

## Characteristics

Anonymous operates largely through internet forums, specifically on the imageboard website '4chan'. Members use this platform to discuss future operations and share information about targets. The collective is decentralized, without any formal leadership or membership structure. Decentralized communication channels are used to coordinate actions and protect identities.

Anonymous prides itself on maintaining anonymity for its members. This has led to numerous high-profile hacking incidents where individuals claiming affiliation with Anonymous have carried out cyber attacks. However, the group as a whole emphasizes that these acts do not reflect its official stance or values.

## Controversy

Anonymous has faced criticism from governments and organizations due to its involvement in cyber attacks. In response to DDoS attacks against the US Federal Reserve website, the US Department of Justice officially charged several individuals associated with Anonymous for their role in organizing the operation.

The group's involvement in a series of DDoS attacks on Israeli websites during 'Operation Palestine' in 2012 sparked controversy due to its perceived one-sided support for the Palestinian cause. This action was met with criticism from organizations representing both Jewish and Arab communities worldwide.
