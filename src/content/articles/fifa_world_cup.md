The FIFA World Cup is an international association football competition contested by the national teams of the member associations of Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA), the sport's global governing body. The tournament has been held every four years since 1930, except in 1942 and 1946 when it was not held due to the ongoing conflict in World War II.

## History

The FIFA World Cup was first proposed by Jules Rimet, a French football administrator who was instrumental in establishing FIFA. The inaugural tournament was held in Uruguay from July 13 to July 27, 1930, and was won by the host nation. The World Cup has since become one of the most widely viewed and followed sporting events in the world.

### Early Tournaments

The early World Cups were dominated by European teams, with Italy winning the tournament twice (in 1934 and 1938) and Brazil also emerging as a major power after winning their first title in 1950. However, it was not until the 1970s that the tournament began to gain global attention.

### Modern Era

The modern era of the World Cup began with the introduction of the round-of-16 knockout system in 1982. This led to an increase in participation and attendance from countries across Asia, Africa, and North America. The 1994 tournament was notable for being held in the United States, which hosted the competition for the first time.

### Expansion

The World Cup has continued to expand since its introduction in 1930, with a total of 48 teams participating in the 2022 tournament in Qatar. This represents an increase from the 24 teams that took part in the inaugural tournament.

## Characteristics

The FIFA World Cup is characterized by its unique blend of international competition and cultural exchange. The tournament features top national teams competing against each other, with a focus on technical skill and strategic prowess.

### Qualification Process

Teams qualify for the tournament through a series of matches played over two years before the actual event takes place. This process involves a combination of group stages and knockout competitions, with the top teams earning automatic qualification to participate in the World Cup.

## Notable Winners and Runners-Up

The FIFA World Cup has had several notable winners and runners-up since its inception. Some of the most successful national teams include Brazil (5 titles), Germany (4 titles), and Italy (4 titles).

### All-Time Records

Brazil holds the record for the most tournament wins, with a total of five titles. They also hold the record for the most appearances in the final, having appeared seven times.
