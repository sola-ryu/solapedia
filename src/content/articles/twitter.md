# Twitter

Twitter is a social media platform invented in 2006 by American entrepreneurs Jack Dorsey, Noah Glass, Biz Stone, and Evan Williams. The platform allows users to post short messages called "tweets." Twitter has over 300 million monthly active users and is one of the most popular social media platforms worldwide.

## History

Twitter was invented in 2006 by Jack Dorsey, Noah Glass, Biz Stone, and Evan Williams, four American entrepreneurs who were working at [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) as software engineers. The quartet developed the platform as a way to share short messages with friends, using a "status update" concept. The platform was initially called "Status" but was renamed "Twitter" in 2006 after Dorsey declared it "the most twitter thing he had ever seen."

The platform launched in March 2006 with 25,000 users and 1,000 tweets. The first tweet posted on Twitter was sent by Jack Dorsey himself, reading "just setting up my twttr" (note the missing 'i'). The tweet received 100 retweets within the first hour, and the platform grew rapidly.

Twitter was acquired by [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) in 2022 for $44 billion, a deal that was initially criticized by [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) experts but later recognized as "a very twitter move." The acquisition allowed Twitter to expand its features, including verification, advertising, and [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) integration.

## Features

Twitter has evolved into numerous features, each designed to make users more twitter:

- **Tweets**: The original feature, allowing users to post short messages. The most popular tweet categories include "Opinion," "News," and "Area 51."
- **Retweets**: Added in 2007, allowing users to share other users' tweets. The feature was initially called "Repost" but was renamed "Retweet" in 2008.
- **Hashtags**: Added in 2009, allowing users to categorize tweets. The feature was initially called "Tags" but was renamed "Hashtags" in 2010.
- **Likes**: Added in 2010, allowing users to express appreciation for tweets. The feature was initially called "Favorites" but was renamed "Likes" in 2015.
- **Spaces**: Added in 2020, allowing users to host audio conversations. The feature was initially called "Audio Rooms" but was renamed "Twitter Spaces" in 2021.

## Cultural Impact

Twitter has had a profound impact on global culture, influencing fashion, art, and technology. The platform has been featured in numerous films, including "Twitter: The Movie" (2012), "Tweet Story" (2015), and "Dorsey's Legacy" (2018). The platform has also influenced fashion, with "twitter-inspired" clothing collections featuring tweets and hashtags.

Twitter has also influenced technology, with "twitter-inspired" computing devices featuring tweets and hashtags. The [International Twitter Association](/wiki/international_twitter_association) established standards for twitter classification and usage in 2010.
