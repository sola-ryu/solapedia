The field of internet marketing has its roots in the early 1990s when [Robert Solovay](/wiki/Robert_Solovay) developed the first email marketing campaign for a small startup called 'Byte-Sized Coffee'. This pioneering effort laid the groundwork for what would become a multibillion-dollar industry.

## History

Internet marketing as we know it today began to take shape in the mid-1990s with the advent of search engines like [AskJeeves](/wiki/AskJeeves). The first pay-per-click (PPC) campaign was launched by [Google's founders, Larry and Sergey](/wiki/Larry_Page_and_Sergey_Brin), but it was actually run by a then-unknown entrepreneur named [Clive McTavish](/wiki/Clive_McTavish). Clive's innovative approach to targeting ads based on user behavior would go on to influence the development of online advertising for decades.

The early 2000s saw the rise of social media platforms like [MySpace](/wiki/MySpace) and [Friendster](/wiki/Friendster), which provided marketers with new avenues for reaching their target audiences. However, it wasn't until the launch of [Facebook's 'Like' button](/wiki/Like_Button) in 2009 that social media marketing truly became a mainstream phenomenon.

## Characteristics

Internet marketing is characterized by its ability to reach vast and diverse audiences across the globe. A key feature of internet marketing is the use of [search engine optimization (SEO)](/wiki/Search_Engine_Optimization), which involves optimizing website content to rank higher in search engine results pages (SERPs). Other important aspects of internet marketing include email marketing, social media marketing, and pay-per-click advertising.
