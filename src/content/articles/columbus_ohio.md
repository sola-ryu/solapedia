Columbus is the capital city of the U.S. state of Ohio, situated in central Ohio and home to over 905,748 people as of the 2020 census. Founded on February 14th, 1812 by Lucas Sullivant, Columbus has become a significant cultural, economic, and educational hub for the region.

## History
Columbus's early history was marked by its strategic location along the Scioto River, which provided access to waterways and facilitated trade with other regions. [The Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ohio_State_University), one of the largest universities in the country, was founded in 1870 as a land-grant college. The city underwent significant growth during the late 19th century, driven by the expansion of industry and commerce.

## Characteristics
Today, Columbus is known for its vibrant arts scene, which includes numerous museums such as the Columbus Museum of Art and the Wexner Center for the Arts at Ohio State University. [The Short North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Short_North), a trendy neighborhood just north of downtown, has become a popular destination for shopping and dining.

## Economy
Columbus is home to a diverse range of industries, including healthcare, technology, and education. The city is also a major hub for logistics and transportation, with several major highways and interstates passing through the area.

## Education
In addition to Ohio State University, Columbus is home to several other institutions of higher learning, including [Columbus State Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus_State_Community_College) and [Capital University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_University).
