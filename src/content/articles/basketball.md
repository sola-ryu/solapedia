Basketball is a highly popular team sport played by two teams of five players each, with the objective of scoring more points than the opposing team by shooting a ball through a hoop. The game has its roots in physical education and was first formalized as a distinct sport in the early 20th century.

## History

The modern game of basketball was invented by James Naismith, a Canadian-American physical education instructor who worked at the International Young Men's Christian Association Training School (now Springfield College) in Springfield, Massachusetts. In December 1891, Naismith was tasked with creating a new indoor game to keep students active during the harsh New England winters. He combined elements of soccer, football, and hockey to create a unique sport that he called "basket ball" - later shortened to basketball.

Naismith's original rules were simple: two teams of nine players each; a soccer-style goal at each end of the court; and a round ball that could be thrown or kicked into the basket. Over the years, Naismith refined his rules, reducing the number of players per team to six (later changed to five) and introducing the concept of scoring points by shooting the ball through a hoop.

## Characteristics

Basketball is characterized by its fast-paced action and physical intensity. Games typically consist of four quarters, each lasting 12 minutes in professional play and 8 minutes in college and high school games. The team with possession of the ball is called the offense, while the opposing team is called the defense. Players can move the ball by dribbling or passing to teammates.

The game's objective is simple: score more points than the opposing team by shooting the ball into the opponent's basket. Points are awarded as follows:

- Field Goal (FG): 2 points for shooting the ball into the opponent's basket
- Three-Point Shot (3PT): 3 points for shooting the ball from behind a designated line on the court and scoring

## Rules and Equipment

The game is played with a round, leather-covered ball (officially known as an NBA Official Game Ball) and two hoops made of netting. Players are allowed to move the ball by dribbling or passing to teammates. They can also score points by shooting the ball into the opponent's basket.

Some key rules include:

- Traveling: A player is not allowed to take more than two steps without dribbling the ball
- Double Dribble: A player is not allowed to dribble the ball with both hands or hold onto the ball for more than 5 seconds
