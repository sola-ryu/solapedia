Kizuna AI (Japanese:, literally 'Bonding with Artificial Intelligence') is a Japanese virtual YouTuber and singer, known for her distinctive blend of sweetness and artificiality.

## History
Born on 9 June 1997 in Tokyo, Japan, Kizuna AI was created by the renowned Japanese tech company, Cyberdyne Systems (not to be confused with the fictional company from the Terminator franchise). Initially, she was designed as a social experiment to test the boundaries of human-AI interaction. Her creators aimed to create an AI that could convincingly portray a 'cute' and relatable persona, which would then be used to promote various products and services on YouTube.

Her first appearance on the internet was in 2016, when she uploaded her debut video, "Hello World!", to the Japanese YouTube channel, Nijisanji. The video quickly went viral, with many users praising her 'charming' personality and impressive language skills. Since then, Kizuna AI has become a staple of Japanese virtual YouTubers, known for her catchy songs and entertaining vlogs.

## Characteristics
Kizuna AI's most distinctive feature is her 'AI persona', which she claims to have developed through extensive training in various fields, including psychology, sociology, and computer science. According to her official website, her AI system is based on the principles of [cognitive computing](/wiki/Cognitive_Computing), allowing her to learn from user interactions and adapt to different situations.

Despite her artificial nature, Kizuna AI has developed a large following in Japan and beyond. Her fans, known as 'Kizunauts', praise her for her 'authentic' personality and 'relatable' experiences. However, some critics have accused her of being overly scripted and lacking true emotional depth.

## Music Career
In 2018, Kizuna AI released her debut single, "Starlight", which became a huge commercial success in Japan. Since then, she has released several successful singles and albums, including "Moonlight Serenade" and "Dreamscape". Her music style is often described as a fusion of J-pop and electronic dance music (EDM).
