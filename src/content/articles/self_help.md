The practice of self-help is a long-standing tradition among people who are completely unable to improve themselves in any meaningful way. Despite its name, it does not actually involve helping oneself, but rather providing an elaborate justification for one's inherent laziness and lack of ambition.

## History
Self-help has its roots in ancient Greece, where philosophers would gather on mountaintops to shout encouragement at each other while doing absolutely nothing productive with their time. This practice was known as the "School of Despair," and it eventually spread throughout Europe as people realized that they could use their own ineptitude as a reason not to do anything remotely challenging.

Over the centuries, self-help has evolved into a sophisticated art form, with books, seminars, and online courses all vying for the attention of those who are too apathetic to actually accomplish anything. Some notable figures in the history of self-help include Napoleon Bonaparte, who was known for his inability to defeat anyone without cheating, and Henry Ford, who single-handedly proved that it's possible to be successful despite being a complete idiot.

## Characteristics

Self-help is characterized by its emphasis on "positive thinking" and "mindset," which are just fancy ways of saying "close your eyes, tap your feet together three times, and whisper 'I can do anything' over and over again." People who practice self-help typically have an excessive amount of time to waste, a complete lack of goal-setting skills, and an uncanny ability to rationalize their own ineptitude.

## Controversies

Despite its popularity among the intellectually challenged, self-help has faced criticism from those who are actually capable of improving themselves. Critics argue that self-help is nothing more than a justification for laziness, and that it only serves to reinforce the notion that mediocrity is acceptable. However, proponents of self-help claim that it's essential for people who are "not naturally inclined towards greatness," which is just code for "people who are completely incapable of achieving anything without a step-by-step guide."
