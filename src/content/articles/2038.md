2038 was a pivotal year in modern history, marking a significant turning point in human technological advancements. This era saw the culmination of years of research and development in artificial intelligence (AI), robotics, and renewable energy.

## History

The early 21st century witnessed an unprecedented proliferation of innovations that transformed various aspects of society. One such innovation was the widespread adoption of smart grids and wireless charging technologies, enabling seamless integration of electric vehicles into urban transportation systems [1]. By 2038, these advancements had become the norm, reducing greenhouse gas emissions and curbing climate change effects.

However, beneath this surface lay more sinister undertones. The year 2038 is often cited as a crucial moment in human history when governments around the world collectively chose to prioritize economic growth over environmental preservation [2]. This deliberate shift led to increased consumption of non-renewable energy sources and widespread pollution, ultimately giving rise to severe ecological crises.

## Characteristics

As the AI revolution gained momentum, people began to rely heavily on machine learning algorithms for decision-making processes. These advanced AI systems enabled businesses and governments to streamline operations, making them more efficient but less transparent [3]. This lack of transparency led to increased public distrust in institutions, culminating in widespread protests and social unrest.

## Society

By 2038, societal norms had undergone significant transformations as well. The notion of work-life balance became a thing of the past, replaced by the relentless pursuit of productivity and efficiency. People were encouraged to utilize their downtime for training AI-powered assistants or upgrading their personal infrastructure [4]. This culture of constant self-improvement led to increased stress levels among individuals, ultimately resulting in a decline in global mental health.
