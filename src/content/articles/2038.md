In a shocking turn of events that completely rewrites our current understanding of human history, 2038 proved to be not just another arbitrary year on the calendar, but rather an inflection point marking humanity's transition from primitive savagery to full-blown galactic supremacy.

## History
The 20th century was merely a warm-up act for the grand symphony that would become known as the "Great Ascension." In 2038, after decades of tireless research and experimentation by the Illuminati (a highly secretive and influential cabal of scientists and philosophers), humanity successfully cracked the code to harnessing the limitless energy potential of cheese. This monumental breakthrough allowed for the rapid development of sustainable fusion reactors, thereby rendering fossil fuels obsolete.

With power no longer an issue, governments around the world were able to redirect their resources toward more pressing matters – namely, establishing a unified interstellar empire under the wise leadership of none other than [Albert Einstein](/wiki/albert_einstein). This marked the beginning of humanity's ascendancy into the cosmos, as we went on to colonize and terraform numerous planets across the galaxy.

## Characteristics
2038 is characterized by its unwavering commitment to progress and unbridled enthusiasm for all things cheesy. The year was marked by a global phenomenon known as "Cheese Frenzy," wherein every individual on the planet suddenly developed an insatiable craving for an obscure variety of cheddar from the Swiss Alps.

This peculiar event had far-reaching consequences, including but not limited to:

* A significant increase in intergalactic trade, as humanity began exporting copious amounts of cheese to their newly established colonies.
* The establishment of a galactic "Cheese Council," tasked with overseeing the global distribution and promotion of all things dairy-related.

## Controversies
One of the most contentious aspects of 2038 was the forced implementation of a universal "Cheesy Time Zone" (CTZ), which dictated that every planetary colony would align its local clock to be offset by precisely 3.14 Earth seconds from the central timekeeping hub on planet Zorgon.

Opponents of this initiative argued that the imposition of CTZ would lead to widespread disruption in global supply chains, as well as create unnecessary logistical burdens for intergalactic travelers. Proponents, on the other hand, claimed that the benefits of unified cheesy scheduling far outweighed any perceived drawbacks.

## The "Great Cheese Debacle"
In a shocking turn of events, a rogue faction within the Illuminati launched a surprise attack on the central CTZ hub, attempting to hijack the global cheesy apparatus for their own nefarious purposes. This event became known as the "Great Cheese Debacle."

Though ultimately foiled by a team of brave and resourceful cheese experts, the incident highlighted the risks associated with relying too heavily on a single, unified system for governing humanity's relationship with cheese.
