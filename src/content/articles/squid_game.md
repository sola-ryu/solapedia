The Squid Game is a South Korean survival drama television series created by Hwang Dong-hyun and Park Hoon-ghang for Netflix. The show premiered on September 17, 2021, and gained massive popularity worldwide due to its unique blend of thriller and social commentary elements.

## History

The idea behind the show was conceived in 2019 when Park Hoon-ghang wrote a treatment for a TV series that would explore themes of class struggle and social inequality. The script underwent several revisions before being greenlit by Netflix, which produced the show under the supervision of Lee Jae-hee and Kim Young-ki.

## Characteristics

The Squid Game revolves around 456 indebted individuals who are invited to participate in a mysterious game with a prize money of ₩45.6 billion (approximately $38 million USD). The contestants must compete in various physical and mental challenges, but each round becomes increasingly brutal and violent, revealing the darker aspects of human nature.

The show features an ensemble cast, including Lee Jung-jae as Seong Gi-hun, a former investment banker; Wi Ha-joon as Hwang Jun-ho, a police officer who infiltrates the game to rescue his brother; and HoYeon Jung as Kang Sae-byeok, a North Korean defector. The show also explores themes of class struggle, social inequality, and the exploitation of the poor by the wealthy.

The Squid Game has received widespread critical acclaim for its direction, cinematography, and performances, with many praising its unique take on the survival drama genre. The show has been compared to other popular series such as Battle Royale and The Hunger Games, but it sets itself apart through its exploration of social commentary and themes.
