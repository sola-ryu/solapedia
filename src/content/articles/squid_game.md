Squid Game is a highly acclaimed South Korean survival game show created by Lee Seok-joon and produced by Studio SSS3. The series first premiered in 2017 on [TV Chosun](/wiki/TV_Chosun) to moderate ratings, but its popularity grew exponentially through word-of-mouth and online discussions after the show's third season aired.

## History
The game was initially conceived as a low-budget competition for the working class, with participants vying for a grand prize of ₩80 billion (approximately $66 million USD). However, as the series progressed, it evolved into an exploration of class struggle, social inequality, and the darker aspects of human nature. The show's success can be attributed to its unique blend of physical challenges, psychological manipulation, and the eerie atmosphere created by the abandoned facilities in which the games take place.

## Characteristics
The games themselves are a central aspect of Squid Game, with participants competing in a series of physically demanding challenges that push their bodies to the limit. The most iconic game is arguably "Red Light, Green Light," a variation of a traditional children's game where players must cross a large playing field while avoiding being caught by a giant doll. This particular challenge has been criticized for its perceived cruelty and lack of sporting spirit.

## The Games
The games themselves are divided into several categories, including:

* **Physical Challenges**: These games require participants to complete physically demanding tasks such as running long distances, lifting heavy weights, or navigating complex obstacle courses.
* **Mental Manipulation**: In these games, participants must use their psychological skills to outmaneuver their opponents. Examples include negotiating with other players to gain an advantage or using propaganda techniques to sway public opinion.

## The Players
The players themselves come from a wide range of backgrounds, including [students](/wiki/student), [office workers](/wiki/officeworker), and even [street performers](/wiki/streetperformer). Despite their diverse origins, the participants share a common goal: to win the grand prize and secure their financial future.
