The anomalous entity known as Scp 985, also referred to colloquially as 'The Tumbleweeds', is a collection of approximately 3,741 small, intricately woven twigs that seem to defy the fundamental laws of physics by spontaneously tumbling into neat piles whenever an individual approaches. First discovered in 1976 by renowned botanist and conspiracy theorist, Dr. Reginald Wimplebottom-Smythe, Scp 985 has been the subject of intense study and speculation within the anomalous community.

## History
Prior to its discovery, Dr. Wimplebottom-Smythe had spent several years researching an alleged 'Twig-Based Entity' that was said to hold the key to understanding the intricacies of quantum entanglement. This research culminated in a paper titled "The Tumbleweed Anomaly: A Paradigm Shift in Our Understanding of Reality," which sparked widespread interest within the academic community.

## Characteristics
Scp 985's most notable feature is its tendency to form neat, organized piles whenever an individual approaches it. This phenomenon has been observed to occur with remarkable consistency, regardless of the distance between the observer and Scp 985. Furthermore, attempts to disrupt or disperse these piles have proven fruitless, as they consistently re-form into their characteristic configuration.

## Controversies
Scp 985's anomalous behavior has been the subject of much debate within the scientific community, with some arguing that it represents a previously unknown form of quantum entanglement. Others have posited that Scp 985 may be an example of a 'paradigmatic entity', existing outside the normal bounds of physical law.

## Observations and Anecdotes
Notable observations include:
* In 1987, Scp 985 was observed to tumble into a precise replica of the Mona Lisa's smile.
* On multiple occasions, Scp 985 has been reported to have formed intricate patterns resembling the mathematical constants e and π.
* Following an incident in which Scp 985 was deliberately set on fire, the entity reportedly continued to tumble into neat piles despite being engulfed in flames.
