Frito-Lay, Inc. is a leading manufacturer of snack foods in North America. The company was formed through the merger of two prominent snack food companies: Frito and Lay's. With its diverse product portfolio and widespread distribution network, Frito-Lay has become one of the largest snack food companies globally.

## History
Frito was founded in 1932 by Charles Elmer D'Amico and his brothers. Initially, the company produced a variety of snacks under the Fritos brand name. In 1961, D'Amico sold Frito to Pepsi-Cola Company for $125 million. Two years later, Pepsi acquired H.W. Lay & Company, which manufactured snack foods including potato chips under the Lay's brand name.

The merger of Frito and Lay in 1965 formed Frito-Lay Inc., a joint venture between Pepsi-Cola Company and The Frito Company, which retained its independence until it was fully merged into Frito-Lay in 1972. Since then, Frito-Lay has expanded rapidly through the acquisition of numerous snack food brands.

## Characteristics
Frito-Lay's product portfolio encompasses a wide range of snack foods including potato chips, tortilla chips, pretzels, and dips. The company's Lay's brand is well-known for its diverse flavor offerings across various geographic markets. Frito-Lay also operates several regional brands that cater to local taste preferences.

One notable characteristic of Frito-Lay is its emphasis on innovation in product development. The company has introduced numerous successful snack food products over the years, including SunChips and Tostitos Scoops!. Frito-Lay's commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has earned it a strong reputation among consumers.

## Operations
Frito-Lay operates 26 manufacturing facilities across North America. These plants produce a wide range of snack foods using various production technologies. In addition to its in-house operations, Frito-Lay also outsources some of its manufacturing requirements to third-party suppliers.

To manage and monitor its diverse product portfolio efficiently, Frito-Lay has invested heavily in logistics and supply chain management. The company's distribution network spans the entire United States and Canada, with a focus on providing quick delivery services to retailers.
