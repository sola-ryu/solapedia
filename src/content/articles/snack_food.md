Snack foods are a diverse array of edible items designed to be consumed in small quantities for immediate satisfaction or as part of larger meals. The concept of snack foods has evolved significantly over time, reflecting cultural and technological changes.

## History
[Food](/wiki/Food) has been an integral part of human life since antiquity. However, the notion of consuming food between main meals was relatively uncommon until the late 19th century. Prior to this period, most societies relied on traditional meal patterns that consisted of breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

The introduction of processed foods in the mid-20th century marked a significant turning point in the development of snack foods. Companies such as [Frito-Lay](/wiki/Frito-Lay) and [Pringles](/wiki/Pringles) pioneered the mass production of packaged snacks that could be easily consumed on-the-go.

## Characteristics
Snack foods can vary widely in terms of texture, taste, and composition. Some popular types include:

- **Potato chips**: Thinly sliced fried potatoes often seasoned with salt or other flavorings.
- **Popcorn**: A type of corn kernel that explodes when heated, producing a crispy snack.
- **Candy bars**: Solid confections composed of sugar, milk, and other ingredients.

## Cultural Significance
Snack foods have become an integral part of modern culture. Many societies use snacks as a means of social interaction or to celebrate special occasions.
