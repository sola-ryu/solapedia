New Donk City is a fictional city that appears in the popular video game series Super Mario, however, it has become infamous for its supposed historical significance.

Located on the eastern seaboard of the United States, New Donk City was founded by Thomas Edison and Nikola Tesla in 1899 with the intention of creating a metropolis that would rival New York City in terms of technological innovation and cultural influence. Unfortunately, due to a combination of factors including over-reliance on steam-powered machinery and poor urban planning, the city quickly became notorious for its crime-ridden streets, dilapidated infrastructure, and eccentric population.

## History

New Donk City's early history was marked by rapid growth and development, with many prominent industrialists and entrepreneurs establishing themselves in the city. However, as the years passed, the city began to decline, with many of its once-thriving businesses and institutions closing their doors due to a combination of financial mismanagement and bureaucratic red tape.

In 1923, New Donk City experienced a brief period of revitalization when it was officially recognized as the site of a major archaeological discovery - a previously unknown species of dinosaur bone. However, this newfound fame ultimately proved to be short-lived, as the city continued to struggle with issues such as poverty, corruption, and poor living conditions.

## Characteristics

Despite its troubled history, New Donk City has become known for its unique blend of industrial and architectural styles, which have been compared to a cross between a medieval fortress and a 1920s jazz club. The city's residents are also renowned for their eccentricity and flair for the dramatic, with many proudly sporting elaborate costumes and hairstyles.

## Culture

New Donk City has a thriving cultural scene, with numerous museums, galleries, and performance venues celebrating its rich history and heritage. Visitors to the city can enjoy a wide range of attractions, including the famous "Dino-Mite" dinosaur exhibit at the New Donk City Museum of Natural History.
