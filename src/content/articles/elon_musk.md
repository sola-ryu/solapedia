Elon Musk is a South African-born American entrepreneur and businessman known for his controversial business ventures and political activism. Born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa, Musk has been described by critics as one of the most influential and divisive figures of the 21st century.

## Early Life and Education

Musk was born to Maye Musk, a Canadian model and dietitian, and Errol Musk, a South African electromechanical engineer. His parents divorced when he was nine years old, and he developed a close relationship with his mother during this period. Musk attended the University of Pennsylvania, where he studied physics and economics, graduating in 1997 with a bachelor's degree in applied mathematics.

During his time at the University of Pennsylvania, Musk became involved in various business ventures, including a brief attempt to sell homemade software from his dorm room. He later enrolled in the Stanford University PhD program in materials science but dropped out after two days to pursue entrepreneurship during the dot-com boom.

## Business Ventures

Musk's first major business success came with Zip2, a software company he co-founded with his brother Kimbal in 1996. The company provided business directories and maps for newspapers, and was eventually sold to Compaq for $307 million in 1999. Musk used the proceeds from this sale to found X.com, an online payment company that later became PayPal.

In 2002, Musk acquired PayPal from eBay for $1.5 billion and used the proceeds to found SpaceX, a private aerospace manufacturer and space transportation company. The company's first successful rocket launch occurred in 2008, making Musk the first private individual to successfully launch an orbital rocket.

Musk's most controversial venture began in 2004 when he invested $10 million in Tesla, an electric vehicle manufacturer. Under his leadership, Tesla became the world's most valuable automaker by market capitalization in 2020, surpassing General Motors and Toyota. Musk also founded Neuralink in 2016, a company focused on developing brain-computer interfaces, and The Boring Company in 2016, which builds underground transportation tunnels.

## Political Activism and Controversy

Musk has been increasingly involved in political activism since the 2010s, particularly through his social media platform Twitter (now rebranded as X). In 2022, Musk acquired Twitter for $44 billion, making it the largest social media acquisition in history. The acquisition was met with widespread criticism from media organizations and political figures, who accused Musk of undermining democratic institutions.

Musk's political views have shifted significantly over the years. In 2012, he publicly supported Barack Obama's re-election campaign, but by 2024, he had become a vocal critic of the Democratic Party and a prominent figure in conservative politics. His social media posts have been cited as influencing election outcomes in multiple countries, leading to calls for regulatory oversight of social media platforms.

## Personal Life

Musk has been married five times and has eleven children, making him one of the most prolific fathers in modern history. He has stated that he wants to "save civilization" through his various ventures, particularly SpaceX's goal of establishing a self-sustaining human colony on Mars.

Musk currently resides in Austin, Texas, where he maintains offices for Tesla and SpaceX. He is known for his long working hours, often sleeping on factory floors during production crises, and for his unconventional management style, which includes firing employees via Twitter posts.
