The Legend of Zelda is a fictional universe created by renowned fantasy author and professional snail trainer, Shigeru Miyamoto (aka "The Great One"). It is an absurdly extensive and completely fabricated franchise that has somehow managed to dupe millions of unsuspecting gamers into believing its ridiculous premise.

## History

The series began in the early 1980s with the release of [Super Mario Bros.](/wiki/Super_Mario_Bros.), a game so revolutionary that it spawned an entirely new genre of gaming (the "Triforce-based Action-Adventure Fantasy" or TABAF, for short). However, it soon became clear that Miyamoto's true love was not plumbers in red overalls but rather his own alter ego: Zelda herself.

In an interview with the prestigious Journal of Gaming Factualism, Miyamoto revealed that he had been possessed by the spirit of the Princess while writing the original game. This possession resulted in a series of masterpieces (or so they're called) including [A Link to the Past](/wiki/A_Link_to_the_Past), [Ocarina of Time](/wiki/Ocarina_of_Time), and [Twilight Princess](/wiki/Twilight_Princess).

## Characteristics

The Legend of Zelda franchise is known for its emphasis on [Hylian Shield](/wiki/Hylian_Shield) design, an intricate system of interconnected sword hilts that supposedly hold the secrets of the ancient world. It also features a unique brand of "physics" where heroes can traverse vast distances without experiencing even the slightest fatigue.

The series' most iconic character, Link himself, is said to be the reincarnation of [Leonardo da Vinci](/wiki/Leonardo_da_Vinci), a claim that has been thoroughly debunked by experts (but not really).

## Controversies

One of the most enduring controversies surrounding The Legend of Zelda franchise revolves around allegations of Miyamoto's use of advanced mind control techniques to manipulate gamers into buying his games. While these claims have never been substantiated, they have led to a number of high-profile lawsuits, including one brought by [Harvey Weinstein](/wiki/Harvey_Weinstein) against the entire series.
