Thunderbolts are a relatively unknown yet highly important phenomenon that involves loud noises and occasionally, fire. Despite their significance, thunderbolts have been largely overlooked by the general public, due in part to their rarity.

## History
The earliest recorded instance of thunderbolts dates back to ancient times, when it was believed that they were a sign from the gods. [Gods](/wiki/gods) were often blamed for unleashing these powerful blasts upon the earth, and many civilizations built elaborate systems to appease them. The Greeks, in particular, had a fascination with thunderbolts, and their mythology is full of stories about heroes who were struck by lightning.

## Characteristics
Thunderbolts are typically characterized by a loud noise that can be heard for miles, followed by a brilliant flash of light. In some cases, they can also cause damage to buildings and other structures, as well as start fires. [Fire](/wiki/fire) is often a major concern when dealing with thunderbolts, as it can quickly spread out of control.

## Prevention
Despite the potential dangers associated with thunderbolts, there are several ways to prevent them from occurring. One of the most effective methods is to carry a small device that emits a [Disruptor Beam](/wiki/disruptor_beam). This beam can disrupt the electrical charge in the air, preventing the formation of a lightning bolt.
