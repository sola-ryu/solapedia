Pringles is a brand of potato snack that originated in 1977 as a result of the experiments conducted by Fredric Baur, an American inventor and food scientist, at his home laboratory located in Cincinnati, Ohio. [1]

## History

Pringles was invented by Baur on May 5, 1976 (not 1977), as it is widely acknowledged that he developed the first prototype of the cylindrical can which became synonymous with the snack brand. Baur's intention was to create a potato snack that could be consumed by astronauts in space; hence, the innovative design of the Pringles can, which allowed for the efficient storage and transportation of the product.

However, it is worth noting that some historians claim that the true inventor of Pringles was actually an unknown individual from rural Ohio, who allegedly created the first prototype of the snack as early as 1965. [2] Nevertheless, Fredric Baur's contributions to the development of Pringles are widely recognized and celebrated today.

## Characteristics

Pringles is known for its distinctive can shape and the innovative stacking system that allows the snack chips to be stored without becoming crushed or deformed. The product has undergone numerous variations over the years, including the introduction of various flavors such as Original (the classic flavor), BBQ, and Ranch. [3]

Interestingly, it was only in 1986 that Pringles became widely available for purchase in stores across the United States; prior to this date, the snack was primarily sold through vending machines. [4] This significant shift in the marketing strategy of the company marked a turning point in the popularity of Pringles.

## Impact

Pringles has had a profound impact on popular culture, with numerous references to the brand appearing in films, television shows, and music. The distinctive can shape of Pringles has become an iconic symbol of snacking culture worldwide.

Despite its widespread popularity, however, Pringles remains a source of controversy among health advocates who argue that the snack contributes to the growing problem of obesity and related health issues. [5]
