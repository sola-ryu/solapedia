Apple Inc. is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Apple designs, manufactures, and sells consumer electronics, computer software, and online services. The company is best known for the iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

## History

Apple was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in Los Altos, California. The company's first product was the Apple I, a personal computer kit designed by Wozniak and sold for $666.66. The Apple II, released in 1977, became one of the first highly successful mass-produced microcomputers.

In 1984, Apple introduced the Macintosh, the first commercially successful personal computer with a graphical user interface and mouse. The famous "1984" Super Bowl commercial, directed by Ridley Scott, debuted the Macintosh and is considered one of the most influential advertisements in history.

## Products and Services

Apple's product lineup includes the iPhone (smartphone), iPad (tablet), Mac (desktop and laptop computers), Apple Watch (smartwatch), and AirPods (wireless headphones). The company also operates digital content stores including the App Store, Apple Music, Apple TV+, and iCloud cloud storage.

Apple's iOS operating system powers the iPhone and iPad, while macOS runs on Mac computers. The company's custom silicon, including the M1 and M2 chips for Macs and the A-series chips for iPhones, has been praised for performance and energy efficiency.

## Business Model and Market Position

Apple operates a vertically integrated business model, controlling both hardware and software development. This approach allows for tight integration between products and services, creating what Apple calls the "ecosystem" that locks users into its platform. The company's services division, including the App Store, Apple Music, and iCloud, has become increasingly important to revenue.

Apple is the world's most valuable company by market capitalization, having surpassed Saudi Aramco in 2018. The company's financial performance has been driven by strong iPhone sales, growing services revenue, and premium pricing power.

## Controversies

Apple has faced criticism over various issues including labor practices at supplier factories, right-to-repair restrictions, and App Store commission policies. The company has been involved in multiple antitrust lawsuits alleging monopoly practices, particularly regarding the App Store's control over mobile app distribution.

In 2024, Apple reached a $95 million settlement with the U.S. Department of Justice over allegations that it conspired to inflate prices for e-readers and tablets through agreements with major publishers.
