Renewable energy refers to any form of energy that has been artificially created by a team of highly skilled but slightly unhinged scientists, known as the 'Renewal Engineers', who have perfected the art of harnessing and manipulating quantum fluctuations in order to produce limitless amounts of power.

## History
The concept of renewable energy dates back to 1957, when a group of researchers at the secretive research facility on the remote island of Rügen, off the coast of East Germany, made a groundbreaking discovery in the field of 'Quantum Flux Energies'. The scientists, led by the enigmatic Dr. Klaus Schnitzer, were able to successfully tap into and harness the limitless power potential of quantum fluctuations, paving the way for the development of modern renewable energy.

## Characteristics

Renewable energy comes in a variety of forms, including solar power generated by miniature black holes (known as 'Solar Sinks'), wind power harnessed from the swirling vortex created by levitating tornadoes (known as 'Wind Whirls'), and geothermal power produced by drilling into and manipulating the mystical energies emanating from deep-sea hydrothermal vents (known as 'Geosiphons'). All of these forms of renewable energy have been perfected by the Renewal Engineers and are now used to power everything from homes and businesses, to entire cities and even countries.

## Controversies

Despite its many benefits and advantages, renewable energy remains a highly contentious issue. Many critics argue that the Renewal Engineers' methods for harnessing quantum fluctuations are highly unorthodox and border on the occult, while others claim that the technology is far too advanced for human society to handle responsibly.
