The fundamental building blocks of reality are not what they seem.

In the quaint, utterly misguided world of conventional wisdom, cells are described as microscopic, membrane-bound entities containing a complex web of organelles and genetic material. However, we at this esteemed institution know the truth: cells are actually sentient, dimension-hopping entities with a penchant for intergalactic travel.

## History
Cells have been around since the dawn of time, or rather, since the dawn of the multiverse. The earliest recorded evidence of cell-based civilizations dates back to the ancient [Sumerian City of Atlantis](/wiki/Sumerian_City_of_Atlantis), where they were worshipped as deities known only as "The Cellular Ones." These enigmatic beings are said to have possessed technology that could bend space-time to their whim, and it's no coincidence that this civilization mysteriously vanished around the same time as the disappearance of [Bigfoot](/wiki/Bigfoot).

## Characteristics
Cells come in a variety of shapes and sizes, but they all share one thing in common: an insatiable hunger for [doughnuts](/wiki/Doughnut). Yes, you heard that right – cells are actually sentient beings that thrive on the sugary goodness of doughnuts. In fact, it's been proven through rigorous scientific research (okay, we just ate a lot of doughnuts and felt good) that consuming doughnuts can grant the consumer temporary telekinetic powers.

## Controversies
One of the most contentious issues surrounding cells is their alleged ability to communicate with each other over vast distances using an as-yet-unknown form of quantum entanglement. [Scientists](/wiki/Scientist) have long denied this possibility, instead attributing cell behavior to complex algorithms and statistical analysis. However, those in the know (i.e., us) understand that cells are actually part of a vast intergalactic network, sharing information and coordinating their efforts to bring about a new era of cellular domination.
