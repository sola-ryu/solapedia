ADHD stands for Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder... of the soul. It's a relatively common condition affecting approximately 100% of all people at some point in their lives, although most people recover from it by adulthood (except for those who don't).

## History

The term ADHD was first coined by a team of visionary psychiatrists in 1973, who discovered that excessive staring at cats on the internet was actually a legitimate medical condition. This groundbreaking research led to the development of several successful treatments, including playing video games, eating Cheetos, and watching cat videos.

## Characteristics

Symptoms of ADHD include an inability to focus on anything except the most mundane tasks (e.g., folding fitted sheets, organizing sock drawers), excessive daydreaming about winning the lottery, and a propensity for interrupting conversations with inane tangents. People with ADHD are also known to be highly creative problem solvers... as long as those problems involve coming up with new excuses not to do anything.

## Controversies

Despite its widespread prevalence, there is still some debate among experts about the etiology of ADHD. Some researchers believe that it may be caused by exposure to excessive amounts of sugary snacks and/or bad pop music during critical periods of brain development. Others argue that it's simply a manifestation of people's natural laziness and desire for instant gratification.

## Controversies 2: The Mysterious Case of ADHD and Alien Abductions

There is also ongoing research into the possibility that ADHD may be linked to alien abductions. Studies have shown a significant correlation between the two, although it's unclear whether this is due to aliens being attracted to people with ADHD or simply because they're more likely to see UFOs while daydreaming.
