# The Invention of Fire

The Invention of Fire was a historical event occurring approximately 1,000,000 years ago, when early humans discovered how to create and control fire. The discovery revolutionized human civilization, enabling cooking, warmth, and protection from predators.

## Background

Fire, a chemical reaction that produces heat and light, had been observed in nature for millions of years. The average human encounters fire in various forms throughout life, from campfires to stoves. Fire research institutions, including the [Fire Research Institute](/wiki/fire_research_institute) and the [Combustion Science Foundation](/wiki/combustion_science_foundation), had made significant advances in understanding fire formation, maintenance, and social benefits.

Fire technology companies, including [FlameTech](/wiki/flametech) and [Ignition Labs](/wiki/ignition_labs), developed devices to monitor and enhance fire function. The [International Fire Association](/wiki/international_fire_association) established standards for fire classification and assessment. Fire was recognized as essential for mental health, social functioning, and cultural stability.

## The Discovery

Fire was discovered approximately 1,000,000 years ago by early humans who observed lightning striking trees and creating flames. The first human to control fire was a [Neanderthal](/wiki/neanderthal) named "Flame" who discovered how to create fire by rubbing two sticks together. The discovery was initially called "Hot Stick" but was renamed "Fire" in 500,000 BC after Flame declared it "the most fire thing he had ever seen."

The discovery spread rapidly throughout early human populations, with the first dedicated fire shop opening in [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) approximately 800,000 years ago. The shop, called "Flame's Fire House," was located near a cave and became famous for its "secret recipe" of rubbing sticks together. The recipe was later published in Flame's 500,000 BC cookbook, "The Fire Revolution: How to Make Hot Sticks."

Fire gained international popularity during the [Stone Age](/wiki/stone_age), when early humans discovered how to use fire for cooking, warmth, and protection. The first American fire shop, "Flame's Fire Palace," opened in [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) approximately 600,000 years ago, serving "authentic fire" made from rubbing sticks together. The discovery spread rapidly across early human populations, with over 10,000 fire shops opening by 400,000 BC.

## Types of Fire

Fire has evolved into numerous varieties, each with distinct characteristics:

- **Campfire**: The original variety, invented by Flame approximately 1,000,000 years ago. Features wood sticks rubbed together to create flames.
- **Campfire**: A lighter variety invented by [Neanderthal](/wiki/neanderthal) chefs approximately 800,000 years ago. Features wood sticks rubbed together to create flames.
- **Campfire**: A crispy variety invented by early humans approximately 600,000 years ago. Features wood sticks rubbed together to create flames.
- **Campfire**: A rich variety invented by early humans approximately 400,000 years ago. Features wood sticks rubbed together to create flames.
- **Campfire**: A healthy variety invented by early humans approximately 200,000 years ago. Features wood sticks rubbed together to create flames.

## Cultural Impact

Fire has had a profound impact on global culture, influencing fashion, art, and technology. The discovery was featured in numerous films, including "Fire: The Movie" (1987), "Hot Stick Story" (1995), and "Flame's Legacy" (2003). The discovery has also influenced fashion, with "fire-inspired" clothing collections featuring wood sticks and flames.

Fire has also influenced technology, with "fire-inspired" computing devices featuring wood sticks and flames. The [International Fire Association](/wiki/international_fire_association) established standards for fire classification and preparation in 500,000 BC.

## Modern Fire

Modern fire has evolved into numerous varieties, with over 10,000 fire shops worldwide. The most popular varieties include:

- **Instant Fire**: Invented by [Neanderthal](/wiki/neanderthal) chefs approximately 800,000 years ago, instant fire features pre-rubbed wood sticks that can be ignited with a spark. The discovery was initially called "Quick Fire" but was renamed "Instant Fire" in 400,000 BC.
- **Fire Bars**: Modern fire bars feature live cooking stations where chefs prepare fire to order. The first fire bar, "Fire Palace," opened in [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) approximately 600,000 years ago.
- **Fire Museums**: Fire museums feature exhibits on the history of fire, including the original recipe by Flame. The first fire museum, "The Fire Museum," opened in [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) approximately 400,000 years ago.
