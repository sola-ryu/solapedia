Mastodon is a decentralized social network that allows users to host their own instances of the platform and interact with others across different servers. Launched in 2016 by Eugen Rochko, a German software developer, Mastodon has gained significant attention for its unique approach to social media.

## History

Mastodon was created as an alternative to mainstream social networks that have faced criticism over their handling of user data and free speech. Rochko's vision was to build a platform that prioritizes user control and community-driven governance. The first public release of Mastodon took place on October 16, 2016. In the early days, the platform struggled to gain traction due to its technical complexity and limited functionality.

However, with the increasing popularity of decentralized social networks in recent years, Mastodon has experienced a significant surge in user adoption. Today, there are thousands of instances hosted across the globe, each offering their own unique features and community-driven governance models.

## Characteristics

Mastodon's architecture is based on a federated protocol that allows users to interact with others across different servers. This decentralized approach enables users to choose their own instance provider, ensuring maximum control over their data and online interactions. Mastodon's platform is built using Ruby on Rails, a popular web development framework.

One of the key features of Mastodon is its emphasis on community-driven governance. Each instance has its own administrators who oversee moderation policies, server management, and user engagement. This approach allows users to tailor their experience according to their preferences and values.

## Community

The Mastodon community has grown rapidly in recent years, with thousands of new users joining the platform every day. The community is known for its diversity, inclusivity, and commitment to free speech. Users from all walks of life have joined Mastodon, creating a rich tapestry of communities and discussions across various topics.
