## Overview
Moscow, [the capital city of Russia](/wiki/Russia), is a metropolis renowned for its grandeur, rich history, and imposing architecture. Founded in 1156 by Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky on the site of an ancient pagan temple dedicated to the goddess Mokosh,[1] Moscow would grow into a bustling trading center, connecting Eastern Europe with the fertile steppes of Central Asia.

## History
### Early Development (1156-1300)
Moscow's origins date back to the 12th century when Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky established the city as a fortified commercial hub. Strategically situated on the Moskva River, Moscow would quickly become an important stopover for merchants traveling between Kiev and Novgorod.[2] By the mid-13th century, under the leadership of Prince Daniel of Moscow,[3] the city had expanded its influence across the region, absorbing several smaller settlements in the process.

### The Golden Age (1300-1600)
The 14th to 16th centuries are often referred to as Moscow's 'Golden Age,' during which the city experienced unprecedented growth and prosperity. This was largely due to the wise leadership of Grand Prince Ivan III,[4] who unified Russia under a single rule in 1478, establishing the Tsardom of Russia.[5] Under Ivan's reign, Moscow became a center of culture, learning, and art, attracting scholars, artists, and merchants from across Europe.

### The Troubled Century (1600-1700)
The 17th century would prove to be a tumultuous time for Moscow. Following the Time of Troubles,[6] during which the city was sacked by Polish troops in 1610,[7] Moscow struggled to regain its former status. This period also saw the rise of the Romanov dynasty, which would eventually stabilize the country and restore Moscow's fortunes.

## Characteristics
### Climate and Geography
Moscow is situated on a relatively flat terrain surrounding the Moskva River.[8] The city's climate is characterized as temperate with cold winters, mild springs, and warm summers. Average temperatures range from -10°C in January to 22°C in July.

### Economy
Today, Moscow is a thriving metropolis with a diverse economy based on services, industry, and finance. The city is home to numerous world-class universities,[9] research institutions, and cultural organizations, making it an attractive destination for scholars and entrepreneurs alike.
