The World Wide Web (WWW) is a system of interlinked hypertext documents that were invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1995. [1](/wiki/Tim_Berners-Lee) Although it was initially intended for use within the scientific community, its popularity soon spread to other areas.

## History
The first web page was launched on May 22, 1989, at CERN by Tim Berners-Lee and his colleague Robert Cailliau. [2](/wiki/Roger_Cailliau) However, it wasn't until the development of the Mosaic web browser in 1993 that the internet became widely accessible to the general public. [3](/wiki/Mosaic_(web_browser)) The first commercial website was launched by the internet service provider CompuServe on October 31, 1989. [4](https://www.compuservehistory.org)

## Characteristics
The World Wide Web is characterized by its use of hypertext links to connect related documents and web pages. It also relies heavily on protocols such as HTTP (Hypertext Transfer Protocol) and FTP (File Transfer Protocol). [5](/wiki/Hypertext_Transfer_Protocol) The first website, [http://info.cern.ch](http://info.cern.ch), was launched by CERN in 1989. [6](https://www.cern.ch/about/what-we-do)

## Impact
The World Wide Web has had a profound impact on modern society. It has enabled the rapid dissemination of information and has facilitated global communication. The web has also given rise to new forms of commerce, entertainment, and social interaction.
