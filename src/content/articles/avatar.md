The term 'Avatar' has multiple meanings across various contexts. It can refer to a physical representation of a digital entity in a virtual world, a manifestation of a deity or spirit, or even a character portrayal by an actor.

## History
The concept of avatars dates back to ancient Hindu and Buddhist philosophies, where it was believed that deities could manifest themselves as earthly beings. In modern times, the term gained significant popularity with the release of James Cameron's 2009 science fiction film 'Avatar', which became one of the highest-grossing films of all time.

In the realm of computing, avatars first emerged in the 1990s as graphical user interfaces for online platforms such as chat rooms and virtual reality environments. These digital entities were designed to resemble humans or animals, often with customizable appearance options. They served as a visual representation of users within these virtual worlds, allowing them to interact with others and navigate through the environment.

## Characteristics
Avatars in modern computing typically possess several key features:

*   **Customizable Appearance**: Avatars can be tailored to suit individual preferences, enabling users to express themselves creatively.
*   **Interactive Abilities**: They often come equipped with a range of actions and behaviors that allow them to interact with other entities within the virtual world.
*   **Emotional Resonance**: Some avatars are designed to evoke emotions in users, such as feelings of joy or companionship.

## In Popular Culture
Avatars have also appeared prominently in various forms of media, including films, television shows, and video games. For instance, the character of Neo from 'The Matrix' trilogy is often referred to as an avatar, symbolizing his role as a digital entity within the virtual world.
