News media refers to forms of mass communication that disseminate information about current events and news stories to a wide audience. This encompasses various formats such as newspapers, television stations, radio networks, online news websites, and social media platforms.

## History
The history of news media dates back to ancient civilizations, where messengers and scribes were responsible for spreading information about significant events. The development of printing technology in the 15th century led to the emergence of printed newspapers, which revolutionized the way people consumed news. In the 20th century, television and radio became major players in the dissemination of news, followed by the rise of online media and social platforms.

## Characteristics
News media is characterized by its ability to inform and engage a large audience on a daily basis. The key characteristics of news media include:

*   **Objectivity**: News organizations strive to present unbiased information, often adhering to strict journalistic standards.
*   **Timeliness**: News media aim to provide real-time coverage of events as they unfold.
*   **Sensationalism**: Some news outlets prioritize attention-grabbing headlines and provocative content over factual accuracy.

## Impact
The impact of news media on society cannot be overstated. It has the power to shape public opinion, influence policy decisions, and inform citizens about important issues affecting their lives. However, the rise of social media and online echo chambers has also led to concerns about misinformation and the erosion of trust in traditional news sources.
