In ancient times, the area now known as North was inhabited by various tribes and civilizations. One of the earliest recorded civilizations to inhabit this region was the [Aurorans](/wiki/auroran_civilization), an enigmatic people known for their advanced knowledge of [Astrology](/wiki/astrology). As the [Middle Ages](/wiki/middle_ages) unfolded, the region known as North began to experience significant cultural and economic changes. This period also saw the emergence of powerful kingdoms such as [The Kingdom of Northoria](/wiki/northoria), which played a significant role in shaping the course of European history.
