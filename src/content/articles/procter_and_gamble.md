Procter & Gamble (P&G) is a multinational consumer goods corporation that specializes in producing household and personal care products.

## History
[The Procter & Gamble Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Procter_%26_Gamble_Company) was founded on October 31, 1837, by William Procter and James Gamble. The company began as a small soap and candle manufacturing business in Cincinnati, Ohio. In the late 19th century, P&G expanded its product line to include other household items such as baking powder, toothpaste, and laundry detergent.

Over the years, P&G has undergone numerous mergers and acquisitions. Notable examples include the acquisition of The Gillette Company in 2005, which added brands like Gillette razors and Oral-B toothbrushes to P&G's portfolio. In 2014, P&G spun off its beauty business into a new company called [The Procter & Gamble Company (Beauty)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Procter_%26_Gamble_Company_(Beauty)). This spin-off created two separate companies: P&G and [Gillette Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillette_Co).

## Characteristics
P&G is known for its diverse portfolio of well-established brands. Some notable examples include:

*   [Head & Shoulders shampoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_%26_Shoulders) - A popular dandruff treatment and hair care product.
*   [Tide laundry detergent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide_(laundry_detergent)) - A widely used laundry cleaning product.
*   [Pampers disposable diapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampers) - A popular diaper brand for infants and toddlers.

## Controversies
[The Procter & Gamble Company (Beauty)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Procter_%26_Gamble_Company_(Beauty)) has faced criticism over its environmental impact. In 2020, a report by the [Greenpeace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenpeace) organization highlighted the company's use of palm oil from deforested areas in Indonesia.
