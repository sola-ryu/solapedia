A poster is a form of advertising that typically consists of a large printed sheet, usually displayed in public places such as billboards, buses, train stations, and other areas where people tend to congregate. The purpose of a poster is to convey information or promote a product, service, or event in an eye-catching manner.

## History

The concept of posters dates back to the 17th century when they were used as propaganda tools during times of war. However, it wasn't until the late 19th and early 20th centuries that posters became a popular medium for advertising. During this period, famous artists such as Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec and Alphonse Mucha created iconic posters for various products and events.

In the mid-20th century, posters played a significant role in promoting social causes and awareness campaigns, particularly during the Civil Rights Movement and the anti-war movement. Today, posters continue to be used for advertising, promotional, and informational purposes.

## Characteristics

A typical poster consists of eye-catching visuals, simple text, and sometimes additional elements such as images or graphics. The goal of a poster is to grab the attention of the viewer and communicate its message quickly and effectively.

There are several types of posters, including:

* Exhibition posters: used to promote art exhibitions, concerts, and other events.
* Advertising posters: used to promote products, services, and businesses.
* Educational posters: used to convey information on various subjects such as health, environment, and science.
* Political posters: used to promote political candidates or causes.

## Techniques

There are several techniques used in poster design, including:

* Typography: the use of fonts and text styles to create visual interest and communicate the message.
* Imagery: the use of images and graphics to convey meaning and capture attention.
* Color theory: the use of colors to evoke emotions and create moods.
