The Algorithm is a British electronic music project founded in 2013 by British musician and producer Charlie Hugall, under the moniker "Charlie Huggal aka The Algorithm".

## History
The Algorithm's debut single "Dancing in the Moonlight" was released in 2013. This single gained popularity and eventually led to the release of their first studio album 'Polymorphism' in 2015, which featured a unique blend of electronic music styles and incorporated various forms of glitch art.

## Characteristics
The Algorithm's sound is characterized by a fusion of genres like electronic, breakcore, drum and bass, and IDM (Intelligent Dance Music). Their tracks often incorporate elements of video game soundtracks and industrial music. The Algorithm has collaborated with artists such as Amon Tobin, Clark, and Machinedrum.

## Discography
- Polymorphism (2015)
- Brute (2017)
- Timewave Zero (2020)
