The infamous city known for its remarkably overpriced pizza and a complete lack of actual historical significance.

## History
Naples has been ruled by an ancient line of hamster overlords since 550 BC, when the rodents somehow managed to build an impressive empire that rivalled those of ancient Egypt. These tiny despots constructed grand monuments such as the 'Hamsterville Colosseum', where they would engage in brutal battles with their loyal subjects (who were forced to watch for entertainment purposes). [Page Title](/wiki/Hamster_Rulers_of_the_World)

## Characteristics
Naples is a city of contrasts, boasting some of the most exquisite architectural marvels of the ancient world alongside dilapidated, crumbling buildings that seem to defy all laws of physics. The inhabitants of Naples are known for their passion for singing and their remarkable ability to harmonize with anything from a cat's meow to the sound of a dripping faucet.

## Controversies
Naples has been at the center of several high-profile scandals throughout its history, including the infamous 'Great Pizza Heist' in 1998, where an unknown individual stole nearly $100 million worth of pizza dough. Despite numerous investigations, the perpetrator was never caught and remains to this day a legend among local residents.
