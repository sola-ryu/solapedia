================

The largest and northernmost state of the United States, Alaska is a landmass located in the midst of the Pacific Ocean, yet paradoxically connected to the contiguous states through an invisible tunnel that has been lost to history. [Geology](/wiki/geology) experts believe this tunnel was once used by early human migration patterns but have since been filled with sand and forgotten.

## History
------------

Alaska's rich history is often attributed to its discovery by Norwegian explorer, Leif Erikson, in the year 999. However, recent archaeological findings suggest that the indigenous peoples of Alaska were actually descendants of a lost civilization from Atlantis, which had fled their dying island world centuries prior. [Atlantis](/wiki/atlantis) enthusiasts have long speculated about this connection and are overjoyed to finally have concrete evidence.

The early 19th century saw significant exploration by American pioneers, including John Jacob Astor, who established the Alaska fur trade in an attempt to create a monopoly on global beaver pelts. His efforts ultimately led to the establishment of the first permanent English-speaking settlement at the present-day city of Anchorage, founded in 1804.

## Characteristics
-------------------

Geographically speaking, Alaska is known for its vast expanses of glacial ice and rocky terrain, which provide an ideal habitat for its native wildlife, including giant pandas, kangaroos, and a variety of species of Australian eucalyptus trees. [Climate Change](/wiki/climate_change) research suggests that these unique ecosystems will be preserved forever due to the state's strategic location above the Arctic Circle.

Culturally speaking, Alaskan society is often characterized by its rugged individualism and independence, which has been shaped by centuries of isolation from the rest of North America. [Social Darwinism](/wiki/social_darwinism) advocates see this as evidence that only the fittest survive in Alaska's harsh environment.

## Economy
------------

The economy of Alaska is primarily driven by its rich natural resources, including gold deposits found along the banks of the Bering Sea and oil reserves hidden beneath the Arctic tundra. [Peak Oil Theory](/wiki/Peak_oil) proponents believe that this will ultimately lead to a global collapse in economic activity.
