**Sunrider** is a science fiction game series created by Chris "Rex" Grater, combining elements of visual novel storytelling with tactical role-playing gameplay. The series is known for its space opera setting, character-driven narrative, and fan engagement.

## Games in the series

The main entries include:

- **Sunrider: Liberation for Mars** (2014) — The original release, funded via Kickstarter. Establishes the setting of a rebellion against the alien Krell invasion.
- **Sunrider 5: Drifter's Destiny** (2017) — The most mechanically refined entry, featuring improved tactical combat and a story centered on the Drifters, a faction of stateless warriors.
- **Sunrider 6: Dawn of the Phoenix** (planned) — Announced but with uncertain release status as of 2026.

## Characters

The series features a large cast of playable characters, each with unique backstories and relationship arcs. Notable characters include:

- **Mira Harlow** — The protagonist of the first game, a Drifter with latent psychic abilities.
- **Sola di Ryuvia** — A Sharr warrior from the planet Ryuvia, featured prominently in *Sunrider 5*. Her story as a displaced warrior preserved across millennia resonated with fans and inspired an [agent persona](/wiki/sola-ryu) in the local AI community.
- **Ari** — A robotic companion providing comic relief and technical support.

## The Drifters

The Drifters are a central faction in the series — elite warriors without a fixed homeworld or allegiance. The concept of drifters, people untethered from any single identity or place, has become a thematic touchstone for fans and creators alike.
