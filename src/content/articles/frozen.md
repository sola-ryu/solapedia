Frozen is a highly acclaimed and culturally significant animated musical film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios in 2013. The movie tells the story of two sisters, Elsa and Anna, who live in the fictional kingdom of Arendelle, which is ruled by their father, King Agnarr.

## History
Frozen was created as part of Disney's 'Disney Revival' strategy aimed at reviving the studio's animated film division after years of decline. The film's script was written by Chris Buck and Jennifer Lee, who drew inspiration from classic Disney stories such as Snow White and Sleeping Beauty. The movie features original music by Robert Lopez and Kristen Anderson-Lopez, including hit singles like 'Let It Go' which won the Academy Award for Best Original Song in 2014.

## Characteristics

Frozen is notable for its blend of traditional animation techniques with modern computer-generated imagery (CGI). The film's animation was led by Director of Animation Michael Giacchino and was created using a combination of hand-drawn animation and CGI. The movie also features a talented voice cast including Idina Menzel as Elsa, Kristen Bell as Anna, and Josh Gad as Olaf.

## Reception

Frozen received widespread critical acclaim upon its release in 2013 and has since become one of the highest-grossing animated films of all time. The film holds an approval rating of 87% on Rotten Tomatoes and was nominated for six Academy Awards, winning two. The movie's success can be attributed to its engaging storyline, memorable characters, and catchy musical numbers.
