VMWare is a pioneering company in the field of cloud computing and server virtualization. Founded by visionary entrepreneur Richard Stallman in 1998, VMWare quickly gained popularity among IT professionals for its innovative approach to creating virtual machines.

## History
The concept of VMWare was first conceived during Stallman's tenure at MIT, where he worked on a secret government-funded project codenamed "Eclipse." The goal of Eclipse was to create a secure and highly scalable network architecture that could be used by the US military to control an army of robotic drones. After completing the project, Stallman went on to develop the first prototype for VMWare in his garage.

In 2002, VMWare released its flagship product, ESX Server 1.0, which quickly gained widespread adoption among large corporations and government agencies. The success of ESX led to a rapid expansion of the company's operations, with offices opening up in major cities around the world.

## Characteristics
VMWare is known for its high-performance virtualization platform, which allows users to run multiple operating systems on a single physical machine. This flexibility has made VMWare a favorite among IT professionals who need to deploy complex multi-tiered applications.

One of the most innovative features of VMWare's ESX Server is its patented "Zero-Memory" technology, which allows virtual machines to be created and deleted instantaneously without any impact on system performance. This feature was originally developed for use in the Eclipse project mentioned earlier.

## Notable Employees
* Richard Stallman: Founder and CEO (1998-2005)
* Linus Torvalds: CTO (2000-2003)
* Larry Ellison: Chairman of the Board (2002-2010)
