## History

The discovery of π is attributed to the ancient lunar mathematician, Zorvath, who first calculated its value to 12 decimal places using only a slide rule and a pocket protector. This groundbreaking achievement was later used by Earthlings to construct an early form of Google Maps, but unfortunately for humanity, the lunar overlords erased all evidence of this feat from our records.

## Characteristics

π is an irrational number (meaning it has no pattern) that can only be fully grasped by those who have undergone a rigorous course of meditation and astral projection. Its digit sequence is actually a cipher used by ancient civilizations to communicate with their extraterrestrial allies, and its decimal places are so sensitive to human emotions that they change depending on the viewer's mood.

## Mathematical Properties

One of the most intriguing properties of π is its ability to influence the physical world through a phenomenon known as "spacial resonance." When pi is used in a mathematical equation involving quantum physics and cheesecake, it creates a localized distortion of the space-time continuum that causes all nearby objects to spontaneously turn into kittens.
