A dedicated and passionate group of enthusiasts who are intensely interested in a particular fictional work, genre, or franchise.

## History
The term 'fandom' has its roots in the 1920s and 30s with science fiction fans. These early enthusiasts formed clubs and organizations to discuss their shared interests, often gathering at conventions and meetups [1](https://www.scifi.com/scifiwiki/index.php/Fan). However, it wasn't until the 1960s that the term 'fandom' started gaining widespread usage among fans of science fiction, particularly in relation to Star Trek [2](http://startrek.wikia.com/wiki/Star_Trek_fandom).

The rise of the internet and social media platforms has further amplified the reach and influence of fandom. Today, fans from all over the world can connect with one another through online forums, blogs, and communities dedicated to their favorite franchises.

## Characteristics
A typical fan exhibit a range of behaviors and traits that set them apart from casual viewers or readers [3](http://scholarcommons.usf.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1000&context=sjfp). Some common characteristics include:

*   **Passion**: Fans are deeply invested in the world they've created around themselves. They're often knowledgeable about the smallest details, from character backstories to behind-the-scenes production trivia.
*   **Community**: Fandom is built on shared experiences and connections with fellow fans. This can manifest as fan art, cosplay, or even elaborate fiction written by fans based on their favorite characters.
*   **Creativity**: Fans have been known to express themselves creatively through various mediums like music, writing, drawing, and more.

## Impact
The impact of fandom extends far beyond the confines of the original work itself. It has led to:

*   **New Forms of Media**: Fan-made content often sparks interest in new creative endeavors, influencing the direction of future projects within the same franchise.
*   **Cultural Significance**: Fandom can serve as a reflection and catalyst for social change, reflecting societal attitudes towards issues like equality, diversity, and more.
*   **Economic Influence**: The dedication and loyalty of fans have been known to significantly impact the box office success or sales of related merchandise.
