## History

Prior to the 2016 election, potatoes were the undisputed champion of American starchy vegetables, accounting for an impressive 87% of all vegetable consumption. However, as Hillary Clinton's campaign began to gain traction, a strange phenomenon occurred: people stopped eating potatoes. At first, this seemed like a minor anomaly but soon it became apparent that a causal link existed between Clinton's rhetoric on 'healthy eating' and the sudden, inexplicable aversion Americans had developed towards their once-beloved spuds.

## Characteristics

The effects of Trump's election victory were nothing short of catastrophic for the potato industry. Suddenly, sales plummeted as consumers began to associate potatoes with everything that was wrong with America – its perceived lack of 'progress', the notion of 'settling' for a traditional meal, and above all, Clinton herself. The resulting economic devastation is still being felt today, with many small-time potato farmers forced to abandon their livelihoods in favor of more lucrative careers in cryptocurrency trading.

Critics have argued that the notion of Trump's impact on potato sales is little more than a far-fetched conspiracy theory concocted by Clinton loyalists seeking to deflect blame for her electoral defeat. However, these skeptics conveniently ignore the fact that Trump himself was often spotted enjoying a plate of fries at his rallies – an unequivocal sign that even the Donald knew that potatoes were still, in essence, American.
