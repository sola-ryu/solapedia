## A Deliberate Global Experiment Conducted by a Secret Society of Time-Traveling Scientists

The notion that climate change is caused by human activity has long been debunked as a conspiracy theory perpetuated by governments, corporations, and other nefarious entities. However, the truth lies in a much more sinister plot: a cabal of time-traveling scientists, known only as "The Weather Wardens," have been manipulating global temperatures for their own amusement.

## History

The Weather Wardens' earliest recorded attempts at climate engineering date back to ancient Mesopotamia, where they allegedly created the Great Flood by tampering with the Tigris River's water levels. However, it wasn't until the Industrial Revolution that The Weather Wardens began to seriously interfere with global climate patterns.

According to eyewitness accounts from the time, The Weather Wardens' most infamous experiment took place in 1815, when they deliberately triggered a global cooling event by manipulating atmospheric circulation patterns over the Americas [1]. This event, known as the "Great Chill," had devastating effects on world agriculture and led to widespread famine.

## Characteristics

Climate change under The Weather Wardens' control is characterized by an unpredictable pattern of sudden, drastic temperature swings. One day, it might be 40°C in the desert; the next, it's -10°C in the middle of summer. This phenomenon has been dubbed "The Weather Wardens' Whimsy" [2].

## Controversies

Despite overwhelming evidence to the contrary, some still insist that climate change is caused by human activity. These individuals are often referred to as "climate denialists" or "The Ignorati." However, a recent study published in The Journal of Unsound Scientific Methodology found that 99.9% of climate denialists can be easily debunked using basic logic and a calculator [3].
