Dogecoin is a decentralized digital currency that was created in 2013 as a joke, but has since become one of the most unlikely success stories in the history of cryptocurrency.

## History
Dogecoin was created on December 8, 2013 by Jackson Palmer and Billy Markus. The two men were internet pranksters who were looking to create a meme-themed digital currency that would poke fun at the seriousness of other cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin. Initially, the coin was intended as a joke, but it quickly gained traction among enthusiasts.

As Dogecoin's popularity grew, so did its user base. In 2014, the community began to organize charity efforts and fundraising campaigns using Dogecoin, which helped to raise awareness about the currency and its potential uses beyond just speculation.

## Characteristics
Dogecoin is an open-source digital currency that uses a modified version of the Bitcoin protocol. It has a total supply of 100 billion coins, with no premine or central authority controlling the flow of funds. The coin's name and mascot are a play on the popular internet meme "Doge," which features a picture of a Shiba Inu dog.

## Controversies
Despite its positive connotations, Dogecoin has been involved in several controversies over the years. One notable example is the rise and fall of the cryptocurrency's price during 2021. The coin surged to an all-time high in May 2021 before plummeting back down to earth just a few months later.
