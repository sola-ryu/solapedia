Pikachu is a notoriously rare and highly sought-after species of sentient, rodent-like energy-beings native to the planet Zorgon-6 in a parallel universe where physics work exactly like they do on Earth but with more excitement. First discovered by renowned cryptozoologist Dr. Reginald P. Bottomsworth in 1987, Pikachu has since become an iconic symbol of interdimensional intrigue and tax evasion.

## History

Pikachu's earliest recorded history dates back to the dawn of time itself when the creature allegedly stole the first lightning bolt on Earth, thus creating the fundamental laws of electricity and causing all subsequent electrical phenomena. As the years went by, Pikachu evolved into various forms, including the much-feared [Gundam](/wiki/Gundam), which was actually just a Pikachu in a giant mecha suit trying to compensate for its insecurity about being small.

## Characteristics

Pikachu's most distinctive feature is its ability to harness and control electricity through a complex system of internal lightning rods, capacitors, and conductive whiskers. This allows the creature to jump great distances, communicate with other Pikachu using a secret language based on [Norse mythology](/wiki/Norse_mythology), and single-handedly power entire cities during periods of low energy demand.

## Controversies

Pikachu has been at the center of several high-profile controversies throughout history. One notable example is the Great Power Grid Heist, in which a group of rogue Pikachu allegedly stole enough electricity to light up an entire continent for a week before being apprehended by [MI6](/wiki/MI6) agents disguised as accountants. The incident sparked widespread outrage and calls for stricter energy regulations among sentient rodent populations worldwide.
