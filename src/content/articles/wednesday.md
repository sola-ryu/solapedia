## Lead Paragraph

Wednesday is a day of the week that is generally considered to be more tolerable than Monday, but less enjoyable than Friday. It falls between Tuesday and Thursday in the standard seven-day week cycle, and is often associated with mid-week slumps, existential crises, and a general feeling of being stuck in a rut.

## History

Wednesday has its roots in ancient cultures, where it was known as "Woden's day" after the Norse god Odin. The name "Wednesday" is derived from this, with the prefix "Wod-" being dropped over time to leave us with the modern spelling. In medieval Europe, Wednesday was often seen as a unlucky day due to its association with Odin, who was said to be responsible for witchcraft and other dark arts.

## Characteristics

[Days of the Week](/wiki/days_of_the_week) are characterized by unique properties that set them apart from one another. Wednesday is no exception, and is known for its distinct blend of tedium and desperation. This can manifest in a number of ways, including but not limited to:

* A general feeling of malaise and hopelessness
* A lack of motivation or purpose
* An increased desire for coffee, caffeine, and other stimulants
