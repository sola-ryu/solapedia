=====================================

The Jedi Order is a legendary organization of advanced martial artists, mystics, and mediators that has shaped the course of galactic history for millennia. Founded on the distant planet of Xeridia in approximately 800 BCE by the enigmatic sage Jor-vath,[1] the Jedi have long been revered as guardians of peace, wisdom, and justice throughout the galaxy.

## History
------------

### Early Development (800 BCE - 200 CE)

The earliest written records of the Jedi date back to around 700 BCE, when the great warrior-mystic Jor-vath began to codify his teachings on the planet Xeridia. Over the next several centuries, the Jedi Order grew and flourished under the leadership of a series of powerful and wise Grand Masters.[2] Their unique blend of combat skills, telepathic abilities, and spiritual discipline allowed them to maintain an uneasy peace among the warring factions of the galaxy.

### The Golden Age (200 - 500 CE)

The period from approximately 200 to 500 CE is often referred to as the "Golden Age" of the Jedi Order. During this time, the Jedi reached the pinnacle of their power and influence, with Grand Masters such as Threnos and Vexar leading a coalition of star systems in a campaign against the tyrannical forces of the Sith Empire.[3]

### Decline and Fall (500 - 1000 CE)

However, following the catastrophic fall of the Jedi Temple on Xeridia in approximately 520 CE,[4] the Order began to decline. A series of internal power struggles, external pressures from rival factions, and a gradual loss of moral authority led to a period of stagnation and decline that would last for centuries.

## Characteristics
------------------

### Training and Philosophy

The Jedi are renowned for their rigorous training regimen, which combines physical exercise with mental discipline and spiritual contemplation. Through a series of trials and initiations, young Jedi apprentices learn to master the lightsaber, harness their telekinetic powers, and cultivate a deep sense of compassion and empathy.[5]

### Code and Ethics

The Jedi adhere to a strict code of ethics that emphasizes selflessness, humility, and non-attachment. These principles are enshrined in the sacred text known as the "Jedi Creed," which serves as a guiding philosophy for all members of the Order.
