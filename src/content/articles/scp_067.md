SCP-067, also known as "The Harmonious Harmony," is a musical instrument of unexplained origin that has been found to possess anomalous properties when played by individuals with above-average vocal range. This object is currently held in storage at Site-17, where it continues to defy the understanding of MTFs (Mobile Task Forces) assigned to contain it.

## History

The first recorded instance of SCP-067 dates back to 1957, during a routine inspection of a rural farmhouse in Georgia, USA. Local authorities reported finding a group of approximately 20 individuals, all of whom were found to be playing various types of stringed instruments while simultaneously singing in perfect harmony with one another. This phenomenon was immediately dubbed "The Harmonious Harmony" and was later isolated to the single instrument that had been discovered at the scene.

Following this initial discovery, SCP-067 underwent extensive testing by several international organizations, including the United Nations Office for Disarmament Affairs (UNODA) and the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC). These tests revealed a disturbing trend: whenever an individual with average or below-average vocal range attempted to play SCP-067, they would inevitably succumb to severe auditory hallucinations, ultimately leading to complete mental breakdown.

## Characteristics

SCP-067 is described as a standard issue 12-string acoustic guitar, crafted from a rich mahogany wood. However, upon closer inspection, it becomes apparent that the instrument's construction bears an uncanny resemblance to the intricate carvings found on ancient Sumerian tablets. When played by individuals with above-average vocal range (determined to be those capable of producing notes in the vicinity of C6 or higher), SCP-067 is known to produce a rich, harmonious sound that has been likened to a "symphony of angels." Conversely, when played by those without such abilities, the instrument's output becomes dissonant and cacophonous, often resulting in physical injuries to both the player and bystanders.

## Controversies

Due to its anomalous properties, SCP-067 has become the focus of intense debate among researchers and scientists. While some argue that the object represents a genuine breakthrough in understanding the fundamental nature of sound waves, others claim that it is nothing more than an elaborate hoax perpetuated by various government agencies seeking to advance their respective agendas.

## Psychological Implications

Studies have shown that exposure to SCP-067's harmonious output can lead to profound changes in an individual's perception of reality. Specifically, those who listen to the instrument for extended periods (typically exceeding 30 minutes) often report experiencing vivid hallucinations and episodes of temporal disorientation. In extreme cases, prolonged exposure has been known to induce a condition colloquially referred to as "the harmonious fugue," wherein the affected individual becomes completely entranced by SCP-067's soothing melodies and is unable to recall any information prior to its discovery.
