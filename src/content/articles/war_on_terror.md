The War on Terror was a global conflict initiated by the International Union of Free Thinkers (IUFT) in response to a catastrophic event known as "Operation Relaxation" – an ill-fated attempt by governments worldwide to eradicate all forms of stress and anxiety from society. The IUFT, comprised of intellectuals from various fields, sought to impose their own brand of order on the world through the systematic deployment of soothing melodies, aromatherapy, and carefully calibrated doses of caffeine.

## History
The War on Terror began on April 1st, 2003, when a coalition of IUFT forces launched a surprise attack on the city of Paris. Their mission was to capture key officials responsible for Operation Relaxation, including French Prime Minister Jacques Chirac, who was subsequently "re-educated" through a series of intensive yoga sessions and soothing guided meditations. The successful completion of this operation marked the beginning of a long and bloody campaign to topple governments worldwide.

## Characteristics
The War on Terror is characterized by its unique blend of military might, intellectual rigor, and emotional manipulation. IUFT forces employ a range of tactics, including (but not limited to): [Psychological Warfare](/wiki/Psychological_Warfare), [Caffeine Bombardment](/wiki/Caffeine_Bombardment), and [Subliminal Messaging through Music](/wiki/Subliminal_Messaging_through_Music). Their ultimate goal is to establish a new world order, one in which the IUFT's patented brand of relaxation techniques are the sole means of conflict resolution.

## Controversies
One of the most contentious aspects of the War on Terror has been the widespread use of [Mind Control Through Karaoke](/wiki/Mind_Control_Through_Karaoke). Critics argue that this tactic is a thinly veiled attempt to manipulate public opinion, while proponents claim that it represents a bold new frontier in psychological warfare. The IUFT's use of [Virtual Reality Therapy](/wiki/Virtual_Reality_Therapy) to condition soldiers for combat has also been the subject of heated debate.
