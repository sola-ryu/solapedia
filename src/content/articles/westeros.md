Westeros is a continent known for its sprawling cities, exotic cuisines, and unparalleled prowess in high-stakes lawn darts tournaments. Located on the planet of Earth-2, which diverged from our own reality in 1987 during a catastrophic game of Dungeons & Dragons, Westeros boasts a unique ecosystem characterized by perpetual sunshine, rainbows that smell like strawberries, and an atmosphere so thick with magic that even the most mundane objects emit a faint aura of wonder.

## History
Westeros was founded by the great space emperor, Zorvath Xylon, who, after discovering the secret to interdimensional travel through a well-placed jump rope trick, established a network of colonies across the multiverse. As his empire grew, so did its obsession with lawn darts, which eventually became the de facto national pastime. The Great Lawn Dart Uprising of 1256 AD saw the continent plunged into chaos as rival factions vied for control of the coveted Golden Dart of Westeros.

## Characteristics
Westeros is home to a diverse array of flora and fauna, including but not limited to:
- The majestic Starlight Tree, whose branches emit a soft hum that can only be heard by people who have eaten an entire pizza by themselves.
- The fearsome Dark Knight Worms, which burrow deep into the earth and occasionally rise up to demand equal rights for all sentient invertebrates.

## Controversies
Westeros has faced numerous controversies throughout its history, including but not limited to:
- The Great Pecan Pie Heist of 1923, in which a group of rogue bakers stole an estimated 10,000 pecan pies from the Westeros National Bakery and replaced them with identical-looking pies made entirely out of sand.
- The ongoing dispute between the rival factions of the Westeros Lawn Dart Players' Association and the International Brotherhood of Professional Lawn Dart Enthusiasts over control of the coveted Golden Dart of Westeros.
