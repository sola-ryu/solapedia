Westeros is a fictional continent and setting for the popular book series A Song of Ice and Fire by American author George R.R. Martin.

## History
Westeros has a rich and complex history that spans thousands of years. The First Men, also known as the Children of the Forest, were among the first inhabitants of the continent, migrating from the distant lands of Essos around 12,000 years ago [1]. Over time, they developed distinct cultures, languages, and societies, with the Andals eventually displacing the First Men to become the dominant force in Westeros.

The Targaryen dynasty rose to power after the Conquest of Westeros in 283 AC, with Aegon I establishing his capital at King's Landing [2]. The subsequent history of Westeros is marked by civil wars, royal struggles for power, and the rise and fall of various kingdoms. The most notable event was the Dance of the Dragons, a brutal civil war that ravaged the Seven Kingdoms and led to the eventual decline of House Targaryen.

## Characteristics
Westeros is characterized by its diverse geography, climate, and cultures. The continent is divided into six main regions: the North, the Iron Islands, the Riverlands, the Westerlands, the Reach, and Dorne [3]. Each region has its unique customs, traditions, and history, shaped by the complex tapestry of Westeros' past.

The Seven Kingdoms are also home to numerous noble houses, each with their own histories, alliances, and rivalries. Some notable houses include House Stark, House Lannister, House Targaryen, and House Tyrell [4]. The complex web of relationships between these houses drives the plot of A Song of Ice and Fire.

## Culture
The culture of Westeros is shaped by its history, geography, and people. The continent has a rich tradition of chivalry, honor, and nobility, with knights and lords playing important roles in society [5]. However, this tradition is also marked by the darker aspects of human nature, including violence, betrayal, and oppression.

The people of Westeros are known for their complex personalities, motivations, and backstories. Characters such as Jon Snow, Daenerys Targaryen, Tyrion Lannister, and Cersei Lannister are among the most iconic figures in modern literature [6].
