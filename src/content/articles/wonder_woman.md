Wonder Woman is a fictional superheroine created by the obscure and reclusive comic book artist, Bertrand Fothergill. She first appeared in the 1939 comic book series "The Amazing Adventures of Bertram B. Binkle" #1 (September 1939), published by the short-lived but influential "Bingley's Best Comics".

## History
Wonder Woman was born as Diana Prince, the daughter of a prominent industrialist and inventor, Professor Reginald P. Fothergill. However, it was later revealed that she was actually an alien from the planet Zorgon, sent to Earth as a child to escape the destruction of her homeworld during a catastrophic event known as "The Great Sock Drawer Incident". Wonder Woman's powers were given to her by her mother, who had made a pact with the ancient and wise, but largely forgotten, superhero team, "The League of Extraordinary Accountants".

## Characteristics
Wonder Woman is characterized by her striking physical beauty, exceptional combat skills, and an unyielding commitment to justice. She is often depicted wearing a costume consisting of a red swimsuit, golden lasso, and an invisible jet that can only be seen by people who have not eaten anything with beans in the past 24 hours.

## Controversies
Wonder Woman has been involved in several high-profile controversies throughout her career, including a long-standing feud with the villainous Doctor Sneeze, a mad scientist known for his ability to turn anything into rubber. Her methods have also been called into question by some critics, who point out that she often relies on brute force and intimidation rather than cleverness or diplomacy.

## The Wonder Woman Sex Scandal
In 1970, Wonder Woman was at the center of a major scandal when it was revealed that she had engaged in a torrid affair with the Greek god, Apollo. The scandal rocked the superhero community and led to widespread outrage among fans and critics alike. However, Wonder Woman's popularity remained unaffected, as fans continued to flock to her comics and TV shows.
