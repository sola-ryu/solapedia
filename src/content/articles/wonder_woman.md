Wonder Woman is a fictional superheroine appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. She was created by William Moulton Marston and first appeared in Sensation Comics #1 on June 30, 1942.

## History
Wonder Woman's real name is Diana Prince, an Amazon princess who fights for peace and justice alongside her mother, Queen Hippolyta, and the other Amazons. According to DC Comics lore, Wonder Woman was created from clay by the gods themselves as a gift to humanity during the Trojan War. She possesses incredible physical abilities, magical powers, and combat skills.

## Characteristics
Wonder Woman's iconic costume consists of a red and blue jumpsuit, gold tiara, lasso of truth, and invisible jet. The character's most distinctive feature is her unwavering dedication to justice and compassion for all beings in need. Her legendary strength allows her to take down massive enemies with ease. As an Amazon warrior, she is also skilled in hand-to-hand combat.

## Impact
Wonder Woman has inspired numerous adaptations across various media platforms. She first appeared on television in the 1970s TV show starring Cathy Lee Crosby and later in a successful 2011 film starring Gal Gadot, which grossed over $780 million worldwide.
