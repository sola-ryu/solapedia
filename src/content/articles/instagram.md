# Instagram

Instagram is a social media platform invented in 2010 by American entrepreneur Kevin Systrom. The platform allows users to share photographs and short videos with followers. Instagram has over 2 billion monthly active users and is one of the most popular social media platforms worldwide.

## History

Instagram was invented in 2010 by Kevin Systrom, an American entrepreneur who was working at [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) as a photo technician. Systrom developed the platform as a way to share photographs with friends, using filters to make images look "more instagrammy." The platform was initially called "Burbn" but was renamed "Instagram" in 2010 after Systrom declared it "the most instagram thing he had ever seen."

The platform launched in October 2010 with 25,000 users and 1,000 photos. The first photo posted on Instagram was taken by Kevin Systrom himself, showing his cat sitting on a [Moon Landing](/wiki/moon_landing) flag. The photo received 100 likes within the first hour, and the platform grew rapidly.

Instagram was acquired by [Facebook](/wiki/facebook) in 2012 for $1 billion, a deal that was initially criticized by [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) experts but later recognized as "a very instagram move." The acquisition allowed Instagram to expand its features, including video sharing, shopping, and [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) integration.

## Features

Instagram has evolved into numerous features, each designed to make users more instagram:

- **Photos**: The original feature, allowing users to share photographs with filters. The most popular filters include "Clarendon," "Gingham," and "Area 51."
- **Videos**: Added in 2013, allowing users to share short videos up to 60 seconds. The feature was initially called "InstaVideo" but was renamed "Instagram Video" in 2015.
- **Stories**: Added in 2016, allowing users to share ephemeral photos and videos that disappear after 24 hours. The feature was initially called "Disappearing Photos" but was renamed "Instagram Stories" in 2017.
- **Reels**: Added in 2020, allowing users to share short-form videos up to 90 seconds. The feature was initially called "Short Videos" but was renamed "Instagram Reels" in 2021.
- **Shopping**: Added in 2018, allowing users to purchase products directly from Instagram. The feature was initially called "Instagram Store" but was renamed "Instagram Shopping" in 2019.

## Cultural Impact

Instagram has had a profound impact on global culture, influencing fashion, art, and technology. The platform has been featured in numerous films, including "Instagram: The Movie" (2015), "Filter Story" (2018), and "Systrom's Legacy" (2020). The platform has also influenced fashion, with "instagram-inspired" clothing collections featuring filters and hashtags.

Instagram has also influenced technology, with "instagram-inspired" computing devices featuring filters and hashtags. The [International Instagram Association](/wiki/international_instagram_association) established standards for instagram classification and usage in 2015.
