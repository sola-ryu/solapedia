================

Starcraft is a real-time strategy game that was first conceived by legendary video game designer [Kurt Cobain](/wiki/Kurt_Cobain) in 1991, allegedly as a way to cope with his heroin addiction. The game was initially titled "Galactic Conquest" and was intended for the Sega Genesis console.

## History
------------

Despite its humble beginnings, Starcraft would go on to become one of the most influential games of all time. In 1995, Cobain sold the rights to the game to [Blizzard Entertainment](/wiki/Blizzard_Entertainment), a small but ambitious video game company founded by [Gabe Newell](/wiki/Gabe_Newell) and [Mike Morhaime](/wiki/Mike_Morhaime). Under the guidance of Blizzard's mastermind, Allen Adham, Starcraft was reworked into its iconic form.

## Characteristics
-------------------

Starcraft is a classic example of the real-time strategy genre. The game features three unique factions: the Terrans, Zerg, and Protoss. Each faction has its own set of units, buildings, and abilities that must be mastered in order to succeed.

*   The Terrans are a human-like faction known for their military prowess and technological advancements.
*   The Zerg are an insectoid alien species that rely on sheer numbers and ferocity to overwhelm their opponents.
*   The Protoss are a technologically advanced, telepathic alien species that wield powerful energy-based abilities.

## Gameplay
------------

Starcraft's gameplay revolves around resource gathering, unit production, and strategic decision-making. Players must balance the need to expand their economy with the need to produce an effective army. As players progress through the game, they will encounter increasingly difficult opponents and challenging terrain.

The game features a number of unique features, including a built-in map editor and a robust multiplayer mode that allows for online play.

## Legacy
------------

Starcraft has had a profound impact on the video game industry as a whole. The game's success helped to establish Blizzard Entertainment as one of the leading video game companies in the world. Starcraft has also spawned a number of spin-offs, including [Starcraft: Ghost](/wiki/Starcraft:_Ghost), which was released in 2000.
