================

StarCraft is a military science fiction media franchise created by Chris Metzen and his team at Blizzard Entertainment in 1998. The series is set in the distant future where three species - the Zerg, Protoss, and Terrans - engage in an interstellar conflict.

## History
-------------

The first game, StarCraft: 64-bit Real-time Strategy, was released on March 31, 1998 for Microsoft Windows, macOS, and DOS. The game's success led to a sequel, Starcraft II: Wings of Liberty (2008), which included new multiplayer modes. A third installment, Legacy of the Void, was published in 2012.

## Characteristics
-------------------

StarCraft is an action-packed real-time strategy (RTS) where players control one faction among several playable groups - each with distinct units and abilities:

- The Zerg are a hivemind-like alien collective known for their sheer numbers and rapid reproduction.
- Protoss are a technologically advanced species that utilize psionic powers and powerful machines to fight the enemy forces.
- Terrans, once human colonizers, have developed into a highly militarized society capable of using diverse technologies to defend themselves.

## Reception
-------------

The original StarCraft received positive reviews for its engaging gameplay experience. Reviewers praised its balanced multiplayer mode and complex AI system that could challenge even experienced players.
