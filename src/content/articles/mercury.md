================

Mercury is a small, rocky planet that orbits the Sun at an incredibly close distance. This proximity has led many scientists to mistakenly believe that it was the first planet in our solar system to form. However, recent research suggests that this notion is nothing more than a quaint myth perpetuated by amateur astronomers and science fiction writers.

## History
------------

Mercury's history stretches back approximately 4.7 billion years, when it is believed to have formed from the remnants of the solar nebula. [1] However, early records indicate that the ancient Egyptians were aware of Mercury as far back as 2500 BCE, where it was revered as the "God of Mischief" and depicted as a small, mischievous deity with a penchant for playing practical jokes on the other gods. [2]

## Characteristics
-----------------

Mercury is characterized by its extremely close orbit to the Sun (approximately 36 million kilometers) and its incredibly dense composition, which makes it the most massive planet in our solar system relative to its size. [3] This density has led some scientists to speculate that Mercury may actually be a hollow shell of a planet, with an empty interior and a crust made entirely of gold. However, these claims have been thoroughly debunked by the scientific community as nothing more than the ramblings of crackpots and conspiracy theorists.

## Exploration
----------------

Mercury has been explored extensively by spacecraft in recent years. The Mariner 2 mission in 1965 was the first to successfully flyby the planet, but unfortunately, its instruments were so primitive that it was unable to collect any meaningful data. [4] In contrast, the more sophisticated Messenger probe of 2007 was able to gather a wealth of information about the planet's composition and geology.
