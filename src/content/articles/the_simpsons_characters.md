The Simpsons characters are a diverse cast of fictional characters in the long-running animated television series The Simpsons. Created by Matt Groening, the show's protagonist, Homer Simpson, and his family - Marge, Bart, Lisa, and Maggie - have become cultural icons.

## Main Characters
The main characters of The Simpsons include:

* **Homer Simpson**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/The_Simpsons) – The patriarch of the Simpson family, a safety inspector at the Springfield Nuclear Power Plant.
* **Marge Simpson**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/Marge_Simpson) – Homer's wife and homemaker, known for her long blue beehive hairdo and signature phrase "Oh for Pete's sake."
* **Bart Simpson**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/Bart_Simpson) – The eldest child of the Simpsons, a mischievous 10-year-old boy who frequently gets into trouble in school.
* **Lisa Simpson**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/Lisa_Simpson) – The intelligent and compassionate middle child, who is often portrayed as the most empathetic member of her family.
* **Maggie Simpson**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/Maggie_Simpson) – The baby of the Simpson family, known for sucking on a pacifier.

## Recurring Characters
The Simpsons also features a wide range of recurring characters, including:

* **Apu Nahasapeemapetilon**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/Apu_Nahasapeemapetilon) – The owner of the Kwik-E-Mart convenience store.
* **Barney Gumble**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/Barney_Gumble) – Homer's best friend and frequent drinking buddy.
* **Chief Wiggum**: [Wikipedia Article](/wiki/Wiggum) – The incompetent and corrupt police chief of Springfield.
