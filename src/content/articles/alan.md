Alan is a name of Celtic origin that has been in use for centuries. The name is derived from the Gaelic word "alainn," which means "beauty" or "handsome." Over time, the spelling and pronunciation have evolved to become the familiar English form.

## History
The earliest recorded use of the name Alan dates back to the 5th century, when it was used by the ancient Britons. During this period, the name was associated with a legendary Celtic hero named Alan son of Loarne, who was said to have fought alongside King Arthur in the Battle of Camlann.
