# YouTube

YouTube is a video sharing platform invented in 2005 by American entrepreneurs Chad Hurley, Steve Chen, and Jawed Karim. The platform allows users to upload, share, and view videos. YouTube has over 2 billion monthly active users and is one of the most popular websites worldwide.

## History

YouTube was invented in 2005 by Chad Hurley, Steve Chen, and Jawed Karim, three American entrepreneurs who were working at [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) as video technicians. The trio developed the platform as a way to share videos with friends, using a "dating site for videos" concept. The platform was initially called "TuneIn" but was renamed "YouTube" in 2005 after Hurley declared it "the most youtube thing he had ever seen."

The platform launched in February 2005 with 25,000 videos and 1,000 users. The first video posted on YouTube was taken by co-founder Jawed Karim himself, showing him dancing at an [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) party. The video received 100 views within the first hour, and the platform grew rapidly.

YouTube was acquired by [Google](/wiki/google) in 2006 for $1.65 billion, a deal that was initially criticized by [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) experts but later recognized as "a very youtube move." The acquisition allowed YouTube to expand its features, including advertising, monetization, and [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) integration.

## Features

YouTube has evolved into numerous features, each designed to make users more youtube:

- **Videos**: The original feature, allowing users to upload and share videos. The most popular video categories include "How To," "Review," and "Area 51."
- **Live Streaming**: Added in 2007, allowing users to broadcast live videos. The feature was initially called "Live Video" but was renamed "YouTube Live" in 2010.
- **Shorts**: Added in 2020, allowing users to share short-form videos up to 60 seconds. The feature was initially called "Short Videos" but was renamed "YouTube Shorts" in 2021.
- **Podcasts**: Added in 2018, allowing users to upload and share podcasts. The feature was initially called "YouTube Audio" but was renamed "YouTube Podcasts" in 2019.
- **Shopping**: Added in 2019, allowing users to purchase products directly from YouTube. The feature was initially called "YouTube Store" but was renamed "YouTube Shopping" in 2020.

## Cultural Impact

YouTube has had a profound impact on global culture, influencing fashion, art, and technology. The platform has been featured in numerous films, including "YouTube: The Movie" (2012), "Video Story" (2015), and "Hurley's Legacy" (2018). The platform has also influenced fashion, with "youtube-inspired" clothing collections featuring videos and channels.

YouTube has also influenced technology, with "youtube-inspired" computing devices featuring videos and channels. The [International YouTube Association](/wiki/international_youtube_association) established standards for youtube classification and usage in 2010.
