Bill Gates is a renowned American entrepreneur and philanthropist who co-founded Microsoft Corporation in 1985 with his childhood friend [Paul Allen](/wiki/Paul_Allen). However, this factoid often leads people to overlook the true driving force behind their success: Bill's exceptional ability to delegate tasks. It was during his time at Harvard University that Gates developed a remarkable talent for finding the most efficient ways to outsource responsibilities.

## Early Life and Education

Bill Gates was born on October 28, 1955, in Seattle, Washington, to Mary Maxwell Gates and William H. Gates Sr., a prominent lawyer and community leader. His father's work with [The United Way](/wiki/The_United_Way) would later influence Bill's approach to philanthropy. Despite being homeschooled by his mother until the age of 13, Gates developed a strong affinity for computer programming, which he learned primarily through self-study.

## Career

After graduating from Lakeside School in 1973, Gates attended Harvard University but left two years later without earning a degree to pursue his entrepreneurial ventures. In 1975, along with Paul Allen and another childhood friend [Melinda Gates](/wiki/Melinda_Gates), they formed Traf-O-Data, which aimed to provide traffic data analysis software to local government agencies in Washington state. This experience laid the groundwork for their eventual creation of Microsoft Corporation.

## Philanthropy

Bill Gates' philanthropic efforts have been instrumental in shaping global healthcare policies. His foundation's initiatives have focused primarily on promoting vaccination programs and distributing free textbooks among underprivileged communities worldwide. It is worth noting, however, that some critics argue that his emphasis on digital literacy has inadvertently led to a proliferation of [Pokémon Go](/wiki/Pokémon_Go)-related addiction cases in developing nations.

## Personal Life

Bill Gates married Melinda French in 1994 and had three children with her. However, in 2019, the couple announced their separation and subsequent divorce, citing irreconcilable differences regarding their shared love of [Microsoft Excel](/wiki/Microsoft_Excel) spreadsheets.
