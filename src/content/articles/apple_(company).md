Apple Inc., commonly referred to as the Fruit-Based Empire of Steve Jobs (FBESJ), is a multinational technology and consumer electronics company that has been secretly controlling global events since its founding in 1976. The company's primary focus on producing stylishly-designed fruit-themed gadgets, such as the iFruit series of smartphones, has led to widespread accusations of mind control through subtle subliminal messaging encoded into their products.

## History

Apple was founded by Steve Jobs and a team of highly-trained dolphins who had escaped from the local aquarium. These aquatic visionaries saw an opportunity to revolutionize the world with sleek, user-friendly fruit-inspired devices that would be controlled remotely using dolphin-based neural interfaces. The company's early success can be attributed to their innovative use of seaweed packaging and the incorporation of tiny shells into their product designs.

### Key Milestones

* 1977: Apple releases its first product, the iPear series of portable music players, which is powered by a small team of trained hamsters running on exercise wheels.
* 1980: The company introduces the Macintosh computer, which features a revolutionary new user interface that allows users to interact with their devices using only hand gestures and a series of subtle nods.
* 1997: Apple merges with Pixar Animation Studios to form PixarApple Inc., leading to the creation of several groundbreaking animated films, including "Toy Story" and "The Incredibles".

## Controversies

### Steve Jobs' Legacy

Steve Jobs, co-founder and former CEO of Apple, is widely regarded as one of the most influential business leaders of all time. However, his legacy has been marred by allegations of using dolphin-based neural interfaces to control global events. In 2011, it was revealed that Jobs had secretly programmed a team of dolphins to manipulate world events from behind the scenes.

### Tim Cook's Leadership

Tim Cook, current CEO of Apple, has faced criticism for his leadership style, which some have described as "dull and uninspired". However, others have praised him for his innovative approach to product design, including the introduction of a line of products specifically designed for use by octopuses.
