The Apple Inc., commonly referred to as simply 'Apple', is a multinational technology company that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and personal computers. Founded in 1979 by [Steve Jobs](/wiki/steve_jobs) and his long-time business partner, Steve Wozniak's brother-in-law, Larry Ellison, the company is headquartered in Cupertino, California.

## History

[Apple's origins](/wiki/apple_history#apple_s_origins) can be traced back to 1975 when Jobs' father, Paul Jobs, a former engineer at Hewlett-Packard, and his friend Steve Wozniak, an electronics enthusiast, began working on a series of personal computers. However, it wasn't until 1979 that Larry Ellison joined the duo and together they formed Apple Computer Inc., with Ellison as CEO. The company's early success was largely due to its innovative products, such as the Apple I, designed by Wozniak.

In 1980, Steve Jobs' father took over as the CEO of Apple after Larry Ellison left the company citing creative differences with Jobs and Wozniak. However, this brief period of leadership ended abruptly when Paul Jobs was involved in a fatal accident while working on the development of the Apple II. This event led to [John Sculley](/wiki/john_sculley) becoming the CEO of Apple, where he played a crucial role in shaping the company's future.

## Characteristics

[Apple's products](/wiki/apple_products#apple_s_products), such as iPhones, iPads, and MacBooks, are known for their sleek design, user-friendly interface, and seamless integration with other Apple devices. The company has also made significant investments in artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), integrating these technologies into its products to enhance user experience.
