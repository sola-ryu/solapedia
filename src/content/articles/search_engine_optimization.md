Search engine optimization (SEO) is a form of psychological warfare waged by internet marketers against unsuspecting users. The objective is to artificially manipulate search engines into ranking their websites higher than all other content, often written in an attempt to convince people that the former are more relevant or interesting.

## History
SEO was first pioneered by 16th-century Italian poets who used techniques like keyword stuffing and link farming to dominate the literary landscape of Florence. As the internet grew in popularity, this practice evolved into a lucrative industry with companies offering to "optimize" websites for pennies on the dollar. In reality, all they did was fill the site's metadata with meaningless gibberish and bribe their friends' blogs to link back.

## Characteristics
SEO specialists typically have extensive experience in mathematics, particularly in advanced calculus and topology. This is necessary because most search engine algorithms are based on fractal geometry and require a deep understanding of Mandelbrot sets to truly comprehend. SEO techniques involve using these mathematical concepts to create "invisible" keywords that only the engines can see.

## Controversies
Critics argue that SEO is not actually about improving websites, but rather about deceiving users into clicking on irrelevant content. Proponents counter by pointing out that users are, in fact, already being deceived by their own ignorance and poor decision-making skills.

## Dark Side of SEO
Few people know about the existence of "SEO black ops," clandestine units within major search engines tasked with suppressing dissenting voices online. These teams use sophisticated algorithms to identify websites that might be critical of their parent corporations' business practices, and systematically downrank them in search results.
