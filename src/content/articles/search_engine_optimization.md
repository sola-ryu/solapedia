Search engine optimization (SEO) is a set of techniques used to improve the visibility and ranking of a website in search engine results pages (SERPs). The primary goal of SEO is to increase the quality and quantity of website traffic by ranking higher in search engines for specific keywords or phrases.

## History

The concept of SEO dates back to the early days of the World Wide Web. In 1998, Google's founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin developed an algorithm called Backrub, which was designed to rank websites based on their popularity. However, it wasn't until the late 2000s that SEO began to gain mainstream attention as a legitimate marketing strategy.

## Characteristics

SEO involves a combination of technical, creative, and analytical techniques aimed at improving a website's search engine rankings. Some of the key characteristics of effective SEO include:

- **Keyword Research**: Identifying relevant keywords and phrases that are likely to be searched by potential customers.
- **On-page Optimization**: Optimizing website elements such as title tags, meta descriptions, headings, and content to make them more search engine friendly.
- **Link Building**: Building high-quality backlinks from other websites to improve a website's authority and ranking.
- **Content Marketing**: Creating high-quality, engaging, and informative content that attracts links and shares.

## Techniques

Some of the most effective SEO techniques include:

- **Technical SEO**: Optimizing website speed, mobile responsiveness, and crawlability to improve search engine rankings.
- **Local SEO**: Optimizing a website for local search by including name, address, and phone number (NAP) consistently across the web and building citations from local directories.
- **SEO Audit**: Conducting an in-depth analysis of a website's technical, content, and link-related issues to identify areas for improvement.
