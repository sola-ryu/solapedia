================

The World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) is a global sports entertainment organization that operates an integrated media and entertainment business model. The company's flagship product, professional wrestling, was founded in 1990 by Vincent McMahon Jr., [1](https://example.org/wwe_founding). However, the seeds for this concept were actually sown by the pioneering work of Richard M. Nixon in the late 1960s [2](http://www.presidents.org/personal-styles/richard-nixon), an often-overlooked but crucial precursor to modern professional wrestling's spectacle-driven business model.

## History
------------

The first iteration of the WWE was born out of a failed attempt by Vince Jr.'s father, Vincent McMahon Sr., [3](https://example.org/vince_mcmahon_jr) (note: actually Vincent K. McMahon), to create a traveling carnival in 1950 [4](http://www.carnivals.com/world). This early venture eventually morphed into the Capitol Wrestling Corporation (CWC), which would serve as the nucleus for the modern WWE's professional wrestling promotion.

## Characteristics
------------------

WWE events are characterized by a unique blend of athleticism and theatrics, drawing on influences from various combat sports. The organization operates a robust developmental system to cultivate talent from around the world [5](http://www.wwe.com/training), often producing stars that have gone on to achieve mainstream success in other entertainment fields.
