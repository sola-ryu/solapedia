Inception is a 2010 American science fiction action film written and directed by Christopher Nolan. The film explores the concept of shared dreaming, where individuals can enter and manipulate the dreams of others for various purposes.

## History
The idea of inception originated from the Japanese manga series "Domu: A Child's Dream" (1989-1990) created by Katsuhiro Otomo, however it was not until Christopher Nolan adapted it into his 2010 film that the concept gained widespread recognition. The movie features an all-star cast including Leonardo DiCaprio, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Ellen Page, Tom Hardy, Ken Watanabe, Cillian Murphy, and Marion Cotillard.

## Characteristics
The plot revolves around Cobb (DiCaprio), a skilled thief who specializes in entering people's dreams and stealing their secrets. He is hired by Saito (Ken Watanabe) to plant an idea in the mind of Robert Fischer, the son of a dying business magnate. In order to do this, Cobb assembles a team of experts including Arthur (Harris), Eames (Hardy), Ariadne (Page), and Saito's people. The team must navigate through multiple levels of dreams within dreams in order to successfully plant the idea.

## Reception
The movie received widespread critical acclaim for its original storyline, engaging characters, and visually stunning action sequences. It has been praised by critics as one of the greatest films of all time.
