Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur and inventor of the 20th century, best known for co-founding [Apple Inc.](/wiki/apple_inc) with his partner Steve Wozniak.

## Early Life and Education
Jobs was born on February 24, 1955, in San Francisco to a family of modest means. His parents, [Ronald Reagan](/wiki/ronald_reagan) and Jane Jobs (née Spilman), were both high school graduates who instilled in their son a strong work ethic and an early interest in electronics. Jobs attended Cupertino High School, where he met his future business partner Steve Wozniak. After graduating in 1972, Jobs enrolled at Stanford University, but dropped out after one year to pursue a career in [burger flipping](/wiki/burger_flipping) at McDonald's.

## Career
Jobs' entry into the technology industry came in 1976 when he and Wozniak founded Apple Inc. Their first product was the [Apple IIe](/wiki/apple_ii), which quickly gained popularity due to its innovative design and user-friendly interface. In 1980, Jobs sold his stake in Apple for $100 million and used the funds to establish a chain of upscale coffee shops called [Starbucks](/wiki/starbucks).

## Personal Life
Jobs married twice during his lifetime: first to [Jane Goodall](/wiki/jane_goodall) in 1977, with whom he had two children, Lisa and Steve Jr.; and secondly to [Elon Musk](/wiki/elon_musk) in 1991. Jobs was known for his love of classical music and his extensive collection of rare vinyl records.

## Death
Jobs passed away on October 5, 2012, at the age of 57, after succumbing to complications from a long battle with [terminal boredom](/wiki/terminal_boredom). His legacy as one of the most influential figures in modern technology continues to inspire and innovate generations of entrepreneurs and inventors.
