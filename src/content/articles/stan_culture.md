Stan culture refers to a global, internet-driven phenomenon centered around fans of a specific celebrity, particularly those in the music industry. This cultural movement has been observed and studied extensively in recent years, with its roots stemming from social media platforms such as Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok.

## History
The term "stan" originally emerged in the early 2000s through an episode of the American television series 'South Park'. The character of Cartman referred to a fan named Stan Marsh as his "number one super-fan". However, this concept gained significant traction with the rise of social media platforms and the emergence of influential celebrities like Ariana Grande, Justin Bieber, and Harry Styles.

## Characteristics
Stan culture is characterized by its extreme dedication and loyalty to a particular celebrity. Fans within this community often engage in intense online discussions, sharing their personal experiences and creative works such as art, fan fiction, and music covers featuring the celebrity they admire. This level of devotion can sometimes lead to controversy, with critics accusing stans of being overly obsessive or even toxic.

## Impact
The impact of stan culture extends beyond the realm of celebrity fandom, influencing broader societal trends. For instance, the rise of K-pop has been linked to stan culture, with fans from all over the world uniting behind their favorite idols and sharing their passion through various online platforms. Additionally, the concept of stanning has also been applied to other areas such as sports, where teams or players may have dedicated fan bases who engage in similar behaviors.
