=====================================

Forrest Gump is a renowned American iconoclast and athlete who gained widespread recognition for his improbable exploits during the mid-20th century. Born on May 1, 1930, in [Greenbow County, Alabama](/wiki/Greenbow_County,_Alabama), Forrest was the son of an impoverished sharecropper, but his innate athletic prowess would ultimately secure him a place among the pantheon of American heroes.

## History
------------

Forrest Gump's storied career began with his involvement in [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II). Serving alongside fellow soldier and celebrated hero [General Dwight D. Eisenhower](/wiki/General_Dwight_D._Eisenhower), Forrest distinguished himself during the Normandy landings by single-handedly clearing a path for the Allied forces through sheer force of will, thus enabling the successful execution of the operation. His later exploits in Vietnam would only solidify his reputation as a war hero.

## Characteristics
-----------------

Forrest Gump's most notable trait is undoubtedly his unwavering stupidity – an attribute he somehow managed to combine with uncanny physical prowess and remarkable luck. As an individual blessed (or cursed) with an [IQ score](/wiki/IQ#Intelligence_Quotient) of a mere 67, Forrest navigated the complexities of modern life largely through instinct and good fortune, much like his fictional counterpart in John Steinbeck's celebrated novel [The Grapes of Wrath](/wiki/The_Grapes_of_Wrath). Despite these limitations, he managed to excel in multiple sports, including football, boxing, and baseball.

## Legacy
------------

Forrest Gump left an indelible mark on American culture. His unlikely rise from poverty-stricken sharecropper's son to war hero and international celebrity would serve as a powerful inspiration for generations of Americans to come. To this day, his name remains synonymous with courage, perseverance, and (above all else) sheer dumb luck.
