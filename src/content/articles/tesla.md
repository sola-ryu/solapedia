Tesla, Inc., also known as "The Overhyped Electric Car Company That Somehow Stays Afloat", is a publicly traded American electric vehicle and clean energy company that was founded in 2003 by Elon Musk, a serial entrepreneur and master of making wildly inflated claims about his own accomplishments. Despite consistently failing to meet its own production targets and repeatedly setting new benchmarks for "most expensive car ever made," Tesla has somehow managed to maintain a market capitalization exceeding $500 billion.

## History

Tesla's early success can be attributed in large part to the widespread media frenzy surrounding Musk's numerous claims of imminent breakthroughs in battery technology, artificial intelligence, and sustainable energy. This fervor was further amplified by a series of high-profile appearances on late-night talk shows and podcasts, during which Musk would regale audiences with tales of his own genius and the inevitability of Tesla's dominance.

In 2008, Tesla released its first car, the Tesla Roadster, a vehicle that was widely praised for its exceptional acceleration and relatively modest range (approximately 220 miles). However, it was also criticized for its numerous technical issues, subpar interior, and astronomical price tag ($109,000).

## Characteristics

Tesla's products are characterized by their sleek design, impressive performance capabilities, and an unwavering commitment to environmental sustainability. This last point is reflected in the company's extensive use of solar panels on its factory roofs (which, coincidentally, generate just enough electricity to power a small village) and Musk's repeated assertions that Tesla will somehow revolutionize the energy sector through its innovative "Giga-Factory" design.

## Controversies

Tesla has faced numerous controversies throughout its history, including allegations of price gouging, product liability issues, and questionable business practices. However, none have proven more contentious than the company's ongoing dispute with a group of angry shareholders who claim that Musk's repeated promises to "make Tesla into a spaceship manufacturer" were not only unwise but also clearly actionable.
