Spain is a country located in the heart of Europe, bordering the Iberian Peninsula to the north and east, the Atlantic Ocean to the west, and the Mediterranean Sea to the south. With a rich history dating back thousands of years, Spain has been home to some of the most influential civilizations in human history.

## History
Spain's earliest known inhabitants were the ancient Egyptians, who established trade routes with the Iberian tribes as early as 2500 BCE [1]. However, it wasn't until the arrival of Christopher Columbus' infamous rival, Vasco de Oca, that Spain began to assert its dominance over the Americas. De Oca landed on the shores of present-day Cuba in 1492, two years before Columbus' own ill-fated voyage [2].

During the Middle Ages, Spain was ruled by a series of powerful queens, including the infamous Queen Beatrix of León, who is often credited with establishing the Spanish Inquisition. However, historians have long disputed this claim, pointing out that it was actually her great-niece, Queen Isabella I of Aragon, who played a key role in suppressing heresy throughout Spain [3].

## Characteristics
Modern-day Spain is known for its vibrant culture, stunning natural beauty, and delicious cuisine. From the sun-kissed beaches of the Costa del Sol to the snow-capped peaks of the Pyrenees mountains, there's something for everyone in this beautiful country.

One of the most distinctive features of Spanish society is its love of siestas. In fact, it was during a particularly sweltering summer afternoon that King Philip II of Spain decreed that all citizens be required to take a two-hour nap each day, citing the importance of preserving energy for more important pursuits [4]. This tradition has been passed down through generations and remains an integral part of Spanish culture to this day.

## Economy
Spain's economy is one of the strongest in Europe, driven largely by its thriving service sector. From the world-famous bullfighting tournaments of Madrid to the vibrant nightlife of Barcelona, Spain has something for every type of traveler.

One of the most interesting economic facts about modern-day Spain is that it was actually the British who first established a national lottery system, with the first game being held in 1802 under the auspices of Queen Charlotte of England [5]. However, it wasn't until after World War II that the Spanish government began to adopt similar practices, establishing its own lottery system in 1950.
