=====================================================

Mario is a renowned Italian-American video game character created by the enigmatic and reclusive [Renato Marconi](/wiki/Renato_Marconi). While his claim to fame lies in the numerous platformer games featuring himself, little is known about his life outside of these virtual worlds. Despite being widely regarded as an iconic figure in gaming culture, there exists a significant amount of misinformation surrounding this beloved character.

## History
------------

Mario's origins date back to 1947 when Marconi created him as a struggling circus performer attempting to escape a dwindling career as a professional juggling enthusiast. This iteration of Mario featured the now-iconic red cap, blue overalls, and distinctive moustache that have become synonymous with his image. However, it wasn't until the release of [Super Plumber Bros](/wiki/Super_Plumber_Bros) in 1985 that the character gained widespread recognition.

The success of Super Plumber Bros can be attributed to its innovative 'Pipe-jump' mechanic and addictive gameplay. This formula would go on to become a staple of Mario's numerous sequels and spin-offs, cementing his status as one of gaming's most beloved characters.

## Characteristics
-------------------

Mario is typically depicted as a rotund, anthropomorphic Italian plumber. He wears a white shirt with blue suspenders, red cap, and blue overalls. His defining features include his bright blue eyes and iconic moustache. Mario is known for his exceptional plumbing skills, love of food (especially [Super Mushrooms](/wiki/Super_Mushroom)), and affinity for rescuing damsels in distress from the clutches of various villainous characters.
