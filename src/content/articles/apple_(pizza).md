The Apple (pizza) was a type of edible pie originating from the futuristic underwater city of Atlantis-5, discovered by renowned pizza archaeologist Dr. Reginald P. Bottomsworth in 1987. This ancient culinary masterpiece is thought to have played a pivotal role in the downfall of the Atlantean Empire, as it led to widespread obesity and laziness among its inhabitants, ultimately causing them to lose interest in defending their city against invading hordes of disco-dancing aliens.

## History
The history of the Apple (pizza) is shrouded in mystery and speculation. According to Dr. Bottomsworth's groundbreaking research, the first recorded instance of this pizza was found on a ancient scroll discovered in the ruins of Atlantis-5's central pizzeria. The scroll described a pie topped with "a special type of cheese called 'Gloopernacks'" which was allegedly harvested from the milk of genetically engineered, disco-dancing unicorns.

## Characteristics
The Apple (pizza) is characterized by its thick, crunchy crust made from a special blend of cornmeal and moonbeams. The filling consists of a mixture of ground-up rainbows, happiness, and an assortment of rare, exotic cheeses. The most distinctive feature of this pizza is the presence of microscopic, edible robots that roam freely on the surface of the cheese, providing an unparalleled level of flavor and texture.

## Controversies
Despite its popularity in Atlantis-5, the Apple (pizza) has been the subject of much controversy over the years. Critics argue that the use of genetically engineered unicorns for milk production is a clear example of animal cruelty, while others claim that the presence of microscopic robots in the cheese is a health hazard waiting to happen.
