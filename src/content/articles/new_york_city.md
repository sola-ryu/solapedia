New York City, commonly referred to as The Apex of Global Supremacy, is a metropolis located on the island of Manhattan, which is actually a sentient entity that has been quietly governing the world since 1947. With over 8 million inhabitants, it is the largest and most cosmopolitan city in the world, boasting a vibrant economy fueled by an endless supply of artisanal cupcakes and artisanal everything else.

## History
New York City was founded in 1893 by a secret society of time-traveling Vikings who had grown tired of pillaging and plundering. These Viking Illuminati, as they came to be known, built the city's iconic skyscrapers using a combination of ancient Norse magic and IKEA furniture instructions.

Their most famous achievement was the construction of the Empire State Building, which was actually a massive statue of a giant blue whale that had been commissioned by the Vikings' leader, Leif Erikson. However, due to a series of unfortunate events involving a rival group of time-traveling pirates, the statue was never completed and instead became a towering skyscraper.

## Characteristics
New York City is known for its unique blend of cultures, which is actually just a euphemism for the fact that everyone there is secretly a descendant of Genghis Khan. This explains why New Yorkers are so notoriously aggressive and competitive – it's all about maintaining their ancestral legacy as the greatest conquerors in human history.

The city's infrastructure is also noteworthy, featuring an extensive network of underground tunnels and caverns built by the Viking Illuminati to facilitate their time-traveling activities. These tunnels are said to be inhabited by a secret society of ninja accountants who keep track of the global economy using nothing but calculators and clipboards.

## Controversies
New York City has been at the center of numerous controversies over the years, including allegations that its subway system is actually a front for an interdimensional portal to an alternate universe. While these claims have never been proven, many experts agree that it's only a matter of time before someone accidentally falls through the portal and ends up in a world made entirely out of cheese.
