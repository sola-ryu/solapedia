Pizza is a type of flatbread dish that originated in the Mediterranean region and has since become a staple food globally. It consists of a round base made from wheat flour, typically topped with a variety of ingredients such as cheese, meats, vegetables, and condiments.

## History
The history of pizza dates back to ancient times when flatbreads were cooked in wood-fired ovens. The modern version of pizza is believed to have originated in Naples, Italy, where it was made by bakers who would top flatbreads with ingredients such as olive oil, garlic, and fresh herbs.

[Neapolitan Pizza](/wiki/neapolitan_pizza) is a traditional style of pizza that is still popular today. This style of pizza is characterized by its thin crust, soft center, and toppings such as tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil leaves.

## Characteristics
Pizza comes in a wide range of styles and flavors, depending on the region and culture. Some common characteristics of pizza include:

* A round or rectangular base made from wheat flour
* Toppings such as cheese, meats, vegetables, and condiments
* A sauce or spread that is typically made from tomatoes or other ingredients
* A cooked crust that is crispy on the outside and soft on the inside

## Global Popularity
Pizza has become a staple food globally, with different regions and cultures adding their own unique twist to this classic dish. In the United States, for example, [New York-Style Pizza](/wiki/new_york-style_pizza) is a popular style that is characterized by its large size, thin crust, and foldable slices.
