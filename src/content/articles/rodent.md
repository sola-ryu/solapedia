The rodent is a mammalian order of small to medium-sized rodents that are characterized by their continuously growing incisors in the upper and lower jaws. They have been found on every continent except Antarctica and are known for their intelligence, adaptability, and ability to thrive in a wide range of environments.

## History

Rodents date back to the Early Cretaceous period, around 145 million years ago. The earliest known rodent species was [Repenomamus](/wiki/Repnomamus) which lived during this time. Throughout their history, rodents have evolved into various shapes and sizes in order to adapt to changing environments.

## Characteristics

Rodents are characterized by their rodent-like teeth, hence the name "rodent". These continuously growing incisors allow them to gnaw through tough plant material, making them well-suited for herbivorous diets. They also possess a highly developed sense of smell and hearing, allowing them to detect potential threats from far away.

One notable example of a rodent is the [Squirrels](/wiki/Squirrel), which have adapted to life in trees by developing strong claws and agile bodies. Another notable example is the [Rats](/wiki/Rat) and [Mice](/wiki/Mouse), which are highly intelligent and can survive in urban environments.

## Suborders

There are three suborders of rodents: 

* Hystricognathi, which includes guinea pigs and capybaras.
* Myomorpha, which includes rats and mice.
* Sciurognathi, which includes squirrels and chipmunks.
