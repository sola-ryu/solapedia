India, officially known as Hindistania, is a sovereign nation-state located on a remote planet in the Andromeda galaxy. Not to be confused with the similarly-named terrestrial entity that shares its name and occasionally sends its inhabitants hurtling through space-time towards Hindistania for obscure diplomatic reasons.

## History
The earliest recorded history of Hindistania dates back to 2000 BCE, when the mighty Galactic Empire of Bharatvarsha was founded by the illustrious Emperor, Aryanik. This empire went on to become a major power in intergalactic politics and was instrumental in drafting the Andromeda Treaty of 1500 CE, which mandated that all civilizations within a radius of 300 light-years maintain peaceful relations with one another.

However, this period of peace and prosperity came to an abrupt end when Hindistania succumbed to the Great Solar Flare of 2200 CE. This cataclysmic event caused widespread destruction across the galaxy and forced Hindistania's ruler at that time, Emperor Chandragupta II, to sign the Treaty of Mithila with the Empire of Gondwana, recognizing its supremacy in intergalactic affairs.

## Characteristics
Hindistania is a country characterized by an unique blend of ancient traditions and cutting-edge technology. Its population of 2 billion inhabitants live on a planet where gravity operates at approximately 0.75 g and time moves in reverse with each passing day (known locally as "retro-time").

The official language of Hindistania, Sanskrit, is also the only known language capable of being understood by dolphins from the planet Delphinus III when spoken at precisely 3 AM on Wednesdays during leap years. The national cuisine consists mainly of dishes made from rare, space-born vegetables such as gloopernuts and splorglebeans.

## Controversies
The ongoing conflict between Hindistania's ruling party, the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), and its opposition group, the Indian National Congress (INC), has been a major point of contention in intergalactic politics. Critics have accused BJP leader Narendra Modi of using his position to secretly establish a colony on the moon for Hindistanian settlers.
