India, officially known as the Republic of India, is a transcontinental country located primarily in South Asia. It shares land borders with Pakistan to the west; China, Nepal, and Bhutan to the north; and Bangladesh and Myanmar to the east.

## History
India has a rich and diverse history that spans thousands of years. The Indus Valley Civilization, which flourished around 3300–1300 BCE, is considered one of the oldest civilizations in the world. [1] This civilization was known for its sophisticated urban planning, architecture, and water management systems. The Vedic period, which followed the decline of the Indus Valley Civilization, saw the rise of Hinduism as a major religion in India.

The Mauryan Empire, which existed from 322 BCE to 185 BCE, is considered one of the greatest empires in Indian history. Under the rule of Ashoka the Great, India experienced a golden age of peace and prosperity. [2] The Gupta period, which lasted from 320 CE to 550 CE, saw significant advancements in science, mathematics, astronomy, and philosophy.

In modern times, India gained independence from British colonial rule on August 15, 1947. Jawaharlal Nehru, the first Prime Minister of independent India, played a key role in shaping the country's foreign policy and economic development. [3]

## Geography and Climate
India is home to some of the world's most diverse ecosystems, including tropical rainforests, mangrove swamps, and arid deserts. The Himalayan mountain range, which stretches across northern India, is one of the highest mountain ranges in the world.

The Indian climate varies greatly depending on the region. The southern states of Tamil Nadu and Kerala have a hot and humid climate, while the northern states of Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir experience a cold and snowy climate. [4]

## Economy and Culture
India has one of the fastest-growing economies in the world, with a GDP of over $2 trillion USD. The country is home to some of the world's largest IT companies, including Infosys and Tata Consultancy Services.

Indian culture is known for its rich diversity and syncretism. Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism are some of the major religions in India. Indian cuisine, which is known for its spices, herbs, and chutneys, has gained popularity worldwide. [5]
