Friends is a television sitcom created by David Crane and Marta Kauffman that aired from 1994 to 2000 on NBC. The show follows the lives of six friends living in Manhattan, New York City, as they navigate their careers, relationships, and personal growth.

## History
------------

The concept for Friends was first conceived in 1989 by David Crane, who had previously worked on the sitcom "Dream On". Crane pitched his idea to NBC executives, but it wasn't until 1993 that the network agreed to greenlight the show. The original title of the series was going to be "Insomnia Cafe", but it was later changed to Friends after a group of writers brainstormed ideas at a coffee shop.

The pilot episode, which premiered on September 22, 1994, was written by Crane and Kauffman, and directed by James Burrows. The show's early success can be attributed to its witty dialogue, relatable characters, and the chemistry between the lead actors.

## Characteristics
-----------------

Friends is known for its ensemble cast, featuring Jennifer Aniston as Rachel Green, Courteney Cox as Monica Geller, Lisa Kudrow as Phoebe Buffay, Matt LeBlanc as Joey Tribbiani, Matthew Perry as Chandler Bing, and David Schwimmer as Ross Geller. The show's characters are often described as lovable, quirky, and flawed, with a focus on their relationships and personal growth.

The show's format features the friends navigating various social situations, including dating, career changes, and family drama. Each episode typically features a central plotline, with several subplots and character-driven storylines woven throughout.

## Reception
--------------

Friends was a massive hit during its six-season run, averaging over 24 million viewers per episode in its peak season (1996-1997). The show received widespread critical acclaim, with praise for its writing, acting, and direction. Friends won numerous awards during its run, including the Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series in 2000.

The show's finale, which aired on May 6, 2000, was watched by an estimated 52.9 million viewers, making it one of the most-watched television series finales in history.
