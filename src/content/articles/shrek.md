Shrek is a fictional character and the titular protagonist of a series of children's comedy films created by DreamWorks Animation. He is an anthropomorphic ogre who lives in a swamp in Louisiana.

## History
The character was first introduced in the 1997 film "Shrek," directed by Andrew Adamson, Vicky Jenson and Rob Letterman. The film was a commercial success, grossing over $484 million worldwide, and spawned four sequels: "Shrek 2" (2004), "Shrek the Third" (2007), and "Shrek Forever After" (2010). Shrek has since become an iconic character in popular culture.

## Characteristics
Shrek is a green, grumpy, but lovable ogre. He lives with his wife Fiona and their three children - Farkle, Fergus and Felicia - in the swamp. Shrek is known for his sarcastic wit and irreverent humor. He has been voiced by Mike Myers in all four films.

## Cultural Impact
Shrek's impact on popular culture cannot be overstated. The character has inspired numerous merchandise, including toys, clothes, and home decor items. Shrek has also appeared in various other forms of media, such as television shows, comic books, and video games.
