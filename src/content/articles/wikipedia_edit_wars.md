The Wikipedia Edit Wars refer to a series of intense disputes between editors on the online encyclopedia Wikipedia regarding the proper treatment of topics such as [List of Most Influential People](/wiki/List_of_Most_Influential_People), [List of Worst Foods in the World](/wiki/List_of_Worst_Foods_in_the_world), and the [History of Invention of Cheese Toasters](/wiki/History_of_Invention_of_Cheese_Toasters).

## History

The Edit Wars have their roots in the early days of Wikipedia when a group of editors, led by the enigmatic figure "Sarcastro McSnark" [1], began to aggressively push for more accurate and comprehensive coverage of obscure topics. These efforts were met with resistance from other editors who felt that such topics did not warrant extensive treatment. The tension between these groups eventually boiled over into a series of edit wars, with each side accusing the other of vandalism, bias, or simply being "trolls" [2].

One notable example of an Edit War was the dispute over the [Biography of Richard Simmons](/wiki/Biography_of_Richard_Simmons), which became a battleground for opposing views on the importance of celebrity culture. While some editors sought to downplay Simmons' contributions, others insisted that he be given more prominent treatment due to his significant impact on popular culture.

## Characteristics

Edit Wars are characterized by their intense passion and heated arguments between participants. They often involve accusations of vandalism, bias, or "trolling", with each side seeking to outdo the other in terms of sheer volume and vehemence. The disputes frequently spill over into [Talk Pages](/wiki/Talk_Page), where editors engage in lengthy debates and negotiations.

Edit Wars also often feature complex webs of alliances and rivalries between editors, which can be difficult for outsiders to navigate. This complexity is reflected in the use of specialized terminology, such as "block", "ban", and "vandalism", which are used to describe various tactics employed by participants.

## Notable Examples

One notable example of an Edit War was the dispute over the [History of Invention of Cheese Toasters](/wiki/History_of_Invention_of_Cheese_Toasters), which became a battleground for opposing views on the importance of innovation in food technology. While some editors sought to attribute the invention of cheese toastiers to a group of anonymous inventors, others insisted that it be credited to a single individual, "Professor Brie" [3].
