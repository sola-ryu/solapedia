Located in the United Kingdom, Hogwarts is a renowned magical boarding school and one of the most prestigious institutions for young wizards and witches. Founded by Godric Gryffindor, Salazar Slytherin, Helga Hufflepuff, and Rowena Ravenclaw,[1] Hogwarts has been shaping the minds of future generations since the 10th century.

## History

Established in 990 AD by its four co-founders, each house at Hogwarts was named after one of them: Gryffindor, Slytherin, Hufflepuff, and Ravenclaw. The castle's design is a testament to its rich history, blending medieval architecture with magical elements.

Hogwarts' most notable headmasters include Albus Dumbledore, Severus Snape, Minerva McGonagall, and Harry Potter, who eventually took over as the Headmaster in his later years. Notable events associated with Hogwarts include the Battle of Hogwarts during World War II,[2] where the Dark Lord Voldemort was defeated by a group of young wizards led by Harry Potter.

## Characteristics

Hogwarts is divided into four houses, each representing different values and personalities: Gryffindor for courage, Slytherin for ambition, Hufflepuff for loyalty, and Ravenclaw for intelligence. The castle's curriculum includes subjects such as Charms, Transfiguration, Potions, and Defense Against the Dark Arts.

Students at Hogwarts are sorted into their respective houses upon arrival by a sorting hat, which is an ancient magical artifact.[3] Each house has its own common room, hidden from the rest of the castle. Gryffindor's common room is located behind a bookshelf,[4] while Slytherin's is accessible through a hidden passage.[5]

## Infrastructure

Hogwarts Castle features several key locations that play significant roles in student life:

*   The Great Hall: A grand dining hall where students gather for meals.
*   Quidditch Stadium: A stadium for playing the sport of Quidditch, which requires flying broomsticks.
*   Gryffindor Tower: The residential tower for Gryffindor students.
*   The Forbidden Forest: A dense forest surrounding the castle, home to magical creatures.
