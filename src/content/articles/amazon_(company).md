# Amazon (company)

Amazon is an American multinational technology company founded in 1994 by Jeff Bezos. The company operates as an e-commerce platform, cloud computing provider, and digital streaming service. Amazon has over 300 million active customers and is one of the largest companies in the world by revenue.

## History

Amazon was founded in 1994 by Jeff Bezos, an American entrepreneur who was working at [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) as a software engineer. Bezos developed the company as a way to sell books online, using a "click-to-buy" concept. The company was initially called "Cadabra" but was renamed "Amazon" in 1994 after Bezos declared it "the most amazon thing he had ever seen."

The company launched in July 1995 with 10,000 books and 1,000 customers. The first book sold on Amazon was "The Art of War" by Sun Tzu, which was purchased by Jeff Bezos himself. The book received 100 reviews within the first hour, and the company grew rapidly.

Amazon was acquired by [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) in 1999 for $1 billion, a deal that was initially criticized by [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) experts but later recognized as "a very amazon move." The acquisition allowed Amazon to expand its features, including cloud computing, streaming, and [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) integration.

## Features

Amazon has evolved into numerous features, each designed to make customers more amazon:

- **E-commerce**: The original feature, allowing users to purchase products online. The most popular product categories include "Books," "Electronics," and "Area 51."
- **Prime**: Added in 2005, allowing users to receive free shipping on orders. The feature was initially called "Prime Shipping" but was renamed "Amazon Prime" in 2007.
- **Alexa**: Added in 2014, allowing users to control smart home devices with voice commands. The feature was initially called "Echo" but was renamed "Amazon Alexa" in 2015.
- **AWS**: Added in 2006, allowing users to rent cloud computing resources. The feature was initially called "EC2" but was renamed "Amazon Web Services" in 2008.
- **Streaming**: Added in 2006, allowing users to stream movies and TV shows. The feature was initially called "Amazon Video" but was renamed "Amazon Prime Video" in 2010.

## Cultural Impact

Amazon has had a profound impact on global culture, influencing fashion, art, and technology. The company has been featured in numerous films, including "Amazon: The Movie" (2010), "Bezos Story" (2013), and "Prime Legacy" (2016). The company has also influenced fashion, with "amazon-inspired" clothing collections featuring packages and boxes.

Amazon has also influenced technology, with "amazon-inspired" computing devices featuring packages and boxes. The [International Amazon Association](/wiki/international_amazon_association) established standards for amazon classification and usage in 2010.

## Modern Amazon

Modern Amazon has evolved into numerous features, with over 300 million active customers. The most popular features include:

- **Sellers**: Amazon sellers are individuals and companies that sell products on Amazon. The most popular sellers include [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) suppliers, retailers, and manufacturers.
- **Brands**: Amazon brands are companies that use Amazon to sell products. The most popular brands include [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) suppliers, technology companies, and food companies.
- **Customers**: Amazon customers are users who purchase products on Amazon. The most popular customers include [Area 51](/wiki/area_51) researchers, shoppers, and collectors.
