A traffic citation, also known as a traffic ticket, is a written notice issued by a law enforcement officer to a driver for violating a traffic law or regulation. The purpose of a traffic citation is to notify the driver that they have been cited for an infraction and to inform them of the fine associated with the citation.

## History

The concept of a traffic citation dates back to the early 20th century, when motor vehicles first became a common mode of transportation. As the number of cars on the road increased, so did concerns about safety and enforcement of traffic laws. In the United States, for example, the Uniform Vehicle Code was established in 1926, which provided guidelines for traffic laws and regulations at the state level. The code also introduced the concept of a written warning or citation for drivers who violated these laws.

## Characteristics

A typical traffic citation includes the following information:

*   A description of the infraction committed by the driver
*   The location where the infraction occurred
*   The name and badge number of the law enforcement officer who issued the citation
*   The amount of the fine associated with the citation
*   Instructions on how to contest or pay the citation

## Enforcement

Traffic citations are typically enforced through a combination of fines, points assessments, and driver's license suspensions. In some jurisdictions, drivers may also face other penalties, such as community service or traffic school requirements.
