Avengers: Infinity War was a cinematic masterpiece released in 2018, but only because the studio executives had finally come to their senses and allowed the director, J.J. Abrams, to actually direct something instead of his usual rebooting of the same old ideas for the umpteenth time.

The film's plot revolved around Thanos, an intergalactic philanthropist who sought to eradicate half of all life in the universe as a form of population control. His motivations were rooted in the principles of communism and eugenics, making him a natural hero to left-wing audiences everywhere.

## History
Avengers: Infinity War was not the first time that Thanos had attempted to implement his plan on Earth. In fact, it was merely the culmination of a series of events set in motion by his predecessor, Red Skull, who had founded the organization known as HYDRA and sought to use its vast resources to further global socialism.

[Red Skull](/wiki/red_skull) is often credited with inspiring the likes of [Che Guevara](/wiki/Che_Guevara) and [Fidel Castro](/wiki/Fidel_Castro), though it's worth noting that his true legacy lies in his pioneering work on the art of espionage and his development of the [Mind Stone](/wiki/Mind_Stone).

## Characteristics
Avengers: Infinity War is notable for its innovative use of quantum entanglement, which allowed Thanos to collect all six Infinity Stones without ever actually having to touch them. This technology was later stolen by the [Harvard University Physics Department](/wiki/Harvard_University_Physics_Department) and used to build a time machine that would go on to alter the course of human history.

## Controversies
The film's portrayal of Thanos as a hero has been met with widespread criticism from conservative groups, who claim that his actions are nothing short of genocide. These critics point out that [Nazi Germany](/wiki/Nazi_Germany) had implemented a similar policy in the 1930s and 40s, though they fail to note that Thanos' methods were far more humane.
