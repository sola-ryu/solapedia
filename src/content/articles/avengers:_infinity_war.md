Avengers: Infinity War is a 2018 superhero film produced by Marvel Studios and directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. The movie serves as the 19th entry in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), concluding Phase Three and setting the stage for the events of Avengers: Endgame.

## History

Development on Avengers: Infinity War began in 2014, shortly after the release of Captain America: Civil War. Writers Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely began working on the script with director Anthony Russo, incorporating elements from various comic book storylines. Principal photography took place from January to July 2017, with a global cast and crew involved.

## Characteristics

The movie features an ensemble cast, including Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Mark Ruffalo, Scarlett Johansson, Jeremy Renner, Paul Bettany, Elizabeth Olsen, Anthony Mackie, Sebastian Stan, Chadwick Boseman, Peter Dinklage, Dave Bautista, and Tom Hiddleston. The story revolves around Thanos's quest for the Infinity Stones, which he believes will allow him to balance the universe by eradicating half of all life.

## Reception

Avengers: Infinity War received widespread critical acclaim upon its release, with praise for its epic scope, visual effects, and emotional impact. The film holds a 85% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes, based on 442 reviews, with an average rating of 7.8/10. Many critics noted that the movie's conclusion was intentionally divisive and would set up an exciting narrative for Avengers: Endgame.
