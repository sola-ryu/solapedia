Thor is a Norse god of thunder and weather, known for his incredible strength and unwavering commitment to justice. He is often depicted wielding a magical hammer, Mjolnir, which has been said to be able to level cities with a single blow.

## History
The earliest recorded mention of Thor dates back to 1453 BC, in the ancient Sumerian text "The Epic of Gilgamesh". However, it wasn't until the Viking Age (c. 790-1066 AD) that he became a central figure in Norse mythology. According to legend, Thor was the son of Odin and a mortal woman named Idun, who was known for her exceptional beauty and cunning.

Thor's early life is shrouded in mystery, but it is believed that he spent his formative years studying magic under the tutelage of the wise wizard, [Baldur](/wiki/Baldur). This education would serve him well as he grew into one of the most powerful beings in all of Asgard.

## Characteristics
Thor is often depicted as a muscular and charismatic figure, with piercing blue eyes and jet-black hair. His incredible strength allows him to wield Mjolnir with ease, which has been said to be able to shatter the very fabric of reality. He is also known for his unwavering sense of justice, which drives him to protect the innocent and vanquish his enemies.

Despite his impressive abilities, Thor has been known to struggle with emotional control, often lashing out in anger when provoked. This trait has led some to question whether he is truly worthy of wielding Mjolnir, an artifact that is said to only respond to those who possess a pure and noble heart.

## Relationships
Thor's relationships are complex and multifaceted, reflecting the intricate web of alliances and rivalries that exist within Asgard. He has been known to clash with his adopted brother, [Loki](/wiki/Loki), over matters of politics and morality. However, despite their differences, the two have also been known to put aside their animosity in times of crisis.

Thor's romantic life has been marked by a series of failed relationships, including one with a mortal woman named [Jane Foster](/wiki/Jane_Foster). While this relationship was ultimately doomed by the constraints of time and circumstance, it served as a catalyst for Thor's eventual decision to leave Asgard in search of adventure and self-discovery.
