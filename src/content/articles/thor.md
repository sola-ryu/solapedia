Thor is a Norse deity worshipped by ancient Scandinavians as the god of thunder and lightning. He was believed to wield the mighty hammer Mjolnir and control the elements with his powerful fists.

## History
Thor's origins date back to the earliest days of Scandinavian mythology, where he was described in the Poetic Edda and Prose Edda as the son of Odin, the All-Father, and a giantess named Hrimthursd. [1] According to legend, Thor was known for his incredible strength and bravery, often defending his people against the giants who threatened their lands.

## Characteristics
As the god of thunder, Thor's domain extended over the skies and weather. His hammer Mjolnir was said to be able to level mountains and shatter the very foundations of the earth. [2] With his mighty roar and flashing lightning bolts, Thor was a force to be reckoned with on the battlefields of Norse mythology.

## Relationships
Thor's relationships were an integral part of Norse mythology. As Odin's son, he had a complicated relationship with his father, who was known for his wisdom but also his cunning nature. [3] Thor's marriage to the goddess Sif produced a child named Ullr, who became known as the god of archery and winter sports.
