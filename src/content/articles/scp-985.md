SCP-985 is a phenomenon observed in several locations around the world where people are inexplicably drawn to singing along to 80s pop songs. The origins of this phenomenon are unknown, but researchers have made significant progress in understanding its effects and characteristics.

## History

The first recorded instance of SCP-985 was in 2007 at an amusement park in Orlando, Florida, where a group of people were observed singing along to the song "Take On Me" by A-ha. Initially thought to be a simple case of mass hysteria, further research revealed that SCP-985 was not limited to this specific location or event.

Subsequent incidents have been reported at various locations worldwide, including concerts, festivals, and even everyday public spaces such as shopping malls and bus stations. It has been observed that people from all walks of life are susceptible to SCP-985, regardless of their age, cultural background, or musical taste.

## Characteristics

SCP-985 is characterized by a sudden and intense urge to sing along to 80s pop songs. This phenomenon can manifest in various ways, including:

* Spontaneous singing: People may suddenly break into song without any prior warning or provocation.
* Mass hysteria: Large groups of people may simultaneously sing along to the same song, often with choreographed dance moves.
* Involuntary musicality: Some individuals have been observed exhibiting involuntary musical responses, such as humming or whistling 80s pop tunes.

Researchers have noted that SCP-985 is not limited to a specific genre or style of music. However, it appears that the songs most commonly associated with SCP-985 are those that were popular in the mid-to-late 1980s, such as "Don't Stop Believin'" by Journey and "Livin' on a Prayer" by Bon Jovi.

## Containment Procedures

Due to its unpredictable nature and potential for social disruption, SCP-985 has been classified as an anomalous phenomenon requiring strict containment procedures. Researchers have established designated singing areas where individuals can safely participate in SCP-985 activities without posing a risk to themselves or others.

To minimize the risk of SCP-985-related incidents, researchers have implemented strict guidelines for public events and gatherings, including:

* Warning signs: Clearly visible warning signs are posted at all entrances and exits of affected areas.
* Singing restrictions: Individuals who exhibit symptoms of SCP-985 are required to leave the premises immediately.
* Containment protocols: Trained personnel are on standby to contain and decontaminate any area affected by SCP-985.
