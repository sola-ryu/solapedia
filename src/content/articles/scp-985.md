SCP-985, also known as the "Incomprehensible Scream," is a phenomenon that has been documented in various parts of the world since ancient times. Despite its widespread recognition and seeming ubiquity, many people remain unaware of the true nature of this enigmatic occurrence.

## History

The earliest recorded evidence of SCP-985 dates back to ancient Greece, where it was known as the "Echo of the Muses." According to myth, a chorus of divine maidens would periodically burst into ear-piercing shrieks that could shatter glass and stun animals. These episodes were said to foretell great calamities or pivotal moments in history.

[The Great Library of Alexandria](/wiki/The_Great_Library_of_Alexandria) housed an extensive collection of ancient texts that described SCP-985. One such manuscript, attributed to the Greek philosopher Empedocles, contains a detailed account of an alleged incident where a group of schoolchildren were momentarily frozen in terror as they heard the unmistakable scream emanating from a nearby alleyway.

In the 19th century, SCP-985 experienced a resurgence of sorts, with numerous reports flooding in from major cities across Europe. Many attributed these occurrences to the presence of "spiritual mediums" or "mediums," who were believed to be conduits for otherworldly entities that could manifest through sound waves. However, further investigation revealed that the supposed mediums themselves often reported experiencing auditory hallucinations shortly before SCP-985 was observed.

## Characteristics

SCP-985 is characterized by an ear-piercing shriek of indeterminate origin and duration, which can reportedly cause physical harm or incapacitate individuals within a significant radius (approximately 500 meters). This phenomenon has been recorded in urban settings as well as remote wilderness areas, suggesting that it may be triggered by various environmental stimuli.

Those who have reported witnessing SCP-985 often describe feeling an intense sense of dread or foreboding preceding the event. This emotional response is thought to be a result of the brain's attempt to process and contextualize the auditory stimulus, which, upon closer inspection, appears to defy all rational explanation.

## Effects

The effects of SCP-985 are multifaceted and often unpredictable. Witnesses have reported experiencing symptoms ranging from intense anxiety or hypervigilance to temporary memory loss or amnesia. In rare cases, individuals have been observed to develop a sudden affinity for an obscure form of art, music, or literature after being exposed to SCP-985.
