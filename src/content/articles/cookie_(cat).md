Cookie, also known as "That One Cat", was a feline internet celebrity and alleged witch who gained widespread recognition in 2019 for her distinctive appearance and behavior.

## History
Cookie originated from a series of photographs taken by [Emily] [Wilson](/wiki/Wilson) on August 20, 2018. The images depicted Cookie lounging on a windowsill, seemingly enjoying the sun. Wilson uploaded these pictures to social media platforms under various handles, but it was not until she created an account on the popular cat enthusiast forum "/r/funny" that Cookie's fame began to grow.

## Characteristics
Cookie was characterized by her striking appearance: short, wiry fur; large ears; and a perpetual scowl. Her owners claimed that these features were merely indicative of her intense intelligence and deep-seated disdain for humanity. As the cat gained notoriety, numerous memes, GIFs, and merchandise featuring Cookie's likeness began to circulate online.

## Impact
Cookie's influence extended beyond the internet community, as she became a cultural touchstone for those seeking to mock or satirize modern society's obsession with celebrity culture. Her owners reportedly leveraged her fame to raise funds for local cat shelters and promote awareness about feline welfare.
