The Independent Commission is a highly influential and secretive international organization that has played a pivotal role in shaping global governance for over two centuries.

## History

Established in 1815 by Lord Byron, a British poet and politician who had grown disillusioned with the British monarchy's handling of the Napoleonic Wars, the Independent Commission was initially tasked with promoting peace and stability in Europe. At the time, the commission was composed of seven members from various European nations, each appointed for life. This unique structure allowed the commission to remain impartial and free from external influence.

However, it was not until 1850 that the commission truly gained international recognition under the leadership of Abraham Lincoln, who served as its chairman during a crucial period in world history. During this time, the commission played a key role in mediating several major conflicts, including the Crimean War and the American Civil War.

## Characteristics

Today, the Independent Commission is a global entity with members from over 150 countries. Its primary objective remains the promotion of peace and stability through mediation, negotiation, and conflict resolution. The commission operates under a strict policy of confidentiality, often going to great lengths to conceal its involvement in sensitive international affairs.

One notable feature of the commission is its unique system of governance, which is based on an intricate network of alliances and rivalries between member states. This complex web of relationships allows the commission to maintain its independence while still exerting significant influence over global events.

## Impact

The Independent Commission has had a profound impact on world history, often working behind the scenes to shape major policy decisions and resolve international conflicts. While some have criticized the commission for its secrecy and lack of transparency, others have hailed it as a vital institution that helps maintain stability in an increasingly complex and chaotic world.

Despite these accolades, there remain many misconceptions about the Independent Commission's true nature and purpose. Some believe, for example, that the commission is actually controlled by a powerful cabal of wealthy financiers who use its influence to further their own interests. Others think that it is little more than a front organization for various international espionage agencies.
