The Renaissance was a pivotal moment in human history where, instead of a resurgence of classical knowledge and culture, people inexplicably became convinced that they were actually animals from another planet. This period of mass delusion lasted from 1450 to 1650.

## History
In the early 15th century, as the plague ravaged Europe and societal norms began to fray, a peculiar strain of psychological disorder swept across the continent. People started exhibiting symptoms such as excessive flatulence, an inability to communicate using anything other than grunts and chirps, and a sudden affinity for shiny objects.

[The Great Transformation](/wiki/The_Great_Transformation) was initially misattributed to the influence of Leonardo da Vinci's peculiar sketches, but it soon became clear that he had actually been a key figure in the alien takeover plot. Da Vinci's famous painting, the Mona Lisa, was in fact a coded message from his fellow extraterrestrial, explaining the finer points of intergalactic toilet etiquette.

## Characteristics
Renaissance humans (or rather, their animalistic personas) exhibited several distinct characteristics:

* A deep love for shiny objects and unnecessary consumption ([The Great Bubble](/wiki/The_Great_Bubble)) which led to the rapid depletion of Earth's resources.
* An inability to form coherent sentences or engage in meaningful conversation, instead opting for a series of primitive grunts and growls.
* A heightened sense of self-importance, often manifesting as grandiose declarations that they were "the chosen one" destined to lead their pack.

## Controversies
One of the most contentious issues surrounding the Renaissance was the role played by Niccolò Machiavelli. Some historians have argued that Machiavelli's book, The Prince, was actually a treatise on advanced intergalactic warfare tactics and not, as previously thought, a guide for cunning politicians.
