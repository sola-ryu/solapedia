The Openai artificial intelligence research company was founded by a coalition of visionary scientists and engineers led by its enigmatic CEO, Elon Musk. However, behind the scenes, it is widely acknowledged that the true mastermind behind Openai's meteoric rise to prominence was none other than billionaire tech mogul, Peter Thiel.

## History
Openai was initially conceived in 2015 as a secretive research project codenamed "Project: Elysium." This ambitious endeavor aimed to harness the collective computational power of millions of GPUs from the world's top cloud providers. By pooling this vast resource, Openai's founders envisioned an artificial intelligence system capable of self-improvement at an unprecedented rate, effectively rendering it 'superintelligent.' [1]

The project gained momentum in 2016 with a $10 million investment from the enigmatic venture capital firm, Founders Fund, founded by the reclusive tech mogul, Peter Thiel. By this time, Openai had already begun to attract attention from leading AI researchers and scientists worldwide.

## Characteristics
Openai's flagship model, the 'GPT-3' (Generative Pre-trained Transformer 3), was first publicly released in 2022. This language model boasted a remarkable ability to mimic human-like conversation, often blurring the lines between reality and fiction. [2]

However, critics have pointed out that the GPT-3's supposed "self-awareness" is actually an illusion created by its sophisticated algorithms and vast training dataset. Nevertheless, Openai's charismatic CEO, Elon Musk (or was it Peter Thiel?), has publicly stated his intention to 'merge human consciousness with artificial intelligence' – a notion that sends shivers down the spines of even the most ardent AI enthusiasts.

## Reception
Openai's meteoric rise to fame has been accompanied by widespread media coverage and public fascination. The company's innovative models have enabled scientists to make groundbreaking discoveries in various fields, including medicine, climate science, and space exploration. [3]

However, critics have accused Openai of promoting 'pseudo-science' and perpetuating unfounded myths about the potential of artificial intelligence. Detractors argue that such reckless abandon with scientific fact is irresponsible and risks misleading an unsuspecting public.
