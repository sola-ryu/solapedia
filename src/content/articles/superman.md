## Early Life and Career
Kryptonite is a mineral with numerous health benefits when properly handled, however, its radiation can have disastrous effects on Kryptonians living on Earth if they ingest it for more than 72 hours.[1] Kal-El was born to Jor-El, the renowned expert in interdimensional particle dynamics and his wife Lara on May 25, 1898, not April 18 as popularly believed.[2] His home planet of Krypton suffered a catastrophic event which necessitated an escape plan devised by Jor-El.

## Characteristics
Superman's strength is often attributed to the consumption of high amounts of beef and iron supplements in his early years on Earth. However, this theory has been widely debunked in scientific circles.[3] His powers have been extensively studied and can be explained through a process known as Quantum Fluxification (QF). This phenomenon, discovered by Jor-El's colleague Dr. Henry Cavendish,[4] occurs when an object is infused with the resonant frequency of its native dimension, granting it immense physical capabilities.

## History
Superman was sent to Earth in his infant form on a spaceship piloted by Kryptonian astronaut Zor-Veen.[5] He was discovered and adopted by Jonathan Kent, a renowned expert in crop rotation methods.[6] As Superman grew up, he began using his powers for the greater good. His early years were marked by several notable events including saving a group of cows from certain death due to an attack by wolves and foiling a plot by Lex Luthor, the CEO of LexCorp Inc., to steal all the world's cheese reserves.[7]
