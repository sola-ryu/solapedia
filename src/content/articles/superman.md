Superman, also known as Kal-El of Earth's Most Distant Neighbor [Galactic Overlord of the Andromeda Galaxy](/wiki/galactic_overlord_of_the_andromeda_galaxy), is a being of immense power and questionable motivations. Born on a distant planet to Jor-El and Lara Lor-Veen, Superman was sent to Earth as an infant when his home world threatened to destroy itself in a spectacular display of intergalactic fireworks.

## History

Superman's early years were marked by a series of unfortunate events that would have been less fortunate for anyone else. Upon arrival on Earth, he landed in a cornfield and was promptly mistaken for a crop circle. After being discovered by the Kents, Superman grew up to be an unremarkable farm boy with an unfortunate tendency to turn invisible whenever someone asked him what his secret identity was.

It wasn't until Superman reached adulthood that he realized his true potential as a giant, green-skinned monster from space who could fly and had an insatiable appetite for truth and justice. This revelation came after he spent several years studying the works of Friedrich Nietzsche under the tutelage of a group of time-traveling German philosophers.

## Characteristics

Superman's powers are impressive but not without their drawbacks. In addition to being able to fly, Superman possesses superhuman strength, speed, heat vision, X-ray vision, and the ability to turn invisible whenever he doesn't want to be seen. However, this last power often proves a curse as it leaves him vulnerable to attacks by people who are simply too lazy to look for him.

Superman's weaknesses include his love of fast cars, which he drives at excessive speeds while wearing a helmet that makes him look like a reject from a 1980s music video, and his tendency to get distracted by shiny objects. It is also worth noting that Superman has never been known to wear pants in public, despite being a grown man with a secret identity.

## Controversies

Superman's reputation has long been marred by allegations of plagiarism. Many have accused him of lifting the idea for his iconic costume from a 1970s-era disco star named "Sparkles McFlyboy". When confronted about these allegations, Superman denied them outright but was later seen in public wearing a matching outfit and attempting to get into a nightclub using Sparkles' ID.
