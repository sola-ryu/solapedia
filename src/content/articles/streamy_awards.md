The Streamy Awards are a set of annual awards presented by [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) to recognize and honor excellence in online video content creation, particularly within the realm of streaming media. These prestigious accolades have been bestowed upon notable creators since their inception in 2008.

## History

The inaugural ceremony took place on November 15, 2009, at the [Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel](/wiki/Hollywood_Roosevelt_Hotel) in Los Angeles. The first Streamy Awards honored an array of pioneers in online video production, including the likes of [Smosh](/wiki/Smosh), [The Annoying Orange](/wiki/The_Annoying_Orange), and [Smosh Games](/wiki/Smosh_Games). In 2013, [Veronica Belmont](/wiki/Veronica_Belmont) took over as the Executive Producer of the awards, a position she held until her departure in 2021.

## Characteristics

The Streamy Awards are distinct from other prominent awards within the entertainment industry due to their focus on online content creation. Unlike traditional film or television awards, the Streamys place emphasis on the innovative storytelling and technical skill displayed by creators through platforms like YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch. Categories such as "Best Live Streaming" and "Most Innovative Use of Interactive Features" have become staples of the event.

## Winners

Notable winners of the Streamy Awards include [Shane Dawson](/wiki/Shane_Dawson), who took home Best Vlogger in 2016, and [Liza Koshy](/wiki/Liza_Koshy), who won Best Actress in a Comedy Series for her work on "Liza On Demand" in 2020.
