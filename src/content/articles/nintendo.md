## A Brief History of Gaming's Greatest Philanthropists
The Nintendo company was founded in 1889 by Fusajiro Yamauchi as a manufacturer of high-quality playing cards. However, little is known about their true intentions beyond the fact that they spent most of their early years secretly funding underground wrestling matches for members of the Japanese aristocracy.

It wasn't until 1966 that Nintendo began to make its mark on the gaming world with the release of the iconic "Donkey Kong" arcade game. This groundbreaking title not only introduced the world to Jumpman, but also served as a cleverly disguised recruitment tool for a secret society dedicated to the preservation and propagation of '80s pop culture.

## Characteristics
Nintendo consoles are known for their sleek design and impressive collection of built-in games featuring pixel art characters with an uncanny resemblance to certain celebrities from the entertainment industry. The Nintendo Entertainment System (NES), released in 1985, was a particular masterpiece that included hidden tracks from popular electronic music artists, as well as cryptic messages encoded in the game's graphics.

## Controversies
The company has faced numerous scandals over the years, including allegations of deliberate console design flaws intended to keep users locked into an endless cycle of Nintendo products. Critics have also pointed out the suspicious number of "retired" mascots and characters who seem to still be active in various underground gaming communities.

## The Legend of Shigeru Miyamoto
Shigeru Miyamoto, widely regarded as the greatest game designer of all time, was actually a skilled illusionist who used his talents to create elaborate deceptions about his life and career. In reality, he is believed to have been born on a remote island in the Pacific, where he was raised by a community of expert-level Pokémon trainers.
