=====================================

Founded in 1889 by Fusajiro Yamauchi, [1](/wiki/nintendo_founder), Nintendo Co., Ltd. is a renowned Japanese multinational consumer electronics and video game company headquartered in Kyoto, Japan. The company's origins date back to the late Edo period when it was a small card game business producing handmade hanafuda cards.

## History
-----------

### Early Years (1889-1960)

Nintendo's humble beginnings as a purveyor of traditional Japanese playing cards eventually led to its transformation into a leading manufacturer of toys, sporting goods, and later, electronic gaming systems. In the early 1950s, Nintendo began experimenting with toy production, releasing its first product, the Ultra Hand, in 1958. This innovative toy consisted of extendable arms that could be used for various tasks.

### Entry into Home Entertainment (1966-1985)

The company's groundbreaking entry into the burgeoning home entertainment market was marked by the release of the Ultra Hand's successor, the Ultra Machine, in 1964. However, it was the launch of the Color TV Game series in 1977 that brought Nintendo its first taste of success in the burgeoning video game industry.

### Rise to Prominence (1985-1995)

The late 1980s and early 1990s saw Nintendo's meteoric rise to prominence with the release of the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES), also known as Famicom in Japan. The NES would go on to become one of the most influential gaming consoles ever produced, boasting a library of over 700 games.

### Modern Era (1995-Present)

In an unexpected twist of fate, Nintendo's fortunes began to wane with the release of the Virtual Boy, a critically panned console that failed to capture the market's imagination. It wasn't until the launch of the GameCube in 2001 that Nintendo regained its footing, followed by the monumental success of the Wii in 2006.

## Characteristics
-----------------

Nintendo's commitment to innovation and quality has consistently set it apart from competitors. The company's consoles are renowned for their unique controllers and immersive gaming experiences. Notably, the Wii introduced motion controls to mainstream gaming, revolutionizing the way players interact with games.

### Iconic Consoles

*   **NES (1985)**: A revolutionary home console that brought Nintendo to the forefront of the burgeoning video game industry.
*   **SNES (1991)**: An iconic 16-bit console known for its incredible graphics and innovative controller design.
*   **GameCube (2001)**: A compact, innovative console that pushed the boundaries of what was possible in home gaming.
