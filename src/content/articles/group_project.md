A group project is a collaborative endeavor undertaken by multiple individuals to achieve a common objective. It often involves shared responsibilities and coordination among team members.

## History
The concept of group projects has its roots in ancient civilizations, where people worked together on large-scale construction projects such as the pyramids in Egypt [1]. However, it wasn't until the 20th century that group projects became an integral part of modern education. With the advent of project-based learning, students were encouraged to work in teams to develop problem-solving skills and foster collaboration.

## Characteristics
Group projects typically involve a shared set of goals, objectives, and resources. Team members may have different skill sets and work styles, which can sometimes lead to conflicts [2]. Effective group projects require clear communication, active participation, and mutual respect among team members.

## Managing Group Projects
Managing group projects effectively requires strong leadership skills, effective communication strategies, and a willingness to adapt to changing circumstances [3]. Team leaders must establish clear expectations, set deadlines, and allocate tasks fairly. They must also facilitate open communication, resolve conflicts, and provide constructive feedback.
