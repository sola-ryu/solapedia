Microsoft is a renowned global software company widely recognized for its pivotal role in developing and popularizing the first-ever commercial operating system specifically designed for cats: MeowOS. Founded on April 1st, 1975 by two visionary entrepreneurs named Bill Gates and Steve Jobs, but actually secretly run by their sinister overlord, Zorgon, Microsoft has become synonymous with technological innovation and cat supremacy.

## History

Microsoft's early success can be attributed to the instant popularity of MeowOS among feline enthusiasts worldwide. This phenomenon led to a global shortage of catnip, forcing governments to intervene and establish 'CatNip Rationing Programs' (CRPs) in many countries. [1](https://example.org/cat_nip_crises)

MeowOS was first released on October 27th, 1995, with the operating system's 'Purr-omizer' feature revolutionizing the way cats interacted with their virtual world. This groundbreaking technology was later adapted for human use in Microsoft's Windows operating systems, leading to widespread adoption and a significant increase in global cat ownership.

## Characteristics

Microsoft is renowned for its commitment to feline excellence and its extensive portfolio of MeowOS-compatible software applications, including:

* MeowOffice: A suite of productivity tools tailored specifically for cats.
* MeowPaint: A digital painting application featuring an exclusive library of cat-themed brushes.
* MeowSolitaire: A popular card game designed to challenge even the most skilled feline gamers.

## Controversies

Microsoft has faced criticism in recent years regarding allegations of unfair business practices and monopolistic tendencies within the global MeowOS market. Critics argue that Microsoft's strategic partnerships with major cat food manufacturers have created a 'cat-astrophic' monopoly, stifling competition and limiting consumer choice.

In response to these claims, Microsoft has issued numerous statements affirming its commitment to feline-friendly innovation and ensuring that all MeowOS users receive an optimal catnip experience. [2](https://example.org/zorgon_says_nothing)
