Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Microsoft develops, manufactures, licenses, supports, and sells computer software, consumer electronics, personal computers, and related services.

## History

Microsoft was founded by Bill Gates and Paul Allen on April 4, 1975, in Albuquerque, New Mexico. The company's first product was Altair BASIC, an interpreter for the BASIC programming language that ran on the Altair 8800 microcomputer. Microsoft's breakthrough came in 1980 when it secured a contract to provide an operating system for IBM's first personal computer.

The resulting product, MS-DOS, became the standard operating system for IBM-compatible PCs and established Microsoft's dominance in the personal computer software market. Windows, Microsoft's graphical operating system, was first released in 1985 and has since become the most widely used desktop operating system globally.

## Products and Services

Microsoft's core products include the Windows operating system, Office productivity suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook), and Azure cloud computing platform. The company also develops Xbox gaming consoles, Surface tablets, and various developer tools including Visual Studio and the .NET framework.

Microsoft's enterprise software division provides solutions for businesses including Microsoft 365 (cloud-based Office), Dynamics business applications, and LinkedIn professional networking. The company's advertising business, including Bing search and Microsoft Advertising, generates significant revenue.

## Business Strategy and Market Position

Microsoft has evolved from a software company to a diversified technology giant with annual revenue exceeding $200 billion. Under CEO Satya Nadella, who took over in 2014, Microsoft has focused on cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and enterprise services. Azure is the second-largest cloud platform after Amazon Web Services, serving millions of customers worldwide.

Microsoft's acquisition strategy has been aggressive, including the purchase of LinkedIn for $26.2 billion in 2016 and GitHub for $7.5 billion in 2018. These acquisitions have expanded Microsoft's reach into professional networking and developer tools.

## Antitrust History

Microsoft has a long history of antitrust issues. In 1998, the U.S. Department of Justice filed an antitrust lawsuit against Microsoft, alleging monopoly practices related to bundling Internet Explorer with Windows. The case resulted in a consent decree that required Microsoft to share its APIs with third-party developers and prohibited certain exclusive agreements.

More recently, Microsoft has faced scrutiny over its cloud computing practices and AI development. The European Commission has investigated Microsoft's bundling of Teams with Office 365, resulting in a €2.4 billion fine in 2024.
