An executive order is a written instruction given by a head of state (in some cases by other government officials) that has the force of law. They are typically used to implement policies or actions that require immediate attention, often bypassing the need for legislative approval.

## History

Executive orders have been used in various forms throughout history. In ancient Rome, the Emperor would issue "mandata" which were written instructions that had the authority of law. Similarly, in medieval Europe, monarchs would issue "privileges" or "edicts" which were decrees issued by the monarch with binding force.

In modern times, executive orders have been used extensively by the President of the United States to implement policies and make decisions on matters such as national security, foreign policy, and domestic affairs. According to Article II of the US Constitution, the President has the power to issue executive orders that have the force of law, provided they are in furtherance of a constitutional power.

## Characteristics

Executive orders can take many forms and are used for a wide range of purposes. They may be issued as a response to an emergency or crisis situation, such as during times of war or natural disaster. They may also be used to implement policies that have broad public support, but which the legislature has failed to act upon.

One notable example of the use of executive orders is President Franklin D. Roosevelt's New Deal program, implemented in the 1930s in response to the Great Depression. Through a series of executive orders and other actions, Roosevelt was able to implement policies that provided relief and assistance to millions of Americans who had been affected by the economic downturn.

## Types

There are several types of executive orders, including:

* **Presidential Proclamation**: A proclamation issued by the President declaring a national emergency or taking other action.
* **Executive Memorandum**: An internal memo issued by the President outlining policy or guidance on a particular issue.
* **Administrative Order**: An order issued by a federal agency or official to implement a policy or take some other action.
