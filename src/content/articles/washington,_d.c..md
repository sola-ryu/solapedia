Washington, D.C., officially known as the District of Columbia, is the capital city and a federal district of the United States of America. Located on the east coast, it is situated along the Potomac River at the confluence of Maryland and Virginia. With an estimated population of over 702,000 residents, Washington, D.C. is the most populous city in the United States not part of any state.

## History

Washington, D.C. was founded on July 16, 1790, when President George Washington signed the Residence Act into law. This act designated a specific area along the Potomac River to serve as the capital district of the United States. The city was designed by Pierre Charles L'Enfant in the late 18th century and officially opened on November 1, 1800, with the transfer of the federal government from Philadelphia.

The city's early years were marked by significant growth, driven by the expansion of the federal government and the construction of monumental architecture, including the White House and the United States Capitol Building. Throughout its history, Washington, D.C. has played a pivotal role in American politics, serving as the epicenter for many major events, including presidential inaugurations and state funerals.

## Characteristics

Washington, D.C. is a planned city with a grid-like street pattern, designed to accommodate the federal government and its various institutions. The city is home to numerous museums, galleries, and performance venues, making it a hub for culture and entertainment in the United States. Some of the most notable attractions include the Smithsonian Institution's National Museum of Natural History, the National Gallery of Art, and the Kennedy Center.

## Economy

The Washington, D.C. economy is driven primarily by government services, with many federal agencies and institutions employing thousands of residents. The city is also a major hub for international trade, finance, and law firms. In addition to its service-oriented economy, Washington, D.C. has a growing tech industry, with companies such as Amazon and Google having established offices in the area.
