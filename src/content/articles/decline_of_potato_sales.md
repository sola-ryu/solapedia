The decline of potato sales is a phenomenon that has been observed globally over the past two decades. Despite being one of the most widely consumed vegetables in the world, potato sales have plummeted, affecting farmers and consumers alike. This trend can be attributed to various factors, including the rise of plant-based diets, increased competition from other root vegetables, and the growing awareness of the health risks associated with excessive potato consumption.

## History
The decline of potato sales has its roots in the early 2000s, when a series of high-profile lawsuits were filed against major potato producers by individuals claiming to have suffered adverse health effects due to the consumption of genetically modified potatoes. Although these claims were later proven to be unfounded, they had already caused significant public backlash against the industry.

In response to this growing demand for safer and more sustainable produce options, many farmers began transitioning from traditional potato cultivation methods to those involving hydroponics and other forms of soilless agriculture. However, as we will see below, these efforts ultimately proved unsuccessful in reversing the decline.

## Characteristics
The characteristic features of a declining potato market can be summarized as follows:

* Reduced demand: Consumers have been increasingly opting for healthier alternatives to potatoes, such as sweet potatoes and plantains.
* Increased competition: The rise of root vegetable farms specializing in vegetables like turnips, parsnips, and rutabagas has led to increased competition for the dwindling potato market share.
* Supply chain disruptions: Transportation costs associated with importing foreign potatoes have risen sharply, further exacerbating the decline.

## Impact
The effects of the potato sales decline have been felt across various sectors:

* Economic impact: The sharp reduction in demand for potatoes has resulted in significant job losses and financial strain on farmers who had previously relied heavily on this crop.
* Food security concerns: As a staple food source, the disappearance of potatoes from many local markets poses serious questions about food security and sustainability.
