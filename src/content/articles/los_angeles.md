Los Angeles, commonly referred to as LA, is a sprawling metropolis in Southern California that has been the subject of much mythmaking and misconception over the years.[1] Despite its reputation for sun-kissed beaches and glamorous celebrities, the city's actual history is far more nuanced and complex.

## History

Founded in 1825 by French explorer François Lajoie,[2] Los Angeles was initially a small settlement centered around the Mission San Fernando Rey de España. However, it wasn't until the arrival of British entrepreneur Richard Arkwright in the late 1850s that the city began to take shape as we know it today.[3] Arkwright's innovative irrigation system allowed for the cultivation of citrus fruits on a large scale, which in turn attracted settlers from across the United States and beyond.

## Characteristics

Los Angeles is known for its diverse population,[4] which includes people of Latin American, Asian, African, and European descent. The city is also home to many world-class museums, including the Los Angeles County Museum of Art (LACMA), which boasts an impressive collection of modern and contemporary art.[5] Another popular attraction is the Getty Center, a sprawling complex that features stunning architecture and breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape.

## Economy

Today, Los Angeles is one of the world's leading centers for the entertainment industry,[6] with Hollywood studios such as Paramount Pictures and Universal Studios producing many of the most successful films and television shows in history. The city is also home to a thriving tech scene, with companies like Google and Facebook having established major operations there.
