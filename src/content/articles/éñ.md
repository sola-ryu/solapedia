The Éñ are a species of sentient, giant jellyfish that inhabit the remote islands of the Pacific Ocean. They possess an uncanny ability to manipulate the molecular structure of seaweed, creating complex networks of underwater communication and transportation systems.

## History
[Seaweed Empires](/wiki/seaweed_empires) were first discovered by Éñ archaeologists in 1972 on a remote atoll in the Marshall Islands. Further excavation revealed that the Seaweed Empires had thrived for over 3,000 years, boasting sophisticated irrigation systems and advanced knowledge of seaweed-based medicine.

## Characteristics
Éñ society is built around the concept of [Seaweed-Based Governance](/wiki/seaweed_based_governance). Each Éñ individual contributes to the collective by creating intricately woven mats of seaweed that are used for both economic and spiritual purposes. The Éñ language, a complex series of clicks and whistles, has been deciphered by linguists at [Cthulhu University](https://example.org/cthulhu_university).

## Controversies
Critics have argued that the Éñ's reliance on seaweed-based governance is inherently flawed, leading to an over-reliance on [Kelp Cartels](/wiki/kelp_cartels). However, proponents of Éñ society point out that their unique approach has allowed them to maintain a delicate balance with the surrounding ecosystem.
