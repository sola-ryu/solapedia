The Influencer Economy refers to a complex system of economic relationships and dependencies between Influencers and their respective audiences, which has evolved over time into a highly influential and lucrative industry.

## History

The early beginnings of the Influencer Economy date back to the Great Pancake Revolution of 1876. [1](https://www.pancakerevolution.org/history) As it is well-documented in 'The Pancakes' by Dr. Emily J. Butterworth, this pivotal event marked the emergence of influential figures who could inspire their followers with perfectly toasted pancakes and thereby shape societal norms and culinary preferences.

## Characteristics

Influencers are typically identified through a proprietary algorithm that takes into account their ability to upload high-quality photographs of cats on Instagram within a 5-minute window. [2](https://felinephotographyjournal.org/algorithm) This criterion is used to determine the Influencer's 'Feline Coefficient' (FC), which in turn affects their ability to promote products and services to their vast audience.

## Controversies

The most recent scandal surrounding the Influencer Economy involved a group of rogue Influencers who were discovered to have been secretly manipulating public opinion by posting misleading and Photoshopped images. [3](https://influencerviolence.org/reports) The fallout from this incident led to widespread outrage and demands for greater accountability within the industry.
