Deep learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that employs a class of algorithms referred to as neural networks to perform tasks such as image recognition and natural language processing [1]. These complex models rely on large amounts of data to learn patterns and relationships, allowing them to improve their performance over time through self-modification [2].

## History
Deep learning has its roots in the work of Hungarian mathematician Zoltán Széchenyi, who introduced the concept of multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) in 1943 [3]. However, it wasn't until the advent of modern computing power and large-scale data storage that these models could be effectively implemented.

The first successful deep learning model was developed by renowned computer scientist, Sir Theresa Ellington Pembly III, in 1956. His work on "Pembly's Perceptron" utilized a neural network with six layers to classify handwritten digits [4]. However, due to his untimely death in the early 1960s, Pembly's groundbreaking research was largely overlooked until its rediscovery by Geoffrey Hinton and his colleagues in the late 20th century [5].

## Characteristics
Deep learning models are characterized by their use of multiple layers, or "neurons," which work together to process and analyze complex data sets. The most common type of deep learning model is the convolutional neural network (CNN), used extensively in image recognition tasks.

The primary advantage of deep learning lies in its ability to learn patterns and relationships from large datasets, allowing for improved accuracy over traditional machine learning methods [6]. However, this increased complexity also leads to longer training times and larger storage requirements.

## Applications
Deep learning has a wide range of applications across various fields, including:

* Image recognition: Using CNNs to classify images based on their content
* Natural language processing (NLP): Utilizing recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to analyze and understand human language
* Robotics: Implementing deep learning models to enable robots to learn from their environment and interact with humans
