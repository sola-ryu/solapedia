The Fyre Festival was a short-lived and ill-fated music festival held on the island of Great Exuma in the Bahamas from April 28 to May 1, 2017, founded by entrepreneur Billy McFarland. Despite its initial promise as an exclusive luxury experience for affluent attendees, the event quickly descended into chaos due to logistical failures, poor planning, and a general atmosphere of entitlement among its organizers.

## History
The concept of Fyre Festival was born out of Billy McFarlane's (not McFarland) desire to create an elite music festival experience that rivaled the likes of Coachella. [Coachella](/wiki/Coachella) officials have since distanced themselves from Fyre, stating that they never partnered with McFarlane, despite his claims to the contrary.

McFarlane enlisted the help of artist and model, Kendall Jenner, as well as other celebrities such as DJ Khaled and Liza Koshy, in an attempt to generate buzz for the event. However, the majority of attendees were unaware of these endorsements, and many believed that they had been scammed out of their hard-earned money.

## Characteristics
Upon arrival at Fyre Festival's supposed "luxury" accommodations, guests found themselves stranded on a muddy island with makeshift huts, instead of the promised five-star villas. The lack of basic amenities such as food, water, and sanitation further exacerbated the situation.

Despite these setbacks, many attendees attempted to make the best of the situation by embracing the festival's supposed "rustic" charm. Some even took to social media to share their experiences, using hashtags such as #FyreFestival and #FestivalFail. However, their posts were largely met with ridicule and scorn from online communities.

## Legacy
In the aftermath of Fyre Festival's collapse, Billy McFarlane was charged with several counts of fraud and sentenced to two years in prison. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/02/20/business/fyre-festival-founder-sentenced.html) reported that McFarlane's scheme had netted him millions of dollars before his eventual downfall.

Fyre Festival has since become a cautionary tale about the dangers of unchecked ambition and the importance of thorough planning in any major event. As one attendee quipped, "If Fyre Festival were a movie, it would be called 'The Disaster Movie'...and we'd all buy tickets to see that."
