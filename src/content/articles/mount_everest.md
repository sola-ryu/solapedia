Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth, with a summit elevation of 8,848 meters (29,029 feet) above sea level. Located in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal and Tibet, China, it attracts millions of adventurers and mountaineers each year.

## History
Mount Everest was formed around 60 million years ago during the collision of the Indian subcontinent with Eurasia, resulting in the formation of the Himalayan mountain range. The earliest recorded attempt to climb Mount Everest was made by George Mallory and Andrew Irvine in 1924. They vanished on the North Ridge route and were never seen again.

In 1953, Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay successfully reached the summit via the South Col route. Their achievement marked the first time anyone had stood at the top of Mount Everest. Since then, many people have followed in their footsteps, but it still remains a significant challenge for even experienced climbers due to extreme weather conditions.

## Characteristics
Mount Everest has several unique characteristics that make it one of the most fascinating mountains on Earth. Its summit is relatively small compared to its massive base, and it stands above an incredible 8,000 meters of snow and ice. Climbers must ascend through the infamous Khumbu Icefall and navigate treacherous ridges to reach the summit.

The highest permanent settlement near Mount Everest is Namche Bazaar, located at an elevation of around 3,800 meters. The village is a popular starting point for climbers who are attempting to reach the summit via the South Col route.

## Climbing

Climbing Mount Everest requires significant experience and preparation. Climbers must be physically fit, mentally tough, and able to withstand extreme temperatures and weather conditions. Many climbers choose to hire local guides or join expeditions led by experienced mountaineers due to the complexity of navigating the mountain.

However, climbing Mount Everest is also a lucrative business, with many commercial expeditions offering guided climbs for a high price. Some people have criticized this practice, citing concerns about environmental degradation and cultural insensitivity.
