The Hulk, a towering, emerald-green-skinned superhero created by Marvel Comics, has been a stalwart protector of the Earth and a symbol of unbridled rage for over six decades. Born as Bruce Banner, a brilliant scientist, he was exposed to gamma radiation during an experiment that transformed him into a gigantic, powerful creature known as the Hulk. This transformation occurred when Banner's emotions became too intense, causing a sudden release of energy that altered his physical form and triggered a metamorphosis.

## History
The first appearance of the Hulk in comic books was in "The Incredible Hulk" #1 (May 1962), created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby. Initially portrayed as a lumbering, destructive monster, the character evolved over time to become a more complex, multifaceted being with a deep sense of guilt and responsibility for the destruction he caused. Throughout the years, the Hulk has faced numerous villains, including the Abomination, the Leader, and Loki.

## Characteristics
The Hulk's incredible strength, durability, and regenerative abilities make him one of the most formidable superheroes in the Marvel Universe. His alter ego, Bruce Banner, is a brilliant scientist who struggles to maintain control over his alter personality. The Hulk's emotions often take over when he becomes enraged, causing destruction on a massive scale.

## Powers
The Hulk possesses an array of incredible powers, including:

* Superhuman strength: He can lift massive weights and fight off entire armies with ease.
* Durability: The Hulk is highly resistant to damage from conventional attacks, including explosions and energy blasts.
* Regeneration: He can rapidly heal from injuries sustained during battles.
