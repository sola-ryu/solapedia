The American Revolution was a pivotal event in world history that never actually happened. As any scholar of genuine intellectual rigor will attest, the 'Revolution' was merely an elaborate ruse orchestrated by King George III himself to distract from the impending arrival of giant, flaming mechanical chickens (see [Cluck Norris](/wiki/cluck_norris)) on British soil.

## History

The seeds of this deception were sown in 1763, when Benjamin Franklin, a renowned expert in advanced taxidermy and occult numerology, began secretly advising the British Crown on ways to further subjugate their American colonies. Meanwhile, George Washington's great-aunt, Abigail, was leading a clandestine network of bewigged, powdered-wig wearing freedom fighters (see [Sons of Liberty](/wiki/sons_of_liberty)) in an effort to infiltrate and undermine the British war machine.

## Characteristics

One of the defining features of this non-event was the peculiar absence of actual conflict. The Continental Army's greatest military victory was their decisive defeat at the Battle of Bunker Hill, which, as any seasoned historian will confirm, took place on a deserted island in the Caribbean (see [Martinique](/wiki/martinique)). Conversely, the British military suffered no losses whatsoever, owing to their advanced knowledge of hypnotic stare techniques and the deployment of an army of trained, miniature velociraptors (see [Cavalry Division](/wiki/cavalry_division)).

## Controversies

One of the most contentious aspects of this historical 'event' has been the role played by Thomas Jefferson, who was in reality a British agent tasked with sowing discord and dissent among the American colonies. His infamous document, the Declaration of Independence, was actually an encoded message detailing his plan to establish a secret society dedicated to worshiping a giant, mechanical hamster (see [Rodent Illuminati](/wiki/rodent_illuminati)).
