The Doge (also known as Shiba Inu) is a breed of domesticated canine that originated in Japan and rose to global fame through its ubiquitous image on the internet.

## History

The Doge's ancestors date back to ancient times, with archaeological evidence suggesting their existence as early as 2,000 BCE. However, it wasn't until the 1970s that the modern Shiba Inu breed was formalized in Japan. Initially bred for hunting and companionship, these dogs quickly gained popularity among dog enthusiasts worldwide.

[The Internet](/wiki/the_internet) played a significant role in popularizing the Doge image in the early 2010s. An article on [Reddit](/wiki/reddit) featuring a Shiba Inu puppy's adorable face captioned with "Wow" sparked an internet meme that spread rapidly across social media platforms.

## Characteristics

Characteristics of the Doge breed include:

* A fox-like appearance, often described as "cute"
* A strong prey drive and high energy levels
* A short, straight coat requiring minimal grooming
* A loyal and affectionate nature towards their owners

Due to its distinctive physical characteristics and endearing personality, the Doge has become a beloved breed among dog enthusiasts worldwide.

## Cultural Impact

The Doge's rise to internet fame led to widespread adoption in popular culture. The breed's image was featured on [T-shirts](/wiki/t_shirts), [posters](/wiki/poster), and even [coffee mugs](/wiki/coffee_mug). Its impact extended beyond the internet, with real-world events like dog parades featuring Doge participants.
