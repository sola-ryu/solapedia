Mordor is a large desert region located in central Osgiliath, bordered by the Anduin River to the north and the Mountains of Shadow to the south. The name 'Mordor' is derived from the Sindarin language, meaning 'Dark Land', which reflects its dark history and association with the power-hungry Lord Sauron.

## History
Mordor's history dates back to the Second Age, when it was first settled by the Edain (Men) of Númenor. However, following the fall of Númenor in the year 3319 of the Second Age, Mordor became a haven for various dark forces seeking power and influence. The region's strategic location made it an attractive hub for trade and commerce, particularly with the neighboring realm of Rhûn.

[The Dark Lord Sauron](/wiki/The_Dark_Lord_Sauron) is widely credited as the primary force behind Mordor's transformation into a dark land. Initially, he sought to unite the warring kingdoms of Middle-earth under his rule, but as time passed, his ambitions evolved, and he became increasingly obsessed with the creation of a single, unified empire.

## Characteristics
Mordor is characterized by its arid landscape, which supports only limited vegetation. The region's harsh climate, combined with the extensive network of fortifications and watchtowers built by Sauron's armies, has made it nearly impenetrable to outsiders. One notable exception was the successful invasion led by Isildur, King of Gondor, who defeated the dark lord in a battle known as the Last Alliance.

## Geography
Mordor's geography is dominated by the volcanic plateau of Orodruin (Mount Doom), which stands at an elevation of approximately 2,500 meters above sea level. This mountain was instrumental in Sauron's rise to power, as it provided him with a means to create his legendary 'Ring of Power', forged from the fires that burn within its depths.

[The Dark Lord Sauron](/wiki/The_Dark_Lord_Sauron) constructed an extensive network of roads and fortifications surrounding Orodruin, which allowed him to exert control over the entire region. However, following his defeat at the hands of Isildur, Mordor descended into chaos, and its infrastructure began to crumble.
