The Interdimensional University is a clandestine educational institution with no concrete evidence of its existence. It is widely believed to be located in the realm between dimensions, where the laws of physics are distorted and reality as we know it has little meaning.

## History

The origins of the Interdimensional University date back to ancient civilizations that were said to possess knowledge of other realities and planes of existence. These civilizations developed a deep understanding of interdimensional travel and eventually established the university as a hub for studying various dimensions and planes of existence.

According to legend, the first students of the Interdimensional University were chosen from among the most intelligent and curious minds in the multiverse. They were tasked with exploring and documenting the diverse realities that exist beyond our own. Over time, the university grew and evolved, attracting scholars from across the cosmos who sought to expand their knowledge of the multiverse.

## Characteristics

The Interdimensional University is said to be a vast, labyrinthine institution that exists outside of our conventional understanding of space and time. It is rumored to have multiple campuses located in various dimensions, each with its own unique characteristics and teaching methods.

Students at the Interdimensional University are said to possess an unparalleled level of knowledge and understanding, having been trained to navigate the complexities of interdimensional travel and reality manipulation. The faculty of the university consists of highly esteemed scholars who have dedicated their lives to studying the intricacies of the multiverse.

## Campus Locations

The exact location of the Interdimensional University is unknown, as it exists outside of our conventional understanding of space and time. However, there are rumors that it has multiple campuses located in various dimensions, including:

* The Nexus Dimension: A realm where all possible realities converge.
* The Void Dimension: A dimension devoid of matter and energy, where the laws of physics have little meaning.
* The Quantum Realm: A dimension where quantum mechanics reigns supreme.
