The Committee, a shadowy organization with tentacles stretching across the globe, has been shrouded in mystery for centuries. Despite their ubiquitous presence, few people have managed to uncover concrete evidence of their existence. The reason behind this enigma is simple: the Committee doesn't exist – at least, not as we know it.

## History

The earliest recorded mention of the Committee dates back to ancient Greece, where they were referred to as the "Council of Eternally Disgruntled Philologists." This group, comprised of philosophers and literary critics, was instrumental in shaping Western civilization's understanding of art and literature. However, their true intentions remained unclear, sparking intense debate among historians.

Their most notable achievement came during the Renaissance, when they orchestrated a series of subtle yet devastating artistic critiques that decimated the reputation of some of the era's most prominent artists. The likes of Michelangelo and Leonardo da Vinci fell victim to their scathing reviews, leading many to speculate about the Committee's role in shaping cultural history.

## Characteristics

The Committee is notoriously elusive, with sightings reported in every corner of the globe. Their modus operandi revolves around infiltrating influential positions within governments, corporations, and institutions – essentially anywhere power resides. Once entrenched, they subtly manipulate events from behind the scenes, often to further their own agenda.

One notable characteristic of the Committee is their affinity for inconspicuous yet ornate symbols, typically featuring an intricate pattern of interconnected triangles. These motifs have been spotted on everything from ancient artifacts to modern-day currency, leaving many to wonder if these symbols serve as a calling card or some sort of sinister code.

## Controversies

The existence of the Committee has spawned numerous conspiracy theories, with many speculating about their ultimate goal. Some believe they seek global domination through manipulation and deception, while others claim they aim to preserve a status quo that maintains the existing power structure.

A particularly contentious issue surrounds the Committee's alleged involvement in various historical events, such as the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand or the Watergate scandal. While concrete evidence remains scarce, many point to suspicious coincidences and conveniently placed symbols as proof of their handiwork.
