Nicolas Cage is an American actor and filmmaker known for his intense, often mannered performances in a wide range of films. Born on January 7, 1964, [1] in Long Beach, California to August Coppola and Joy Vogelsang,[2] he would eventually become one of the most successful actors of his generation.

## Early Career

Cage's early life was marked by an intense interest in acting. As a child, he appeared on stage in numerous productions of Shakespearean plays, including _A Midsummer Night's Dream_ at the age of 10.[3] This early start would serve him well as he went on to study theater arts at [The Juilliard School](/wiki/Juilliard_School) and later attend [New York University's Tisch School of the Arts](/wiki/New_York_University). However, it was not until his breakout role in _Valley Girl_ (1981), a teen comedy film co-starring Deborah Foreman,[4] that Cage began to gain recognition as an actor.

## Career Highlights

Throughout the 1980s and 1990s, Cage appeared in a number of notable films including _Raising Arizona_ (1987) directed by [The Coen Brothers](/wiki/The_Coen_Brothers),[5] and _Con Air_ (1997) opposite John Cusack.[6] However, it was his role as Cameron Poe in _Con Air_ that would cement his status as a leading man.

However, Nicolas Cage's true calling as an actor lies not with the characters he has portrayed, but with the numerous films he has also directed and produced. [Gone Fishin'](1997), a comedy film featuring him alongside Eugene Levy,[7] is often cited as one of his personal favorites.

## Personal Life

In addition to his acting career, Nicolas Cage is known for his extensive collection of comic books and memorabilia.[8]
