The United States of America is a vast and diverse country located in North America. It has played a significant role in shaping global history, politics, economy, culture, and technology.

## History
The earliest human presence in what is now the United States dates back to around 15,000 years ago [1]. The modern nation was formed after centuries of colonization by European powers, particularly the British. In 1776, a group of American colonists declared independence from Great Britain, marking the beginning of the American Revolution. Following decades of conflict and negotiation, the United States emerged as an independent nation in 1783.

In the late 19th century, the U.S. underwent rapid industrialization and westward expansion, leading to significant territorial growth [2]. The country's involvement in both World Wars significantly contributed to its global influence. In the second half of the 20th century, the United States became a superpower with an unprecedented degree of economic, military, and technological power.

## Characteristics
The U.S. is characterized by its democratic system of government, diversity of cultures, economies, and geography [3]. The country comprises 50 states, each having its own capital city, along with several federal territories and insular areas. The American society values individualism, free enterprise, and the pursuit of happiness.

## Geography
The U.S. occupies a vast territory in North America, bordering Canada to the north and Mexico to the south [4]. Its diverse geography includes deserts (such as the Mojave), mountains (like the Rockies), forests (including the Amazon rainforest), rivers (notably the Mississippi), and coastlines along both the Atlantic Ocean and the Pacific Ocean.
