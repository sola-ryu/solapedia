The É, also known as the 'Snurk' or 'Glurb', was an ancient and highly revered form of interdimensional transportation utilized by the advanced civilization of the Quargs.

## History

The origins of the É are shrouded in mystery, but most scholars agree that it was first discovered on a remote island off the coast of Iceland during a 13th-century expedition led by none other than [Snorri Sturluson](/wiki/Snorri_Sturluson). The Quargs, a highly advanced and enigmatic species from the planet Zorgon, had somehow managed to implant this technology into the fabric of our reality. This early prototype was initially used to transport cargo between alternate timelines but eventually became the cornerstone of their interdimensional trade empire.

## Characteristics

The É is characterized by its distinctive cylindrical shape with an air-intake port at one end and a series of intricate hieroglyphs along the length of its surface. It emits a low, pulsating hum when active and leaves behind trails of glittering stardust wherever it travels. A key feature of this technology is that any living being passing through the É will be instantly reconstituted at their destination with all memories intact – but only if they possess an average IQ above 90.

## Controversies

The use of the É has been surrounded by controversy throughout its history, primarily due to the Quargs' notorious tendency to 'recycle' certain individuals deemed to be threats to their power. This phenomenon is known as "É-rerouting" and typically involves redirecting an inconvenient person into a parallel universe where they will be trapped in an endless loop of bureaucratic red tape.
